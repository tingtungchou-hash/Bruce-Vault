# Original Text
quid? cum commiserari, conqueri, et ex illius invidia deonerare aliquid et in te traicere coeperit, commemorare quaestoris cum praetore necessitudinem constitutam, morem maiorum, sortis religionem, poterisne eius orationis subire invidiam? vide modo, etiam atque etiam considera. mihi enim videtur periculum fore ne ille non modo verbis te obruat, sed gestu ipso ac motu corporis praestringat aciem ingeni tui, teque ab institutis tuis cogitationibusque abducat.

# Text Analysis
quid? cum ==commiserari==, conqueri, et ex illius invidia ==deonerare== aliquid et in te traicere coeperit, commemorare quaestoris cum praetore necessitudinem constitutam, morem maiorum, sortis religionem, poterisne eius orationis ==subire== invidiam? vide modo, etiam atque etiam considera. mihi enim videtur periculum fore ne ille non modo verbis te ==obruat==, sed gestu ipso ac motu corporis ==praestringat== aciem ingeni tui, teque ab institutis tuis cogitationibusque abducat.

# Translation
what? when he started to symphasise, to complain, and to unload something out of the plots of his and through them against you, (when he started) to call to mind the established personal bound of the quaestor with the praetor, the mos maiorum, the constraints of the lot, are you able to go under the plot of his oration? please only see, and consider once and over again. it seems to me that danger is going to happen lest he not only obscures you with words, but also with his own gesture and also to dazzle you with the movement of his body, and lead you away from your instituts and cognition. 

# Vocabulary

1. commiseror, commiserārī, commiserātus sum v. dep.
	- 词形：文中 commiserārī 为主动不定式（异态），与 conquerī 并列，受 coeperit 支配，表示（故作）诉苦、博取同情
	- 词源：com-（加强／共同）+ miseror（“哀叹、怜悯”，出 miser“可怜的”）→“深表同情、哀叹”；同族 miser（可怜者）、misereor（怜悯）、misericordia（慈悲）、miserābilis（可悯的）
	- 义：(1) 同情、怜悯；(2) 诉苦、哀叹、博取同情（演说修辞，唤起怜悯）
	- 本句：cum commiserārī, conquerī … coeperit——“当他开始（故作）诉苦、哀叹、博取同情时”
	- 警示：commiserārī 为异态动词（形被动而义主动）；与 conquerī（抱怨）、miserērī（怜悯）近义；commiseror 侧重“表现／唤起同情”，非单纯内心怜悯

2. deonerō, deonerāre, deonerāvī, deonerātum v. tr.
	- 词形：文中 deonerāre 为主动不定式，与 commiserārī、conquerī、trāicere 并列；宾语 aliquid，来源状语 ex illius invidiā
	- 词源：dē-（“下、去”）+ onerō（“装载、加负”，出 onus“负担”）→“卸下负担”；同族 onus（负担）、onerārius（运货的）、exonerō（卸除）；英语 exonerate 出 ex + onus
	- 义：(1) 卸下、卸货；(2) 卸除、解除（负担、责任）；(3) 转嫁（负担）——如本句“把恶名从某人身上卸下转嫁给你”
	- 本句：ex illius invidiā deonerāre aliquid et in tē trāicere——“从他的恶名里卸下一部分，转嫁到你身上”
	- 警示：deonerō 由 dē + onus（负担）构成，“卸下负担”，勿与 dehonestō（使蒙羞）混；近义 exonerō；本句借“卸货”喻“卸恶名、转嫁”

3. subeō, subīre, subiī, subitum v. tr./intr.
	- 词形：文中 subīre 为主动不定式；宾语 invidiam，poterisne … subīre …——“你承受得住……吗”
	- 词源：sub-（“在下”）+ eō, īre（“走”）→“走到下面”→“进入、承受”；同族 eō／īre（走）、adeō（走近）、ineō（进入、开始）、pereō（消亡）
	- 义：(1) 走到下面、进入；(2) 承担、承受、遭遇（负担、危险、指责）；(3) 屈服、服从
	- 本句：poterisne eius ōrātiōnis subīre invidiam?——“你承受得住他这番陈词招来的恶名（敌意）吗？”
	- 警示：完成时 subiī（亦作 subīvī），supine subitum；此处义“承受、面对”，非字面“在下面走”；勿与 subicere（置于下、使服从）混

4. obruō, obruere, obruī, obrutum v. tr.
	- 词形：文中 obruat 为现在时主动虚拟式第三人称单数，处于 nē 引导的恐惧／目的从句（nē … obruat … praestringat … abdūcat）
	- 词源：ob-（“朝向、覆盖于上”）+ ruō（“冲、倒、塌”）→“向……倾倒覆盖”→ 掩埋、压倒；同族 ruō（冲、塌）、dīruō（摧毁）、prōruō（向前倒）
	- 义：(1) 覆盖、掩埋、堆没；(2) 压倒、压垮、使不知所措（言辞、指责、灾难）；(3) 淹没
	- 本句：nē ille nōn modo verbīs tē obruat——“怕他会用言辞把你压垮（淹没）”
	- 警示：obruat 为虚拟式（nē 从句），非陈述式；ob- + ruō（冲、倒）；勿与完成分词 obrutus（“被埋的”）误判时态；近义 opprimō（压倒）

5. praestringō, praestringere, praestrīnxī, praestrictum v. tr.
	- 词形：文中 praestringat 为现在时主动虚拟式第三人称单数，与 obruat、abdūcat 并列（nē 从句）；宾语 aciem ingenī tuī
	- 词源：prae-（“在前”）+ stringō（“束紧、轻擦、剥”）→“在面前擦过／束紧”→ 使（刃）钝、使目眩；同族 stringō（束紧、擦过）、perstringō（掠过、轻责）、aciēs（刃、锐）
	- 义：(1) 束紧；(2) 轻触、擦过；(3) 使（刃）变钝、消磨、削弱；(4) 使目眩、使（眼／心智）失明——本句“使你才智的锋刃变钝／晃花你的心智”
	- 本句：gestū ipsō ac mōtū corporis praestringat aciem ingenī tuī——“凭手势和身体的动作用，把你才智的锋刃弄钝（晃花你的心智）”
	- 警示：praestringat 为虚拟式；aciēs 本义“刀刃、锋利”，引申“目力／心智的敏锐”，praestringere aciem 是“使目光／锋刃变钝、晃眼”的固定意象；prae- + stringō，勿与 perstringō（掠过、轻责）混
