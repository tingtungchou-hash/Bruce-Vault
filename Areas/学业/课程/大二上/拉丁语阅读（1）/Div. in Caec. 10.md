# Original Text
nunc quoniam quibus rebus adductus ad causam accesserim demonstravi, dicendum necessario est de contentione nostra, ut in constituendo accusatore quid sequi possitis habeatis. ego sic intellego, iudices: cum de pecuniis repetundis nomen cuiuspiam deferatur, si certamen inter aliquos sit cui potissimum delatio detur, haec duo in primis spectari oportere, quem maxime velint actorem esse ii quibus factae esse dicantur iniuriae, et quem minime velit is qui eas iniurias fecisse arguatur.

# Text Analysis
nunc quoniam quibus rebus adductus ad causam accesserim demonstravi, dicendum necessario est de contentione nostra, ut \[in constituendo accusatore] (quid sequi possitis) habeatis. ego sic intellego, iudices: cum \[de pecuniis repetundis] nomen ==cuiuspiam== deferatur, **si** certamen inter aliquos sit cui potissimum ==delatio== detur, haec duo in primis spectari oportere, quem maxime velint actorem esse ii quibus factae esse dicantur iniuriae, et quem minime velit is qui eas iniurias fecisse ==arguatur==.

# Translation
Now since I have demonstrated that I approach to this case, bond by these reasons, it should necessarily be said about the contest of ours, that you have in deciding an accuser what you're able to follow (criteria). I thus think, judges: since the name of anyone is given about bribery, if the decisive contest among us two is one to which the most powerful accusation is given, it is proper that these two things be viewed as among the very first, i. e. whom the Sicilians, to whom it is said that injustice was inflicted, wish the most to be the accuser, and whom Verres, who it is shown to have done those injustice, wishes the least to be the accuser. 

# Vocabulary

1. cuiuspiam（quispiam 的属格单数；不定代词）
	- 词形：quispiam, quaepiam, quidpiam / quippiam（不定代词）；属格单数作 cuiuspiam。与 aliquis（“某人”）、quisquam（“任何人”）、ullus（“任何一个”）同属不定与泛指词系统
	- 词源：quis（疑问／不定词干）＋ -piam（加强虚词），字面“不知哪一个、某一个”。L&S 即以本句为例证：“cum de pecuniis repetundis nomen cuiuspiam deferatur, Cic. Div. in Caecil. 3, 10”
	- 义：some one, any one, something（“某人、某人某物”）；比 aliquis 更不确定、更泛指，语气弱于 quisquam
	- 本句：cum [de pecuniis repetundis] nomen cuiuspiam deferatur——“当有人因勒索钱财（pecuniae repetundae）一案被具名控告时”。cuiuspiam 作 nomen 的定语（属格），指“某个人的名字”
	- 警示：勿与 quisquam 的属格 cuiusquam 相混：quispiam 表“某人”（确有其人，只是不确定是谁），quisquam 表“任何人”（多用于否定、疑问、条件句）。此处用 cuiuspiam 而非 cuiusquam，正因为并非否定语境。变格要跟 quispiam 一整套（quodpiam / quidpiam），不是 quisquam 那一套

2. dēlātiō, dēlātiōnis f.（第三变格法）
	- 词形：dēlātiō, -ōnis f.；动词 dēferō, dēferre, dētulī, dēlātum
	- 词源：由 dēferō（“把……送下、交上”）派生，加 -tiō 表动作；nomen deferre（“把某人的名字呈报上去”）是罗马刑事程序的固定说法，指正式向长官告发某人，dēlātiō 即这一“具名告发”的行为
	- 义：an accusation, denunciation（“告发、指控”），尤指正式提名控告（the formal laying of information）
	- 本句：si certamen inter aliquos sit cui potissimum delatio detur——“若在诸人之间发生争执，争论该由谁最优先获得控告权”。delatio 在此指“控告资格的授予”，正是本篇争夺的焦点
	- 警示：勿与 accūsātiō（“起诉、控诉”，指正式的法庭控诉）相混：dēlātiō 侧重“告发、提名”这一前置动作（与 nomen deferre 相应）。《学说汇纂》即以此区分：“Indicasse est detulisse; arguisse accusasse et convicisse”

3. arguātur（arguō, arguere, arguī, argūtum 3 的现在时虚拟式被动，第三人称单数）
	- 词形：arguō, arguere, arguī, argūtum 3（主动）；此处 arguātur 为现在时虚拟式被动。与名词 argūmentum、形容词 argūtus 同族
	- 词源：L&S 联系希腊语 ἀργός（“明亮的”）、梵语 árjunas（“明亮”）、argentum（“银”），本义“使明亮”，引申为“使显明、证明”——与 clārus / clārō（“明亮／使明亮”）同理
	- 义：(1) 使显明、表明、证明（make clear, show, prove）；(2) 加 aliquem，指控、指责、举发（accuse, charge）
	- 本句：is qui eas iniurias fecisse arguatur——“那个被指控犯下这些不法之事的人”。此处为被动 + fecisse 不定式，arguī + 不定式即“被指控做了……”
	- 警示：勿与 accūsō（“控诉”，正式起诉）相混：arguō 语气更宽，既可指“证明、显示”，也可指“举发、指责”；古注（Fest.）称“Argutum iri in discrimen vocari”，谓“被 arguere”即“被拖入危境”，可见其法律色彩。注意 arguātur 用虚拟式，是依 qui 从句（表特征、带结果意味）的需要
