# Original Text
nunc quoniam quibus rebus adductus ad causam accesserim demonstravi, dicendum necessario est de contentione nostra, ut in constituendo accusatore quid sequi possitis habeatis. ego sic intellego, iudices: cum de pecuniis repetundis nomen cuiuspiam deferatur, si certamen inter aliquos sit cui potissimum delatio detur, haec duo in primis spectari oportere, quem maxime velint actorem esse ii quibus factae esse dicantur iniuriae, et quem minime velit is qui eas iniurias fecisse arguatur.

# Text Analysis
nunc quoniam quibus rebus adductus ad causam accesserim demonstravi, dicendum necessario est de contentione nostra, ut \[in constituendo accusatore] (quid sequi possitis) habeatis. ego sic intellego, iudices: cum \[de pecuniis repetundis] nomen ==cuiuspiam== deferatur, **si** certamen inter aliquos sit cui potissimum ==delatio== detur, haec duo in primis spectari oportere, quem maxime velint actorem esse ii quibus factae esse dicantur iniuriae, et quem minime velit is qui eas iniurias fecisse ==arguatur==.

# Translation
Now since I have demonstrated that I approach to this case, bond by these reasons, it should necessarily be said about the contest of ours, that you have in deciding an accuser what you're able to follow (criteria). I thus think, judges: since the name of anyone is given about bribery, if the decisive contest among us two is one to which the most powerful accusation is given, it is proper that these two things be viewed as among the very first, i. e. whom the Sicilians, to whom it is said that injustice was inflicted, wish the most to be the accuser, and whom Verres, who it is shown to have done those injustice, wishes the least to be the accuser.
