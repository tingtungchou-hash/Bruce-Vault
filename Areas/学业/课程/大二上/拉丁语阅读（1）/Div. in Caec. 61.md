# Original Text
ac vide quid differat inter meam opinionem ac tuam. tu cum omnibus rebus inferior sis, hac una in re te mihi anteferri putas oportere, quod quaestor illius fueris: ego, si superior omnibus rebus esses, hanc unam ob causam te accusatorem repudiari putarem oportere. sic enim a maioribus nostris accepimus, praetorem quaestori suo parentis loco esse oportere; nullam neque iustiorem neque graviorem causam necessitudinis posse reperiri quam coniunctionem sortis, quam provinciae, quam offici, quam publici muneris societatem.

# Text Analysis
ac vide quid differat inter meam opinionem ac tuam. tu \[cum omnibus rebus inferior sis], hac una in re te mihi anteferri putas oportere, quod quaestor illius fueris: ego, si superior omnibus rebus esses, hanc unam ob causam te accusatorem ==repudiari== putarem oportere. sic enim a maioribus nostris accepimus, praetorem quaestori suo parentis loco esse oportere; nullam neque iustiorem neque graviorem causam necessitudinis posse reperiri quam coniunctionem sortis, quam provinciae, quam offici, quam publici ==muneris== societatem.

# Translation
and see what was different between my opinion and yours. since you are inferior with all the things, in this one thing alone you think it is proper that you be preferred to me, that you had been his quaestor: but I, if you were superior in terms of all other things, would still think it to be proper for you to be denied the advocacy for this one reason alone. this indeed we carried off from our ancestors, that it is proper for the praetor to be at the place of parent to his quaestor; no reason of personal bond can be found to be neither more justified nor heavier than the connection by lots, of association of province, of office, of service. 

看看我的看法与你的看法有何不同吧。你在一切方面都处于下风，却认为仅凭一点你就应当被置于我之上，即你曾是他的财务官；而我却认为，纵使你处处占优，仅凭这一条理由，你也应当被拒绝授予控告权。因为我们从祖先那里承袭的教诲是：行政长官应当把自己的财务官视如父辈；再没有比命运的联结、行省的共处、职责的承担、公共职务的合作更为正当、更为沉重的亲缘关系之由了。

# Vocabulary

1. repudio（动词）
	- 词形：*repudiō, repudiāre, repudiāvī, repudiātum*，第一变格法动词；本句 *repudiari* 即 *repudiārī*，现在时被动不定式
	- 词源：由 *repudium*“休妻、解除婚约；拒绝”转来；*re-* 表“回、反”，*repudium* 本指把妻子“退回”
	- 义：(1) 休弃、与……离婚；(2) 拒绝接受（遗产、条件等）；(3) 泛指拒绝、摒弃、唾弃（reject, refuse, spurn, repudiate）
	- 本句：*tē accūsātōrem repudiārī putārem oportēre*——“我认为你应当被拒绝授予控告人资格”；*repudiārī* 是 *putārem* 所支配的间接陈述中的被动不定式，*tē* 为其主语，*accūsātōrem* 为表语
	- 提示：注意语态——本句是“你被拒绝”，不是“你拒绝”；同族 *repudium*（休婚）、*repudiātiō*（拒绝）
2. munus（名词）
	- 词形：*mūnus, mūneris* n.，第三变格法中性名词；本句 *muneris* 为单数属格，受 *publicī* 修饰
	- 词源：古写作 *moenus*，词根 *mu-*；同族 *mūnia*“义务、职守”，*mūniceps* / *mūnicipium* 则由 *mūnus* 加 *capere* 构成，本指“承担公职者”
	- 义：(1) 职务、职责、公职（service, office, duty）；(2) 负担、义务、贡赋；(3) 礼物、馈赠；(4) 公共表演、角斗演出（由高级长官、尤以营造官举办）
	- 本句：*pūblicī mūneris societātem*——“公共职务上的共事关系”；此处取“公职、公务”义，与 *sortis*、*prōvinciae*、*officiī* 并列，说明财务官与长官之间的紧密联系
	- 提示：*mūneris* 是单数属格，不是复数主格或宾格 *mūnera*；中性名词，勿误作阳性。词义由“义务”经“以职务为名所给的赠礼”发展出“公共演出”义，*mūnus gladiātōrium* 即角斗表演
