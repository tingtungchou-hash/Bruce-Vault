# Original Text
tu horum nihil metuis, nihil cogitas, nihil laboras: si quid ex vetere aliqua oratione, 'Iovem ego optimum maximum,' aut 'vellem, si fieri potuisset, iudices,' aut aliquid eius modi ==ediscere== potueris, ==praeclare== te paratum in iudicium venturum arbitraris.

# Text Analysis
tu horum nihil metuis, nihil cogitas, nihil laboras: si quid ex vetere aliqua oratione, 'Iovem ego optimum maximum,' aut 'vellem, si fieri potuisset, iudices,' aut aliquid eius modi ediscere potueris, praeclare te paratum in iudicium venturum arbitraris.

# Translation
You fear nothing of them, you know nothing of them, you work hard for nothing: if something out of some old oration like "I (call) Iupiter O. M." or "I wish, if it had been able to take place, judges" or you are able to know something of this kind by hear, you think you're prepared to go to the court. 

你对此毫不畏惧，毫不思虑，毫不用心：只要你能从某篇旧演说里背下点什么，比如“我请至善至大的朱庇特作证”，或者“诸位陪审官，我本希望，倘若可能的话”，或者诸如此类的话，你就以为自己准备得极好，可以上法庭了。
