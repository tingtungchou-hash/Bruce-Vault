# Original Text
hic ego de te plura non dicam: unum illud credo omnis animum advertere, te adhuc a nullis nisi ab Siculis potuisse cognosci; Siculos hoc dicere, cum eidem sint irati cui tu te inimicum esse dicis, sese tamen te actore ad iudicium non adfuturos. quare negent ex me non audies: hos patere id suspicari quod necesse est. illi quidem, ut est hominum genus nimis acutum et suspiciosum, non te ex Sicilia litteras in Verrem deportare velle arbitrantur, sed, quod isdem litteris illius praetura et tua quaestura consignata sit, asportare te velle ex Sicilia litteras suspicantur. 

# Text Analysis
hic ego de te plura non dicam: unum illud credo omnis animum advertere, te adhuc a nullis nisi ab Siculis potuisse cognosci; Siculos hoc dicere, cum eidem sint irati cui tu te inimicum esse dicis, sese tamen te actore ad iudicium non adfuturos. ==*quare== negent* ex me non audies: hos ==patere== id suspicari quod necesse est. illi quidem, ut est hominum genus nimis ==acutum== et suspiciosum, non te ex Sicilia litteras in Verrem deportare velle **arbitrantur**, sed, quod isdem litteris illius praetura et tua quaestura ==consignata== sit, ==asportare== te velle ex Sicilia litteras **suspicantur**. 

# Translation
now I you say no more about you: I believe one thing to ~~change~~ ***enter*** the ~~entire~~ mind ***of all***, i. e. that you had been able to be recognised by no one other than by the Sicilians till now; that the Sicilians said this, although they are irritated to the same person to whom you say you are an enemy, they are not entering the court with you as the advocate. ~~thus~~ ***what*** they deny ~~what~~ you will not listen from me, ~~that these open~~ ***let them guess*** what is necessary to be suspected. they also, as the human race is too sharp and suspicious, think that you want to sent letters from Siciliy to Verres, but, because ***the seal of*** his praetorship and your quaestorship ~~is sealed to~~ is on those same letters, they suspect that you want to carry away letters from Sicily.  

关于你，我在这里不再多说了。我相信大家都注意到了这一点：直到现在，除了西西里人，没有人认识你。而西西里人却说：尽管他们憎恨的正是你自称与之敌对的那个人，可要是由你担任控诉人，他们仍然不会出庭。因此，你不会听我说他们否认此事；你就让他们去猜那件他们非猜不可的事吧。他们这些人，按人类这个族类的性子，本来就过于敏锐、过于多疑：他们并不认为你想把控告维勒斯的文书从西西里带出去；但由于那些文书上同样签有他任裁判官、你任财务官的印记，他们便怀疑你是想把那些文书从西西里运走。

# Vocabulary
- quare：疑问副词，“为什么”；也可作连接副词“因此、所以”。本句 quare negent 是间接疑问句，意为“他们为什么说不／为什么否认”。
- negent：negō, -āre 的第三人称复数现在时主动虚拟式，“他们说不／他们否认”；因位于 quare 引导的间接疑问句中而用虚拟式。
- patere：此处不是 pateō（“敞开”）的不定式，而是 patior, patī 的第二人称单数现在时主动祈使式，“让／允许”。hos patere id suspicari = “让他们去怀疑那件事”。
- acutum：acūtus, -a, -um，“尖锐的、敏锐的”，是 acuō 的完成时被动分词作形容词；本句为中性单数，与 hominum genus 一致，意为“过于敏锐”。
- consignata：consignō, -āre 的完成时被动分词，阴性单数主格，与 quaestura（或整个复合主语）一致，“被签押、被盖印、被记录”。本句指同一批文书上有维勒斯任裁判官、凯基利乌斯任财务官的签押。
- asportare：asportō, -āre 的现在时主动不定式，“带走、运走”，与 deportare 近义；本句与 velle 连用，asportare te velle = “你想把……带走”。
