# Original Text
nam civibus cum sunt ereptae pecuniae, civili fere actione et privato iure repetuntur. haec lex socialis est, hoc ius nationum exterarum est, hanc habent arcem, minus aliquanto nunc quidem munitam quam antea, verum tamen si qua reliqua spes est quae sociorum animos consolari possit, ea tota in hac lege posita est; cuius legis non modo a populo Romano, sed etiam ab ultimis nationibus iam pridem severi custodes requiruntur. 

# Text Analysis
nam civibus cum sunt ereptae pecuniae, civili ==fere== actione et privato iure repetuntur. haec lex socialis est, hoc ius nationum exterarum est, hanc habent arcem, minus ==aliquanto== nunc quidem munitam quam antea, verum tamen si qua reliqua spes est quae sociorum animos ==consolari== possit, ea tota in hac lege posita est; cuius legis non modo a populo Romano, sed etiam ab ultimis nationibus iam ==pridem== severi custodes requiruntur. 

# Translation
Since the wealth has been taken away, according to entirely civil action and private right, they are returned to the citizens. this law pertains to the allies, this right pertains to foreign nations, they have this fortress, something now less defended compared to before by some little, indeed still if the hope that is left is able to encourage the spirit of the allies, that entire hope lays in this law; not only by the Roman people but even by the farest peoples, strict garrison are required of this law, both now long ago.  

# Vocabulary

1. ferē（不变副词；另有加强形 fermē）
	- 词形：ferē（亦作 fermē），不变副词，无屈折
	- 词源：ferē 与加强形 fermē（-me 后缀）同源；词根或与 fer-（负、载）相关，本义“一总、齐”，遂引申为“大体、近乎”。L&S 首列“altogether, quite, entirely”为古体义
	- 义：(1) altogether, quite, entirely（完全、一总——古体/强义，多与 omnēs、nēmō 等连用）；(2) generally, usually, for the most part（通常、大抵）；(3) about, nearly, almost（大约、将近——表数或量）
	- 本句：cīvīlī ferē āctiōne et prīvātō iūre repetuntur——“（这些钱财）大体上是凭民事的诉讼与私法来追讨的”。ferē 修饰 cīvīlī（āctiōne）；本篇译文作“entirely”，取古体义 (1)
	- 警示：ferē 与 fermē 义近，常通用；勿与 ferō（“承载”）的变位形相混。位置灵活，可置于所修饰词前或句中
	- 关联：同义 paene, prope, circiter, plērumque；ferē 常与 omnēs、semper、nēmō 连用，表“几乎全部／几乎总是”

2. aliquantō（aliquantum 的夺格，作副词用；差量夺格）
	- 词形：aliquantō ＝ 不定词 aliquantus, -a, -um（“相当多的”）的中性夺格，副词化；与比较级连用
	- 词源：aliquantus ＝ ali-（＝ aliquis“某个”的词干）＋ quantus（“多么大”），字面“某一数量的”；其夺格 aliquantō 表差量，遂成副词“略、些”
	- 义：作副词 somewhat, to some extent, considerably（有些、略、相当——修饰比较级或程度）；aliquantō … quam …“比……略……”
	- 本句：minus aliquantō nunc quidem mūnītam quam anteā——“如今确实比先前略为疏于设防”。aliquantō 为差量夺格，修饰比较级 minus，表差量“略、一些”；minus … quam“比……更少／不如”
	- 警示：重点警示——aliquantō（“略、些”，夺格副词）与 aliquandō（“曾经、有时”，时间副词）形极近而义异，须辨 -quantō（量）与 -quandō（时）；亦勿混 aliquot（“若干”，不变形容词，接名词）
	- 关联：同族 aliquantum, aliquantulus, aliquid；差量夺格同类 multō, paulō, tantō, quantō（皆与比较级连用）

3. cōnsolor, cōnsōlārī, cōnsōlātus sum 1（异相动词 deponent；此处 cōnsōlārī，现在时不定式）
	- 词形：cōnsolor, -ārī, -ātus sum 1（异相）；此处 cōnsōlārī ＝ 现在时不定式（主动义）
	- 词源：con-（加强或“共同”）＋ sōlor（“安慰、抚慰”），本义“（彻底）安慰、抚慰”；同族 sōlor, cōnsōlātiō（“安慰”）、sōlācium（“慰藉”，本篇第 17 段 solācium 同源）
	- 义：to comfort, console, encourage, cheer（安慰、抚慰、鼓励）；与 sōlor, lēniō, mulceō 义近
	- 本句：sī qua reliqua spēs est quae sociōrum animōs cōnsōlārī possit——“如果还剩下什么能够抚慰盟邦人心的希望的话”。cōnsōlārī 为不定式，宾语 sociōrum animōs，与 possit 构成 spēs … quae … possit 关系从句的谓语内容（潜势虚拟）
	- 警示：cōnsolor 为异相动词，形被动而义主动；勿因词尾 -ārī 误作被动“被安慰”，此处是主动“（能）安慰（人心）”
	- 关联：同义 sōlor, lēniō, mulceō；反义 vexō, afflīgō, cruciō；cōnsōlātiō、sōlācium 同族

4. prīdem（不变副词）
	- 词形：prīdem，不变副词
	- 词源：出 prīs（古体，＝ prior“在先的”）＋ -dem（指示-强调后缀，如 īdem、quīdam 中的 -dem），本义“早在先前”；同族 prius, prīmus, prīstinus
	- 义：long ago, long since, before, formerly（很久以前、早已）；固定搭配 iam prīdem“早已、早就”
	- 本句：iam prīdem sevērī cūstōdēs requīruntur——“（民法的）严格守护者早已为（人们所）求取（期盼）”。iam prīdem 为固定搭配，表“早已”；requīruntur 为历史现在被动
	- 警示：prīdem 表“（自今回溯）很久以前”，与 ōlim（“从前、有一次”）、quondam（“曾经”）近义而分工不同；勿与 prīdiē（“前一天”）相混
	- 关联：iam prīdem 为 Cicero 常用；同义 iam diū, iam dūdum（“早已、久已”）
