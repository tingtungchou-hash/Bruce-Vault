# Text 

\[Cn. Pompeio \[primum] et M. Crasso coss.]  
*C. Verres*, 
	<= perfunctus \[quaestura] \[legatione] \[praetura urbana], 
	\[patre C. Verre] et \[praetextato filio et filia ==numpta==]
	uxorem habens, 
**cum** 
	**==successisset==** in Sicilia *C. Sacerdoti* praetori 
	eam**que** (=Sicilia) \[non sibi sucedente Arrio] \[per triennim] **tenuisset** 
	at**que** in ea **fuisset** libidinose avare crudeliterque versatus, \[succedente tandem \[in eadem provincia] Metello (=*dative*, governed by the particle of succedo)] 
repetundarum reus ab Siculis postulatus est. 

Qui (= Siculi) omnes, \[praeter Syracusanos ac Mamertinos], M. Tullium, 
\[illo iempore] ==florentem== <= \[defensionibus amicorum], 
\[ad accusandum descendere] **compulerunt**, 
	<= \[iam pridem] (=for a long time already) illis necessitudine ==copulatum==, 
		**quod** quaestor in Sicilia fuisset Sex. Peducaeo, 
		et **quod**, \[cum **decederet**], in illa oratione 
			<= quam lilybaei habuit 
		multa his \[benigne] **promissiset**. 

# Translation 

When Gn. Pompeius and M. Crassus were consuls, C. Verres, after the discharge of the quaestorship, a legateship and the urban praetorship, C. Verres being his father and with a son in toga praetexta (i.e. an adult son) and a married daughter, having a wife, after he had taken over from C. Sacerdos the praetor, had held it (i.e. the province of Sicily) for three years since Arrius did not succeed him, and had conducted himself in it licentiously, greedily and cruelly, with Metellus at last succeeding him in the same province, was accused of extortion by the Sicilians.

All of them (i.e. the Siculi), except the Syracusans and the Mamertines, compelled M. Tullius, at that time at the height of his flourishing through the defences of his friends, to descende into accusing, since he had long since been bound to them by a tie of obligation: because he had been quaestor in Sicily under Sex. Peducaeus, and because, when he was departing, in a speech which he delivered at Lilybaeum, he had promised them many things kindly.
