# Original Text
hi sciunt hoc non modo a me petitum esse, sed ita saepe et ita vehementer esse petitum ut aut causa mihi suscipienda fuerit aut officium necessitudinis repudiandum. sed quid ego his testibus utor, quasi res dubia aut obscura sit? adsunt homines ex tota provincia nobilissimi, qui praesentes vos orant atque obsecrant, iudices, ut in actore causae suae deligendo vestrum iudicium ab suo iudicio ne discrepet. omnium civitatum totius Siciliae legationes adsunt praeter duas civitates; quarum duarum si adessent, duo crimina vel maxima minuerentur quae cum his civitatibus C. Verri communicata sunt. 

# Text Analysis
hi sciunt hoc non modo a me petitum esse, sed ita saepe et ita vehementer esse petitum ut aut causa mihi suscipienda fuerit aut officium necessitudinis ==repudiandum==. sed quid ego his testibus utor, ==quasi== res dubia aut obscura sit? adsunt homines ex tota provincia nobilissimi, qui ==praesentes== vos orant atque ==obsecrant==, iudices, ut \[in actore causae suae deligendo] vestrum iudicium ab suo iudicio ne ==discrepet==. omnium civitatum totius Siciliae legationes adsunt praeter duas civitates; quarum duarum si adessent, duo crimina vel maxima minuerentur quae cum his civitatibus C. Verri communicata sunt. 

# Translation
they know this is not merely sought from me, but is sought so often and vehemently that either the case will be taken by me, or the duty of deep bond will be cast off. but why do I even employ these witnesses, as if the thing is either doubtful or obscure? present are the most integret men from the entire province, who present at sight ask and beseech you, judges, that in choosing the advocate of their own your judgement do not disagree with from their own judgement. The embassies of all cities of the entire Sicily are present except two cities; of these two if anyone had been present, two grestest crimes would have been lessened which had been shared between C. Verres and these two cities. 

# Vocabulary

1. repudiō, repudiāre, repudiāvī, repudiātum 1（此处 repudiandum，动形容词 gerundive，中性单数主格）
	- 词形：repudiō, -āre, -āvī, -ātum 1；此处 repudiandum ＝ 动形容词（gerundive，将来时被动分词）中性单数主格，与 officium 性数格一致
	- 词源：出 repudium（re- 加与 pudet“羞耻”相关的词根，本义“休弃、断绝（婚姻）”；L&S 标 repudium 为 pudet 一系的法律术语 t. t.），本义“（决绝地）推开、休弃”；同族 repudium（“休妻、拒绝”）、repudiātus（“被休弃的”）
	- 义：(1) 特指 to cast off, put away, divorce, repudiate（休弃妻室或未婚妻、断绝婚约）；(2) to reject, refuse to accept（拒绝接受，如遗产）；(3) 引申 to reject, refuse; to scorn, disdain, repudiate（拒绝、谢绝、蔑视）——最常见且属典雅散文用语（very freq.），同义 reprobō, aspernor, respuō
	- 本句：officium necessitudinis repudiandum——“亲缘的义务必须被抛弃”。officium 为中性，故用 repudiandum；与并列表中修饰 causa（阴性）的 suscipienda 同表“必须……”
	- 警示：此处 repudiandum 是动形容词（gerundive），非现在分词，含“必须被拒绝”的义务语气；勿与现在分词 repudiāns 相混

2. quasi（不变词，副词 / 连词）
	- 词形：quasi（古体 QVASEI），＝ quam ＋ sī 的缩合，作不变的副词兼连词
	- 词源：由 quam（“如同”）＋ sī（“假如”）缩合，本义“如同（假如）”
	- 义：(1) 本义 as if, just as, as it were（好像、宛如——引虚拟比较；同族 velutī, sīcutī, tamquam）；(2) 引申 about, nearly, almost（大约、近乎——表数量，同族 circiter, ferē）；(3) quasi … quasi（一部分……一部分……）
	- 本句：quasi rēs dubia aut obscūra sit——“好像事情可疑或含混似的”。quasi 引虚拟比较从句，从句动词须用虚拟式，故 sit 用虚拟式
	- 警示：此处取本义 (1)（“好像”，引比较从句），非 (2) 的“几乎、约莫”（那是对数量的用法）；正因是虚拟比较，sit 才用虚拟式，与英语 as if … were 同理

3. praesēns, praesentis（形容词；praesum 的现在分词形容词化）
	- 词形：praesēns, -entis；此处 praesentēs ＝ 阳性复数主格
	- 词源：prae-（“在前”）＋ sum 的词根，本义“在前存在的、在场的”；出自 praesum（“在前、在场”）的现在分词
	- 义：(1) that is before one, in sight or at hand, present, in person（在场的、眼前的、亲自的）；(2) 时间义 present（现在的，与 praeteritus, futūrus 对举）；(3) immediate, instant, prompt（立即的、即刻的）；(4) 引申 efficacious, powerful（灵验的、有力的）
	- 本句：quī praesentēs vōs ōrant atque obsecrant——“他们亲自在场，向你们请愿、恳求”。praesentēs 作定语修饰 quī（即 hominēs）
	- 警示：praesēns 虽为 praesum 的分词，此处已形容词化，取 (1)；其夺格单数指人称常作 praesente、指事物常作 praesentī（不规则）

4. obsecrō, obsecrāre, obsecrāvī, obsecrātum 1（此处 obsecrant，现在时主动直陈第三人称复数）
	- 词形：obsecrō, -āre, -āvī, -ātum 1（亦作 opsecrō）；此处 obsecrant ＝ 现在时主动直陈第三人称复数
	- 词源：ob-（“对着、朝向”）＋ sacrō（“献祭、使神圣”，出 sacer“神圣”），本义“对着（神或人）以神圣之物为凭恳求”，即“苦苦哀求”；同族 obsecrātiō（“恳求”）、sacrō（“奉献”）
	- 义：to beseech, entreat, implore, supplicate, conjure（恳求、哀求、祈求）——语气强于 rogō，同族 obtestor, precor, supplicō；常接宾格（人或事）＋ ut / nē 从句
	- 本句：vōs ōrant atque obsecrant, iūdicēs——“他们请求并苦苦哀求你们，法官们”。orō 与 obsecrō 近义叠用，加强恳求语气
	- 警示：obsecrō 由“以神圣之物起誓恳求”引申为一般“恳求”，非宗教专用；L&S 标其为典雅常用（class.）

5. discrēpō, discrēpāre, discrēpāvī, discrēpātum 1（此处 discrēpet，现在时主动虚拟式第三人称单数）
	- 词形：discrēpō, -āre, -āvī, -ātum 1；此处 discrēpet ＝ 现在时主动虚拟式第三人称单数（受 nē 支配）
	- 词源：dis-（“分开、相离”）＋ crepō（“发响、嘎吱作响”），本义“发出不谐之声、声响不合”，遂引申为“不一致、有分歧”；同族 crepō（“响”）、increpō（“斥责”）
	- 义：(1) 本义 to differ in sound, sound differently, not to harmonize（声音不谐、不合调；少用，多见于 Cicero）；(2) 引申 to disagree, be different, vary, differ（不一致、有分歧、相异）——最常用，同族 differō, distō
	- 本句：ut … vestrum iūdicium ab suō iūdiciō nē discrēpet——“使你们的判断不要与他本人的判断相左”。nē discrēpet 为目的从句（虚拟式）；discrēpō 惯用 ab ＋夺格表“与……相异”
	- 警示：此处 discrēpet 为虚拟式（nē 目的从句），勿误作陈述式；discrēpō 表“相异”常搭 ab ＋夺格（或 cum ＋夺格）
