# Original Text
atque huiusce rei iudicium iam continuo video futurum. si enim mihi hodie respondere ad haec quae dico potueris, si ab isto libro, quem tibi magister ludi nescio qui ex alienis orationibus compositum dedit, verbo uno discesseris, posse te et illi quoque iudicio non deesse et causae atque officio tuo satis facere arbitrabor; sin mecum in hac prolusione nihil fueris, quem te in ipsa pugna cum acerrimo adversario fore putemus? esto, ipse nihil est, nihil potest; at venit paratus cum subscriptoribus exercitatis et disertis. est tamen hoc aliquid, tametsi non est satis; omnibus enim rebus is qui princeps in agendo est ornatissimus et paratissimus esse debet. verum tamen L. Appuleium esse video proximum subscriptorem, hominem non aetate sed usu forensi atque exercitatione tironem.

# Text Analysis
atque huiusce rei iudicium iam ==continuo== video futurum. si enim mihi hodie respondere \[ad haec quae dico] potueris, si ab isto libro, quem tibi magister ludi (nescio qui \[ex alienis orationibus] compositum) dedit, verbo uno discesseris, posse te ***et*** illi quoque iudicio non deesse ***et*** causae atque officio tuo satis facere ***arbitrabor***; sin mecum in hac ==prolusione== nihil fueris, quem te in ipsa pugna cum acerrimo adversario fore putemus? ==esto==, ipse nihil est, nihil potest; at venit paratus cum subscriptoribus exercitatis et disertis. est tamen hoc aliquid, tametsi non est satis; omnibus enim rebus is qui princeps in agendo est ornatissimus et paratissimus esse debet. verum tamen L. Appuleium esse video proximum subscriptorem, hominem non aetate sed usu forensi atque exercitatione tironem.

# Translation
and I see there is going to be a judgement of this thing without delay. if indeed you're able to responde to me today what I say, if from that book - which I do not know is composed of other orations - the teacher of the school gave to you, you gave one word, I think that you can both do not lack the court to him and make enough of the case and your duty; if not with me in this practice fight you were nothing, what do we think to be going to happen with you in the very fight with so harsh an opponent? granted, he is nothing, he is able to do nothing; but he came prepared with incited and skillful subscriptors. thus still there's something, though is is not enough; indeed in terms of all things he who is he first in conducting should be most skillful and prepared. still Lucius Appuleius the immediately next subscriptor is a freshman not only in terms of age but also in terms of forensic practice and exercise. 

而且，我看这件事的裁断马上就要见分晓。因为，如果你今天能够对所说的这些话作出回应，如果你能脱离那本书哪怕一个字——那本书是不知哪个小学教师从别人的演说中拼凑起来交给你的——我就会认为，你在那场审判中也能担当得起，也能对得起你的事业和职责；但如果你在这场预演中对我毫无表现，那么，在与最凶猛的对手真正交锋时，我们还能指望你怎么样呢？就算如此——他本人不名一文，也毫无能耐；可他带着一批训练有素、能言善辩的副控诉人（联署协助控诉者）有备而来。不过，这一点也算有些分量，尽管还不足够；因为在一切方面，居于主控（首席）地位的人，都应当是最出色、准备最充分的。然而，我看到卢基乌斯·阿普莱乌斯是他的次席副控诉人——此人论年纪不算轻，论法庭阅历和实务却仍是个新手。

# Vocabulary

1. continuō adv.
	- 词形：副词；句中 iam continuō … futūrum，iam + continuō 加强“即刻”之意
	- 词源：由 continuus（“连绵不断的”，出 contineō“连在一起”）派生的副词 →“不间断地”→“立即、接连地”；同族 contineō（持、连）、continuātiō（延续）、continuus（连续的）
	- 义：(1) 立即、马上、即刻（forthwith, at once）；(2) 连续地、不断地
	- 本句：atque huiusce reī iūdicium iam continuō videō futūrum——“我看这桩事的裁断马上就要见分晓”
	- 警示：continuō 在此为副词“立即”，勿读作形容词 continuus 的夺格（同形）；近义 statim、prōtinus；注意“立即”与“连续”两义须靠语境分辨

2. prolūsiō, -ōnis f.
	- 词形：第三变格阴性名词，文中为夺格单数 in hāc prolūsiōne；Cicero 把与凯基利乌斯的这场预审交锋喻为正式审判（pugna）前的“预演”
	- 词源：prō-（“在前”）+ lūsiō（“演练”，出 lūdō“玩、演练”）→“事前演练、序幕”；同族 lūdō（玩、演）、lūsus、allūsiō、illūsiō、dēlūsiō；英语 prelude（prae + ludere）、prolusion 出此
	- 义：(1) 预演、序幕、开场练习；(2) 引申：正式较量前的初步交锋（本句）
	- 本句：sin mēcum in hāc prolūsiōne nihil fueris——“但若你在这场预演（初步交锋）中对我毫无表现”
	- 警示：prolūsiō 由 prō- + lūdō 构成，本义“预演”，与 pugna（正式搏斗）对举；勿与 prōlātus（prōferō 的分词）形近相混

3. sum, esse, fuī, futūrus v. irreg.（文中 estō，将来命令式）
	- 词形：estō 是 esse 的将来命令式第二人称单数（2sg estō；3sg estō；2pl estōte；3pl suntō）；此处不作字面命令，而是成语化让步“就算如此”
	- 词源：esse（“是、存在”）的将来命令式，词尾 -tō 为将来命令式标记；无特殊词源，属 sum 的不规则变位
	- 义：(1) 将来命令式：“你须是／你将来要是”；(2) 成语化让步：“姑且如此、就算如此”（granted, be it so, suppose it is so）
	- 本句：estō, ipse nihil est, nihil potest; at …——“就算如此——他本人不名一文、毫无能耐；可是……”
	- 警示：estō 是将来命令式，非现在命令式 es／este；estō 单用作“granted”是 Cicero 常见的让步套语；勿与 edō（吃）的将来命令式 estō 混（此处为 esse）；与虚拟式 sit（“就算是”）功能相近而形式不同
