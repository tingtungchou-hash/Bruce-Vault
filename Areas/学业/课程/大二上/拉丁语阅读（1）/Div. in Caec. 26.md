# Original Text
ego in hoc iudicio mihi Siculorum causam receptam, populi Romani susceptam esse arbitror, ut mihi non unus homo improbus opprimendus sit, id quod Siculi petiverunt, sed omnino omnis improbitas, id quod populus Romanus iam diu flagitat, exstinguenda atque delenda sit: in quo ego quid eniti aut quid efficere possim, malo in aliorum spe relinquere quam in oratione mea ponere. 

# Text Analysis
ego in hoc iudicio mihi Siculorum causam receptam, populi Romani susceptam esse arbitror, **ut** mihi non unus homo improbus opprimendus sit, id quod Siculi petiverunt, sed omnino omnis improbitas, id quod populus Romanus iam diu flagitat, exstinguenda atque delenda sit: in quo ego quid ==eniti== aut quid efficere possim, malo in aliorum spe relinquere quam in oratione mea ponere. 

# Translation
In this judgement, I think by me the case of the Sicilians is received, by me the case of the Roman People is undertaken, that no one wicked man should be oppressed by - what the Sicilians persue - but all the wickedness as a whole whould be oppressed, what the Roman People for so long persues, that I think should be eliminated and damaged: ~~that in which~~ ***in this thing what*** I'm able to force ~~somgthing~~ or effect ~~something~~, I wish more to leave in the hope of others than to put in my oration. 

我认为，在这桩审判中，我已承接了西西里人的案件，也已担起了罗马人民的案件：因此我所需打垮的，并不是某一个恶人——这正是西西里人所求的；而是要彻底铲除、消灭全部的恶本身——这正是罗马人民久已迫切要求的。至于我在此案中究竟能奋力做到什么、能成就什么，我宁愿把它留给别人的期望去承载，而不愿把它写进我自己的演说里。

# Vocabulary

- enitor, eniti, enisus sum — 奋力、竭力；勉力成就、努力做到（deponent 异态动词）。本句 `eniti` 为现在时不定式，与 `efficere` 并列，受 `possim` 支配：`quid eniti aut quid efficere possim` = “我究竟能奋力做到什么、能成就什么”。Lewis & Short 正以此句为书证。警示：`eniti` 在此不是完成时形式，而是 deponent 动词的现在时不定式，与被动不定式同形；其完成分词为 `enisus`（亦作 `enixus`），与 `nitor, niti`（倚靠、奋力）同源。
