# Original Text
ac si tibi nemo responsurus esset, tamen ipsam causam, ut ego arbitror, demonstrare non posses: nunc ne illud quidem cogitas, tibi cum homine disertissimo et ad dicendum paratissimo futurum esse certamen, quicum modo disserendum, modo altercandum, modo omni ratione pugnandum certandumque sit. cuius ego ingenium ita laudo ut non pertimescam, ita probo ut me ab eo delectari facilius quam decipi putem posse. numquam ille me opprimet consilio, numquam ullo artificio pervertet, numquam ingenio me suo labefactare atque infirmare conabitur; novi omnis hominis petitiones rationesque dicendi; saepe in isdem, saepe in contrariis causis versati sumus; ita contra me ille dicet, quamvis sit ingeniosus, ut non nullum etiam de suo ingenio iudicium fieri arbitretur.

# Text Analysis
ac si tibi nemo responsurus esset, tamen ipsam causam, ut ego arbitror, demonstrare non posses: nunc ne illud quidem cogitas, tibi cum homine ==disertissimo== et ad dicendum paratissimo futurum esse certamen, <= (**qui*cum*** modo disserendum, modo ==altercandum==, modo \[omni ratione] pugnandum certandumque **sit**). cuius ego ingenium ita laudo ut non pertimescam, ita probo ut me ab eo ==*delectari*== facilius quam *decipi* putem posse. numquam ille me opprimet consilio, numquam ullo artificio ==pervertet==, numquam ingenio me suo ==labefactare== atque infirmare conabitur; novi omnis hominis petitiones rationesque dicendi; saepe in isdem, saepe in contrariis causis *versati* sumus; ita contra me ille dicet, ==quamvis== sit ingeniosus, ut non nullum etiam de suo ingenio iudicium fieri arbitretur. 

# Translation
and if he is going to respond nothing to you, still you're not able to demonstrate the case itself as I assume: now you know not even this, that to you there will be a decisive contest with a man most skillful and prepared to speak, with whom there's only discussion, only dispute, only fight and contest with all oratory. this man's talent I praise so much that I will not fear, I approve of so much that I think I am able to be delighted by him more easily than to be captured. never will he oppress me with debate, never will he ruin me with any skill, never will he strive with his own talent to shake me in my mind or enfeeble me; I knew ways of attack and reasons of this entire man in terms of addressing a speech; often in these same things, often in contrary cases we engaged ourselves; he talked so contrary to me, ~~anyone you wish is talented~~ ***however much talented he is***, that he thinks some sort of judgement about his own talent is indeed going to take place. 

# Vocabulary

1. disertus, -a, -um adj.（最高级 disertissimus, -a, -um）
	- 词形：文中 disertissimō 为最高级夺格单数阳性，与 homine（homō 的夺格）一致，构成 cum homine disertissimō“与一个最能言善辩的人”；比较级 disertior，最高级 disertissimus
	- 词源：由 disserō（dis- + serō“连接、编排”，即“有条理地铺陈、论述”）派生，字面近“能条分缕析地讲”；同族 disserō（论述）、dissertus（disserō 的完成分词，“论述过的”）、dissertiō（论述）
	- 义：(1) 能言善辩的、口才出众的（eloquent）；(2) 表达清晰、条理分明的
	- 本句：cum homine disertissimō et ad dīcendum parātissimō futūrum esse certāmen——“（你）将与此等最善辩、最擅长陈词之人有一场较量”
	- 警示：disertus（形容词“善辩的”）勿与 dissertus（分词“论述过的”）相混；此处为最高级 + 夺格（cum 支配），不是与格；近义词 ēloquēns、facundus 侧重点略异

2. altercor, altercārī, altercātus sum v. dep.
	- 词形：文中 altercandum 是 altercor 的 gerundive（被动将来分词）中性单数，与 sit 构成无人称迂说（gerundive of obligation）altercandum [sit]“须（与之）争辩”；与 disserendum、pugnandum、certandum 并列
	- 词源：alter（“两者中的另一个”）→ altercor“站到另一边、与对方争执”；同族 altercātiō（口角、争辩）、alter（另一个）；英语 altercation 即出此
	- 义：(1) 争辩、拌嘴、口角；(2) 互不相让地交锋辩论
	- 本句：modo disserendum, modo altercandum … sit——“时而须讨论，时而须争辩”
	- 警示：altercandum 是表义务的 gerundive（无人称），不是作名词的 gerund；altercor 为异态动词（形被动而义主动），勿误作被动；勿与 alter（另一个）、altercātiō（名词）相混

3. dēlectō, dēlectāre, dēlectāvī, dēlectātum v. tr.
	- 词形：文中 dēlectārī 为被动不定式（dēlector, dēlectārī），与 dēcipī 并列，受 putem posse 支配；mē dēlectārī“我被取悦／以……为乐”
	- 词源：dē- + lactāre（lactō“引诱、哄诱”，为 laciō 的反复形），字面“以甘言诱之”→ 使愉悦；同族 dēlectātiō（乐趣、愉悦）、dēlectābilis（可悦的）
	- 义：(1) 使高兴、使愉悦（主动）；(2) 被动 dēlectārī + abl.：以……为乐、从……得乐趣
	- 本句：ita probō ut mē ab eō dēlectārī facilius quam dēcipī putem posse——“我如此认可他，以致我觉得自己更容易被他打动（取悦），而不是被他欺骗”
	- 警示：此处为被动不定式，勿读作主动“取悦”；dēlectō 及物，被动义“被取悦”；勿与 dēligō（挑选／喜爱）、dīligō（珍爱）混淆

4. pervertō, pervertere, pervertī, perversum v. tr.
	- 词形：文中 pervertet 为将来时主动陈述式第三人称单数（pervert-e-t）；承前宾语 mē
	- 词源：per-（“彻底、贯穿”）+ vertō（“转”），字面“彻底翻转”→ 颠倒、颠覆、败坏；英语 pervert 出此；同族 vertō（转）、subvertō（颠覆）、convertō（转变）
	- 义：(1) 翻转、颠倒；(2) 颠覆、败坏、腐蚀（秩序、道德）；(3) 曲解、歪曲
	- 本句：numquam ūllō artificiō pervertet——“他绝不会用任何伎俩把我带坏／引入歧途”
	- 警示：pervertet 是将来时（-e- 主题元音），非完成时；宾语 mē 承上句省略；与 prōvertō、dēvertō 形近，注意前缀之别

5. labefactō, labefactāre, labefactāvī, labefactātum v. tr.
	- 词形：文中 labefactāre 为主动不定式，与 infirmāre 并列，作 conābitur 的补足不定式；numquam … mē … labefactāre atque infirmāre conābitur
	- 词源：labō（“摇晃、将倒”）+ faciō（“使”）→“使摇晃”（亦作 labefaciō）；同族 labō（动摇）、labāscō（开始摇晃）、lābēs（坍塌）；与 labor（劳作）、labrum（唇）无涉
	- 义：(1) 使摇晃、使将倒；(2) 动摇、削弱（意志、地位、信念）
	- 本句：numquam ingeniō mē suō labefactāre atque infirmāre conābitur——“他绝不会试图用他的才华来动摇并削弱我”
	- 警示：由 labō + faciō 构成，勿按 lab- 误系 labor／labrum；此处为主动不定式，非分词；labefactāre 与 infirmāre 近义连用，加强语气

6. quamvis adv./conj.
	- 词形：quam + vīs（volō 的第二人称单数，“随你多少”）→“无论到何种程度”；作让步连词时后接虚拟式，此处 quamvis sit ingeniosus 构成让步从句
	- 词源：quam（“如、多么”）+ vīs（“你愿”），字面“尽你所要之多”→ 无论多么；同类用法另见 quamlibet、quantumvis
	- 义：(1) 让步连词：“尽管、虽然、无论多么”（+ 虚拟式）；(2) 直接修饰形容词／副词：“无论多么……的”
	- 本句：ita contrā mē ille dīcet, quamvis sit ingeniosus, ut …——“他对我陈词时，尽管才华出众，也会……（以致他觉得人们也在评判他自己的才华）”
	- 警示：quamvis 表让步，勿与 quam（“比、如何”）及 quamquam（“虽然”，多带陈述式）相混；接从句用虚拟式（sit），非陈述式
