# Original Text
etenim si plurimas a tuo praetore iniurias accepisses, tamen eas ferendo maiorem laudem quam ulciscendo mererere; cum vero nullum illius in vita rectius factum sit quam id quod tu iniuriam appellas, hi statuent hanc causam, quam ne in alio quidem probarent, in te iustam ad necessitudinem violandam videri? qui si summam iniuriam ab illo accepisti, tamen, quoniam quaestor eius fuisti, non potes eum sine ulla vituperatione accusare; si vero non ulla tibi facta est iniuria, sine scelere eum accusare non potes. quare cum incertum sit de iniuria, quemquam horum esse putas qui non malit te sine vituperatione quam cum scelere discedere?

# Text Analysis
etenim si plurimas a tuo praetore iniurias accepisses, tamen eas ferendo *maiorem* laudem *quam* ulciscendo ==mererere==; ***cum*** vero nullum illius in vita rectius factum sit quam id quod tu iniuriam appellas, hi statuent hanc causam, quam \[ne in alio quidem] **probarent**, in te iustam \[ad necessitudinem violandam] videri? qui si summam iniuriam ab illo accepisti, tamen, quoniam quaestor eius fuisti, non potes (eum sine ulla ==vituperatione== accusare); si vero non ulla tibi facta est iniuria, sine scelere eum accusare non potes. quare cum incertum sit de iniuria, quemquam horum esse putas qui non *malit* te sine vituperatione *quam* cum scelere discedere?

# Translation
and indeed even if you received a lot from your praetor, still I praesied them greater in terms of bearing than you deserve by practising revenge; since nothing of him is made more lawful than what you call injustice, will they set up the case, which they not even ***approve of in another one*** ~~in another way prove~~ to seem justified ***in you*** for the sake of violating personal bond ~~against you~~? even if you received the greatest injustice from him, still because of having been his quaestor, you can't accuse him without any censure; if indeed no injury has been done to you, you're not able to accuse him without a crime. thus since it is unclear regarding the injury, whotever of these do you think is one who do not wish more that you quit without censure than with crime? 

因为即使你从你的长官那里遭受了极多的侵害，你忍受这些侵害也比报复它们更能赢得赞扬；然而，他一生中做得最正当的事，恰恰就是你所称为侵害的那件事，难道这些陪审法官会判定：这个连在别人身上他们都不会认可的理由，在你身上却被视为足以正当化破坏亲近关系的理由？因为如果你从他那里受到了极大的侵害，那么，由于你曾是他的财务官，你毕竟不能不受到任何指责地控告他；而如果你根本没有受到任何侵害，那么你控告他就不能没有罪过。所以，既然侵害与否尚不确定，你认为这些人当中会有谁不宁愿让你不受指责地退出，而要让你带着罪过退出呢？
