# Original Text
verum id mihi non sumo, iudices, et hoc non modo in oratione mea non pono, sed ne in opinione quidem cuiusquam relinquo, me omnibus patronis esse praepositum. non ita est; sed unius cuiusque temporis, valetudinis, facultatis ad agendum ducta ratio est. mea fuit semper haec in hac re voluntas et sententia, quemvis ut hoc mallem de iis qui essent idonei suscipere quam me, me ut mallem quam neminem. 

# Text Analysis
verum id mihi non sumo, iudices, et hoc non modo in oratione mea non pono, sed ***ne*** in opinione ***quidem*** cuiusquam relinquo, me omnibus patronis esse praepositum. non ita est; sed unius cuiusque temporis, valetudinis, facultatis ad agendum ducta ratio est. mea fuit semper haec in hac re voluntas et sententia, ==quemvis== ut hoc ==mallem== de iis qui essent idonei suscipere quam me, me ut mallem quam neminem. 

# Translation
For me, I do not take it to be true, judges, and I not only do not put this in my oration, but also do not even leave in anyone's opinion, that I am preferred to all other patrons (of the Sicilians). It is not like that; but consideration is led to action of one particular time, health, ability. My will and consideration have always been, in this thing, very much that I prefer those who were ideal to uptake the case rather than me, and that I prefer myself to nobody.  

# Vocabulary

1. quīvīs, quaevīs, quōdvīs（此处 quemvīs，阳性单数宾格）
	- 词形：quīvīs（＝ quī＋vīs 缩合的不定代词，另作 quīvīs, quaevīs, quidvīs），三性异干；此处 quemvīs ＝ 阳性单数宾格
	- 词源：quī（关系-疑问代词词干）＋ -vīs（＝ vīs / vel，“任意、随便”，出 volō“愿”），字面“你愿要的那一个”，遂为“任何一个、无论谁”
	- 义：(1) whoever you will, anyone at all（无论谁、任何一个——代词性用法）；(2) any … whatsoever（无论哪个——形容词性用法）
	- 本句：quemvis ut hoc mallem de iis qui essent idonei suscipere quam me——“我宁可让随便哪个合适的人（而非我自己）来接这案子”。quemvīs 为阳性单数宾格，与 suscipere 构成宾格＋不定式，作 mallem 的宾语内容，并与 quam me 构成比较
	- 警示：quemvīs 与让步连词 quamvīs（“尽管、虽然”）形近而不同——此处是代词 quī 加 -vīs 的屈折形，非连词 quam 加 vīs。须凭句法功能辨别
	- 关联：同义不定代词 quīlibet（“任何一个，不论哪个”）、quisque；-vīs 亦见于 quamvīs、quantumvīs 等

2. mālō, mālle, māluī（此处 māllem，未完成时主动虚拟式，第一人称单数）
	- 词形：mālō, mālle, māluī（不规则动词，无完成被动分词）；此处 māllem ＝ 未完成时主动虚拟式，第一人称单数（1sg. imperf. subj. act.）
	- 词源：mā-（＝ magis，“更”）＋ volō（“愿、想要”）缩合，本义“更想要、宁可”；volō / nōlō / mālō 同出 volō 一词族
	- 义：to prefer, wish rather（宁愿、宁可）
	- 本句：quemvis ut hoc mallem … suscipere quam me, me ut mallem quam neminem——两处 māllem 分属两个 ut 引出的内容从句（即 haec voluntas et sententia 的具体内容）。主句 fuit 为过去时，从句取未完成时虚拟式；与 quam 呼应表比较（“宁……而不……”“宁取此而不取彼”）
	- 警示：māllem 勿与 mālleus, -ī m.（“锤子”）相混；亦勿与 volō 的未完成虚拟式 vellem（“但愿”）相混——语义相近而词干不同（mālu- 对 vol-）
	- 关联：nōllem（“宁愿不”）；māllem … quam … 为常见比较搭配；参见同页 vellem、mālim 等虚拟式形式
