# Original Text
nunc tantum id dicam quod tacitus tu mihi adsentiare, nullam rem in me esse quam ille contemnat, nullam in te quam pertimescat. itaque magnus ille defensor et amicus eius tibi suffragatur, me oppugnat; aperte ab iudicibus petit ut tu mihi anteponare, et ait hoc se honeste sine ulla invidia ac sine ulla offensione contendere. 'non enim,' inquit, 'illud peto quod soleo, cum vehementius contendi, impetrare: reus ut absolvatur non peto, sed ut potius ab hoc quam ab illo accusetur, id peto. da mihi hoc; concede quod facile est, quod honestum, quod non invidiosum; quod cum dederis, sine ullo tuo periculo, sine infamia illud dederis, ut is absolvatur cuius ego causa laboro.'

# Text Analysis
nunc tantum id dicam quod tacitus tu mihi ==adsentiare==, nullam rem in me esse quam ille ==contemnat==, nullam in te quam ==pertimescat==. itaque magnus ille defensor et amicus eius tibi suffragatur, me oppugnat; aperte ab iudicibus petit ut tu mihi anteponare, et ait hoc se honeste sine ulla invidia ac sine ulla offensione contendere. 'non enim,' inquit, 'illud peto quod ==soleo==, cum vehementius contendi, ==impetrare==: reus ut absolvatur non peto, sed ut potius ab hoc quam ab illo accusetur, id peto. da mihi hoc; concede quod facile est, quod honestum, quod non invidiosum; quod cum dederis, sine ullo tuo periculo, sine infamia illud dederis, ut is absolvatur cuius ego causa laboro.'

# Translation
now I say it which you agree with me unspokenly, that nothing is in me which he desdains, that nothing is in you which he fears greatly. and thus the great defensor and his friend support you, opposes me; he openly sought from judges that you be preferred over me, and says he honestly contend this without any jealousy and offence. "not indeed" he said, "I persue what I'm accustomed to, since they effect to be contended more vehemently: as the accused I do not seek in order that I am acquited, but in order that he is accused more possible by this one than by that one, this I persue. give me this; concede what is easy, what is respected, what is not plotful; because when you give it, you give it without any danger of yours, without ill fame, so that he is acquited for whose case I struggle." 

现在我只需说一点，这一点你即便默不作声也会赞同我：我身上没有什么是他所轻视的，你身上也没有什么是他所畏惧的。因此，他那位大名鼎鼎的辩护人兼朋友支持你，攻击我；他公开向审判者请求让你优先于我，并声称自己提出这一要求光明正大，不会招来任何嫉妒，也不会冒犯任何人。“因为，”他说，“我所请求的，并不是我惯常在更为激烈地力争时所求得的那个结果：我不请求让被告无罪开释；我所请求的是，让被告宁可由此人来控告，而不是由彼人来控告。把这一点给我吧；答应这件容易的事、体面的事、不会招人忌恨的事吧；你一旦答应，便是在毫无自身危险、毫无污名的情况下答应下来：让那个人获释——我正是为他而奔走。”

# Vocabulary

1. adsentior（亦作 assentior；主动形 adsentio），assentīrī，assēnsus sum，第四变位异相动词。词形：文中 adsentiāre 为第二人称单数现在时虚拟式（异相动词，形式被动、意义主动）。词源：ad-“向”＋sentiō“感觉、认为”，字面为“在感觉上靠向某人”，故表“附和、赞同”；ad- 在 s 前同化为 as-，因此 ads-／ass- 两种拼写并存。义：同意、赞同某人或某事，常与与格或中性宾格连用。本句：quod tacitus tu mihi adsentiāre——“这一点你即便默不作声也会赞同我”；quod 为中性宾格，mihi 为与格，虚拟式构成特征性关系从句。警示：古典散文通用异相形式 assentior，主动形式 adsentiō 罕见，西塞罗仅书信中三见；勿与 assentor, assentārī“奉承、一味附和”相混。关联：sentiō、sēnsus、assēnsiō、assēnsus。

2. contemnō（亦作 contempnō），contemnere，contempsī，contemptum，第三变位动词。词形：文中 contemnat 为第三人称单数现在时主动虚拟式。词源：con-（加强）＋temnō“轻视”，同根词有 contemptus、contemptim。义：轻视、蔑视、认为不足挂齿；引申为“不惧怕、不以为意”。本句：nullam rem in me esse quam ille contemnat——“我身上没有哪一样东西是他认为不足挂齿的”，即他对我无所不忌惮；quam 的先行词是 rem，contemnat 因处于间接引语中而用虚拟式。警示：此处 L&S 直接以此句为证，注明 contemnat = nōn timeat，不可只按“鄙视”理解；勿与 condemnō“定罪、谴责”相混。关联：contemptus、contemptim、dēspiciō、neglegō；反义 timēō、metuō。

3. pertimescō，pertimescere，pertimuī，第三变位起始动词。词形：文中 pertimescat 为第三人称单数现在时主动虚拟式。词源：per-（加强）＋timescō“开始害怕”，而 timescō 为 timeō“害怕”的起始形式；-scō 表“开始进入某种状态”。义：大为恐惧、十分害怕、对……极为忌惮。本句：nullam in te quam pertimescat——“你身上没有哪一样东西是他所畏惧的”；quam 的先行词为 rem，虚拟式同样因间接引语。警示：per- 只加强程度，不改变支配关系，仍带宾格；勿与 perterreō“使惊恐”（及物）相混；完成时 pertimuī。关联：timeō、timor、timidus、horrēscō。

4. soleō，solēre，solitus sum，第二变位半异相动词。词形：文中 soleō 为第一人称单数现在时主动陈述式。词源：传统词源上与 suēscō“习惯于”、cōnsuēscō、cōnsuētūdō“习惯”相联系（L&S 以 cf. suesco 标示）。义：惯常、习惯于、往往；后接不定式。本句：quod soleō, cum vehementius contendi, impetrare——“我惯常在更为激烈地争讼时求得的东西”；quod 为 impetrāre 的宾语，cum 为时间连词，contendī 为第一人称单数完成时。警示：半异相动词，现在系统主动、完成系统被动形（solitus sum）；勿与 sōlus“独自的”相混；ut solet 为插入语“如常”。关联：suēscō、cōnsuētūdō、solitus。

5. impetrō，impetrāre，impetrāvī，impetrātum，第一变位动词。词形：文中 impetrāre 为现在时主动不定式，依附于 soleō。词源：in-＋patrō“完成、实行”，字面“使之完成”，故为“求得、办成”。义：（通过请求或努力）求得、获准、达到；可带宾格，或以 ut／nē 引出内容从句。本句：quod soleō... impetrare——“我惯常……求得的东西”，所指即被告的无罪开释（illud）。警示：勿与 imperō“命令、统治”（带与格）相混；impetrāre aliquid ab aliquō 为“向某人求得某物”。关联：patrō、patrātus、perpetrō、impetrātiō。
