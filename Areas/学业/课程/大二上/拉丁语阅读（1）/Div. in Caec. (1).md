# Original Text 
si quis vestrum, iudices, aut eorum qui adsunt, forte miratur me, qui tot annos in causis iudiciisque publicis ita sim versatus ut defenderim multos, laeserim neminem, subito nunc mutata voluntate ad accusandum descendere, is, si mei consili causam rationemque cognoverit, una et id quod facio probabit, et in hac causa profecto neminem praeponendum mihi esse actorem putabit. 

# Text Analysis 
**si** quis <= vestrum, iudices, aut {quis} eorum <= qui adsunt, forte **miratur** me, 
	<= (**qui** tot annos \[in causis iudiciisque publicis] ita **sim** versatus **ut** **defenderim** multos, **laeserim** neminem), 
\[subito nunc] \[mutata voluntate] \[ad accusandum] descendere, 
**is** {=quis ita miratur}, **si** (mei consili {<consilium}) => causam rationem**que** **cognoverit**, 
*una et id quod facio* **probabit**, et \[in hac causa] ==profecto== neminem ==praeponendum== mihi esse actorem **putabit**. 

# Translation 
If anyone of you, judges, or anyone of those who are present, marvels hard at that I, who is so engaged that many years in cases and public jurisdiction that I had defended many while attacked nobody, now so quickly with changed will descend into accusation, then he, if he had knew the reason and reasoning of my decision, and
and in this case, nothing is laid forth by my as an advocate
