# Original Text
adductus sum, iudices, officio, fide, misericordia, multorum bonorum exemplo, vetere consuetudine institutoque maiorum, ut onus huius laboris atque offici non ex meo, sed ex meorum necessariorum tempore mihi suscipiendum putarem. quo in negotio tamen illa me res, iudices, consolatur, quod haec quae videtur esse accusatio mea non potius accusatio quam defensio est existimanda. defendo enim multos mortalis, multas civitates, provinciam Siciliam totam; quam ob rem, quia mihi unus est accusandus, prope modum manere in instituto meo videor et non omnino a defendendis hominibus sublevandisque discedere.

# Text Analysis
adductus sum, iudices, officio, fide, misericordia, multorum bonorum exemplo, vetere consuetudine institutoque maiorum, ut onus huius laboris atque offici non ex meo, sed ex meorum necessariorum tempore mihi suscipiendum putarem. quo in negotio tamen illa me res, iudices, ==consolatur==, quod haec quae videtur esse accusatio mea non potius accusatio quam defensio est existimanda. defendo enim multos mortalis, multas civitates, provinciam Siciliam totam; quam ob rem, \[quia mihi unus est accusandus], ==prope modum== manere in instituto meo **videor** et non omnino a defendendis hominibus sublevandisque discedere.

# Translation
I'm bound, judges, by duty, faith, mercy, the example of many good people, the time-honoured tradition and institutions of our ancestors, that not out of me, but out of the situation of my bonds, I assume the load of this labour and duty should be done by me. In which business still, judges, that thing encourages me, that what seems to be my accusation should be estimated as a defence no less than an accusation. I defend many mortal men, many cities, the entire province of Sicily; for this reason, because only one should be accused by me, in a similar way I was seen to remain in my way of conduct and not to totally leave away from defending and elleviating the pain of people. 

# Vocabulary

1. prope modum（副词短语，亦作 propemodum）
	- 词形：prope 为副词（“近于、几乎”）；modum 为 modus, -ī m. 的宾格，此处作状语性宾格；二者连用，凝固为短语
	- 词源：prope 本义“在近处”，与介词 prō 同源，同族有 propior、proximus；modus 本义“尺度、量度”，引申为“限度、方式”，同族有 moderor、modestus、modicus
	- 义：almost, nearly, pretty much, to a great extent（几乎、差不多、大致）
	- 本句：quam ob rem, quia mihi unus est accusandus, prope modum manere in instituto meo videor et non omnino a defendendis hominibus sublevandisque discedere——“因此，因为我要控告的只有一个人，我似乎几乎仍留在自己原来的行事路数上，并未完全离开为人们辩护、替人们纾困这件事”。prope modum 修饰 manere，并与句末的 non omnino（并非完全）配成一对弱化语气的说法
	- 警示：勿与 ad modum（依照……的方式，后接属格或形容词）、eius modi（这一类的）、modo（刚才／只要）相混；要表达“以相似的方式”应用 eodem modo / similiter / pari modo。此处 prope 是副词“几乎”，不是形容词“相似的”
