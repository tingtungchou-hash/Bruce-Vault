# Original Text
Putasne te posse quae C. Verres in quaestura, quae in legatione, quae in praetura, quae Romae, quae in Italia, quae in Achaia, Asia Pamphyliaque peccarit, ea, quem ad modum locis temporibusque divisa sint, sic criminibus et oratione distinguere? Putasne posse, id quod in eius modi reo maxime necessarium est, facere ut, quae ille libidinose, quae nefarie, quae crudeliter fecerit, ea aeque acerba et indigna videantur esse his qui audient atque illis visa sunt qui senserunt? 

# Text Analysis
Putasne te posse quae C. Verres in quaestura, quae in legatione, quae in praetura, quae Romae, quae in Italia, quae in Achaia, Asia Pamphyliaque peccarit, ea, quem ad modum locis temporibusque divisa sint, sic criminibus et oratione ==distinguere==? Putasne posse, id quod in eius modi reo maxime necessarium est, facere ut, quae ille libidinose, quae nefarie, quae crudeliter fecerit, ea aeque acerba et indigna **videantur** esse his qui audient atque illis **visa sunt** qui **senserunt**? 

# Translation
Do you think that you are thus able to distinguish with crimes and oration what C. Verres wronged in quaestorship, in legation, in praetorship, in Rome, in Italy, in Achaia, Asia and Pamphylia - these in that way are ~~divided~~ ***distributed*** both in terms of time and places? Do you think that you're able to do what is most necessary for the accused in that way, so that what he did with lust, nefarity, cruelity, are seen by those who ~~hear~~ ***audit*** them, or are seen by those who know them ***(suffer from him themselves)***, to be things equally harsh and of indignity? 

你以为，你能把盖乌斯·维勒斯在财务官任上、在使节任上、在裁判官任上，在罗马、在意大利、在阿凯亚、亚细亚和潘菲利亚所犯下的种种罪过，依照它们在地点与时间上的分布，同样用罪状和陈述逐一分辨清楚吗？你以为你能做到那件对这类被告而言最要紧的事吗——让他那些出于恣意、伤天害理、残忍成性而做出的事，在听审者看来，与在亲身遭受者看来，同样地令人痛切、令人愤慨？

# Vocabulary

1. distinguō, distinguere, distīnxi, distīnctum（此处 distinguere，现在时主动不定式）
	- 词形：第三变格法动词；distinguō, distinguere, distīnxi, distīnctum；此处 distinguere 为现在时主动不定式，作 posse 的补足语（Putasne te posse … distinguere）
	- 词源：dis-（“分开”）+ 词根 stig-（“刺、刻记号”；见希腊语 στίζω“刺、刻”，拉丁语 stīgma“烙印”）；字面“用记号分开”，故为“区分、划界”。同族 distīnctiō（“区分、差别”）、distīnctus（“分明的、清晰的”）
	- 义：(1) 分开、隔开（separate, divide）；(2) 区分、辨别（distinguish, discriminate）；(3) 点缀、装饰（adorn, diversify；本句不取此义）
	- 本句：ea … criminibus et oratione distinguere——“用罪状与陈述把这些（罪行）逐一分辨清楚”。与前半句 quem ad modum locis temporibusque divisa sint 形成对照：材料按地点、时间被划分（dīvidō），演说者则须再用 crimina 与 oratio 把它切分开（distinguō）
	- 警示：勿按英语 distinguish 的单一义理解；distinguō 的核心是“用标记分开”，既可指实际的分隔，也可指修辞上的条分缕析。distinguō 与 dīvidō 近义而角度不同：dīvidō 强调“分成若干部分”，distinguō 强调“划出界限、使之判然有别”。此处是不定式，不是命令式
