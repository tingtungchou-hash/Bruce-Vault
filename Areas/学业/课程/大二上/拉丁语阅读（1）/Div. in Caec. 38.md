# Original Text
Putasne te posse quae C. Verres in quaestura, quae in legatione, quae in praetura, quae Romae, quae in Italia, quae in Achaia, Asia Pamphyliaque peccarit, ea, quem ad modum locis temporibusque divisa sint, sic criminibus et oratione distinguere? Putasne posse, id quod in eius modi reo maxime necessarium est, facere ut, quae ille libidinose, quae nefarie, quae crudeliter fecerit, ea aeque acerba et indigna videantur esse his qui audient atque illis visa sunt qui senserunt? 

# Text Analysis
Putasne te posse quae C. Verres in quaestura, quae in legatione, quae in praetura, quae Romae, quae in Italia, quae in Achaia, Asia Pamphyliaque peccarit, ea, quem ad modum locis temporibusque divisa sint, sic criminibus et oratione ==distinguere==? Putasne posse, id quod in eius modi reo maxime necessarium est, facere ut, quae ille libidinose, quae nefarie, quae crudeliter fecerit, ea aeque acerba et indigna **videantur** esse his qui audient atque illis **visa sunt** qui **senserunt**? 

# Translation
Do you think that you are thus able to distinguish with crimes and oration what C. Verres wronged in quaestorship, in legation, in praetorship, in Rome, in Italy, in Achaia, Asia and Pamphylia - these in that way are divided both in terms of time and places? Do you think that you're able to do what is most necessary for the accused in that way, so that what he did with lust, nefarity, cruelity, are seen by those who hear them, or are seen by those who know them, to be things equally harsh and of indignity? 

你以为，你能把盖乌斯·维勒斯在财务官任上、在使节任上、在裁判官任上，在罗马、在意大利、在阿凯亚、亚细亚和潘菲利亚所犯下的种种罪过，依照它们在地点与时间上的分布，同样用罪状和陈述逐一分辨清楚吗？你以为你能做到那件对这类被告而言最要紧的事吗——让他那些出于恣意、伤天害理、残忍成性而做出的事，在听审者看来，与在亲身遭受者看来，同样地令人痛切、令人愤慨？
