# Praeteritio的两种使用模式

经过对一些praeteritio的例子的阅读，我发现可以对之前笔记的用语进行一些精细化。西塞罗其实不乏在自己对praeteritio的定义上对它的使用，也就是避免冗长、不体面、说不清、易被驳倒或不宜径直点明；但这并不影响对praeteritio的理解方向：和诸多克制简短的praeteritio相比，*de imperio Pompeio*和*in Caeciliulum*中的几例和西塞罗的定义相脱离的使用反而显得像是一种异常现象，这不能不使人好奇西塞罗的用意——他自己的“制造疑窦”足以解释这几处冗长的例子吗？他如何理解自己的这种行为呢？

现在我认为，我的研究流程应该需要稍作修改：首先尝试寻找程式语言（praetereo ... / (ego) non dico）或语法结构界定praeteritio，梳理可以算作praeteritio的用例；其次，要基于西塞罗*Rhetorica ad Herennium*中的定义，尝试划定praeteritio忠实或不忠实于定义的两种使用；再重点关注不忠实的一类，尝试结合语境考虑其修辞效果

> **in Caec. 29** ==Nec ea dico==, quae si dicam tamen infirmare non possis, te, antequam de Sicilia decesseris, in gratiam redisse cum Verre; Potamonem, scribam et familiarem tuum, retentum esse a Verre in provincia, cum tu decederes; M. Caecilium, fratrem tuum, lectissimum atque ornatissimum adulescentem, non modo non adesse neque tecum tuas iniurias persequi, sed esse cum Verre et cum illo familiarissime atque amicissime vivere. Sunt et haec et alia in te falsi accusatoris signa permulta, quibus ego nunc non utor: hoc dico, te, si maxime cupias, tamen verum accusatorem esse non posse. 

> **de imperio Gn. Pompeio 45** Iam accepta in Ponto calamitate ex eo proelio, ==de quo vos paulo ante invitus admonui==,—cum socii pertimuissent, hostium opes animique crevissent, satis firmum praesidium provincia non haberet,—amisissetis Asiam, Quirites, nisi ad ipsum discrimen eius temporis divinitus Cn. Pompeium ad eas regiones fortuna populi Romani attulisset. Huius adventus et Mithridatem insolita inflammatum victoria continuit, et Tigranem magnis copiis minitantem Asiae retardavit. Et quisquam dubitabit quid virtute perfecturus sit, qui tantum auctoritate perfecerit? aut quam facile imperio atque exercitu socios et vectigalia conservaturus sit, qui ipso nomine ac rumore defenderit?

# Praeteritio与Damnatio Memoriae的勾连

Praeteritio的格式——“宣称”不言明，实则揭示——让我想起Damnatio Memoriae，在Damnatio Memoriae的实施过程中，以碑铭除名为例，似乎一些碑铭除名的方式仍能让人辨识出来，Polly Low写道：

> not so much as an attempt to **obliterate memory** entirely as to **transform honorific commemoration into a form of visible denigration**. That is: the power of an act of _damnatio_ relies, at least in part, **on the viewer of a monument being able to supplement the gaps in an inscription with their own knowledge of what those gaps had once contained**, and the reasons why the text had been removed - Polly Low, Remembering, Forgetting, and Rewriting the Past 

Praeteritio和Damnatio Memoriae二者都出现了宣称和实行之间的错位、都是通过否认来施行、都需要观者的先在了解（the viewer ... supplement(s) the gaps in an inscription with their own knowledge of what those gaps had once contained）来实现效果，前者否认提及，实则详述，后者宣称要抹除记忆，实则重构和加强了记忆；可以说二者在结构上是同构的。

当然，不能因此说二者有什么直接联系或者历史的影响，我更倾向于说Damnatio Memoriae的机制有助于理解Praeteritio和演说中的记忆操纵，但二者间意图层级的差别（是否刻意为之？例如，Damnatio Memoriae留下痕迹在多数情况下还是一个“执行不力”的技术问题）、时间性的差别（praeteritio是即时演说，而Damnatio Memoriae涉及了对过去立起来的碑铭的修改，由此和更广的时间尺度上的历史产生了联系）等，还需要更谨慎的考量。

# 文献阅读

截至目前找来的讨论Praeteritio的文献有：
- Snoeck Henkemans (2009) Manoeuvring strategically with praeteritio. *Argumentation*. 
- Ulrike Claudia Ariane Stephan (2016) Limits of Speech: Studies on Omissions in Ancient Oratory and Rhetoric. (PhD thesis, University of London) 
- Joy Connolly (2007) The State of Speech: Rhetoric and Political Thought in Ancient Rome. 
以及一篇牛津书目
- Robert N. Gaines (2017) Latin Rhetoric 
同时，还有关于damnatio memoriae的：
- Adrastos Omissi (2016) Damnatio Memoriae or Creatio Memoriae? Memory Sanctions as Creative Processes in the Fourth Century AD. *The Cambridge Classical Journal*  
- Harriet I. Flower The Art of Forgetting: Disgrace and Oblivion in Roman Political Culture. 

其中，Gaines的书目、Henkemans和Omissi的文章已经读过。Omissi的文章中提到，Damnatio Memoriae这个术语让人误以为其所指涉的概念是古罗马人有而如今丢失的，然而，恰恰与这种印象相反，它本身是十七世纪的产物，古罗马人没有一个叫做Damnatio Memoriae的术语，Damnatio Memoriae也不是一个有清晰概念和实践的法令实体。这警示我应该更加谨慎地处理Praeteritio，以及它和Damnatio Memoriae的遥相呼应：正如上次笔记中的Poebammon the Sophist所说，Praeteritio是一种思想（νόημα）上的修辞，它是一种表达意思的方式，而和文字的字面排布（如格位、连系词、标点等）没有直接联系；这就牵扯出了我们如何界定Praeteritio的问题。Henkemans从现代修辞理论出发给出了Praeteritio的不同类型，我认为可资参考，但在处理上文提及的西塞罗的不同用法时帮助不大。 

由于讨论范围仅限于Praeteritio的文章相对有限，我接下来打算从已有的例子入手，查阅它们的commentary，或是查阅Gaines书目当中关于omission/memory/irony等的书目、参考Brill Companian to Latin Rhetoric，以便找到更多围绕它或古或今的学术讨论。
