# Original Text
ac vide quantum interfuturum sit inter meam tuamque accusationem. ego etiam quae tu sine Verre commisisti Verri crimini daturus sum, quod te non prohibuerit, cum summam ipse haberet potestatem: tu contra ne quae ille quidem fecit obicies, ne qua ex parte coniunctus cum eo reperiare. quid? illa, Caecili, contemnendane tibi videntur esse, sine quibus causa sustineri, praesertim tanta, nullo modo potest? aliqua facultas agendi, aliqua dicendi consuetudo, aliqua in foro, iudiciis, legibus aut ratio aut exercitatio?

# Text Analysis
ac vide quantum interfuturum sit inter meam tuamque accusationem. ego etiam quae tu sine Verre ==commisisti== Verri crimini daturus sum, quod te non prohibuerit, cum summam ipse haberet potestatem: tu contra **ne** quae ille quidem fecit obicies, **ne** qua ex parte coniunctus cum eo reperiare. quid? illa, Caecili, contemnendane tibi videntur esse, sine quibus causa sustineri, praesertim tanta, nullo modo potest? aliqua facultas agendi, aliqua dicendi consuetudo, aliqua in foro, iudiciis, legibus aut ratio aut ==exercitatio==?

# Translation
and see there would be what between my accusation and yours. even I will assign to the crime of Verres what you commited without Verres, because ~~you~~ ***he*** had not stopped ~~him~~ ***you***, when he had the greatest power: you rather, (acted) lest either that you leaked what he did, or that you are found connected with him from that part. what? does it seems to you that it should be desdained, Caecilius, without which the case is in no way able to be undertaken, especially ~~as a whole~~ ***so great a case***? some faculty of conducting, some custom of speaking, some in the forum, some courts, laws either reason or training? 

你且看看，我的控诉与你的控诉之间将有多大的差别。凡你未经维勒斯之手所犯下的事，我甚至要算作维勒斯的罪状，因为他身居最高权力，却没有制止你；你反过来，连他本人做过的事都不敢加以指责，唯恐在哪个方面被人发现与他有牵连。怎么，凯基利乌斯？难道在你看来，那些东西是可以轻视的吗？没有它们，一桩案子——尤其是如此重大的一桩——根本无从支撑：一定的诉讼能力，一定的演说习惯，以及在广场、法庭和法律事务中的一定方法或训练。

# Vocabulary

- commīsistī：committō, committere, commīsī, commissum 3. 的完成时主动直陈第二人称单数，“犯下、做出（错事、罪行）；托付、交付；交战”。文中 quae tū sine Verre commīsistī 为关系从句，quae 为中性复数宾格，作 commīsistī 的宾语；整个从句作 Verrī crīmini datūrus sum 的宾语（先行词省略，关系词被吸引入从句）。此处 sine Verre 意为“未经维勒斯参与、在维勒斯不知情的情况下”。警示：勿与 commīsī（完成时第一人称单数）相混，-istī 才是第二人称单数词尾。参见 [[committō, committere, commīsī, commissum 3.]]。
- exercitātiō, exercitātiōnis f.（第三变格法）：练习、训练、实践，尤指演说与诉讼方面的训练。文中 aliqua ... exercitātiō 为名词性列举的末项，与 ratiō 并列，共同说明 illa 所指为何（“某种方法或训练”）；in forō, iūdiciīs, lēgibus 同时统摄 ratiō 与 exercitātiō。词源：exercitō（exerceō 的反复动词）+ -tiō。
