# Original Text
fortasse dices: 'quid ergo? haec in te sunt omnia?' Vtinam quidem essent! verum tamen ut esse possent magno studio mihi a pueritia est elaboratum. quodsi ego haec propter magnitudinem rerum ac difficultatem adsequi non potui, qui in omni vita nihil aliud egi, quam longe tu te ab his rebus abesse arbitrare, quas non modo antea numquam cogitasti, sed ne nunc quidem, cum in eas ingrederis, quae et quantae sint suspicari potes? 

# Text Analysis
==fortasse== dices: 'quid ergo? haec in te sunt omnia?' ==Vtinam== quidem essent! verum tamen **ut** esse possent magno studio mihi a pueritia est elaboratum. quodsi ego haec propter magnitudinem rerum ac difficultatem adsequi non potui, <= (qui in omni vita nihil aliud egi, quam longe tu te ab his rebus abesse arbitrare, quas non modo antea numquam cogitasti, sed ne nunc quidem, \[cum in eas ingrederis], quae et quantae sint suspicari potes)? 

# Translation
maybe you'll say, "what then? are these all in you?" ~~there're one thing yet~~ ***I wish they are indeed***! indeed however, ~~as~~ ***in order that*** they're able to be, it is laboured to me with great passion from childhood. but if I wasn't able to follow this because of the magnitude of things and difficulty, which in the entire life I did nothing else, how long do you think to be absent from these things, what did you not only never be aware of before but also even now, when you set out into these affairs, you are not able to suspect what and how great they are. 

也许你会说：“那么怎样？这一切你身上都有吗？”但愿如此！不过，为了能使它们如此，我从少年时代起就付出了极大的努力。但假如我由于事情本身的重大和艰难而未能达到这些——我毕生所做的事，除此以外别无其他——那么，你又以为自己离这些事情有多远呢？这些事情你从前不仅从未想过，而且甚至现在，当你开始涉足其中时，也无法猜度它们是什么、有多么重大。

# Vocabulary

1. fortasse, adv.
	- 词形：副词；异体 fortassis。此处 fortasse dīcēs，fortasse 修饰全句，dīcēs 为 dīcō 的将来时直陈式第二人称单数
	- 词源：与 fors（“机缘、偶然”）同源，一般认为由 fors sit an（“或许是”）一类结构凝缩而来；字面“凭机缘”，引申为“也许”。同族 fors、fortūna（“命运、运气”）、fortuītō（“偶然地”）、forsitan（“也许”）
	- 义：也许、或许、可能（perhaps, maybe, possibly）
	- 本句：fortasse dīcēs——“也许你会说”。Cicero 预设对方的反驳，用 fortasse 使语气显得从容，留有余地
	- 警示：勿与 forte 混淆：forte 本为 fors 的夺格，意为“偶然、碰巧”，也可作“也许”，但更偏古旧或诗语；fortasse 是散文中最常用的“也许”。另注意 fortasse 通常带直陈式（如这里的 dīcēs），而 forsitan 常带虚拟式

2. utinam, 祈愿小品词
	- 词形：由 uti（ut 的旧形式）+ nam（强调小品词）构成；后接虚拟式。此处 Vtinam quidem essent，essent 为 sum 的未完成时虚拟式、第三人称复数，与 haec ... omnia 一致
	- 词源：uti + nam，字面近于“但愿如此”。同族 ut、utī
	- 义：但愿、要是……就好了、我多么希望（if only, would that, I wish）
	- 本句：Vtinam quidem essent!——“但愿它们真的都在我身上！”这是对 Caecilius 质问的让步性回答，言下之意是事实并非如此；quidem 加强愿望的语气
	- 警示：utinam 后必须用虚拟式，不用直陈式。时态决定愿望的性质：现在时虚拟式指将来可能实现（utinam sint），未完成时虚拟式指现在与事实相反（utinam essent），过去完成时虚拟式指过去与事实相反（utinam fuissent）。勿与目的或结果连词 ut 混淆；Vtinam 中的 V 只是大写 U
