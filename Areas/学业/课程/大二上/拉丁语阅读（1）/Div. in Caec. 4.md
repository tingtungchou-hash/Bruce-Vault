# Original Text
tuli graviter et acerbe, iudices, in eum me locum adduci ut aut eos homines spes falleret qui opem a me atque auxilium petissent, aut ego, qui me ad defendendos homines ab ineunte adulescentia dedissem, tempore atque officio coactus ad accusandum traducerer. dicebam habere eos actorem Q. Caecilium, qui praesertim quaestor in eadem provincia post me quaestorem fuisset. quo ego adiumento sperabam hanc a me posse molestiam demoveri, id mihi erat adversarium maxime; nam illi multo mihi hoc facilius remisissent si istum non nossent, aut si iste apud eos quaestor non fuisset. 

# Text Analysis
tuli \[graviter et acerbe], iudices, in eum me locum adduci 
**ut** **aut** eos homines spes falleret (qui opem a me atque auxilium **petissent**), **aut** ego, qui me \[ad defendendos homines] \[ab ==ineunte== adulescentia] **dedissem**, \[tempore atque officio] coactus ad accusandum **traducerer**. 
dicebam habere eos actorem Q. Caecilium, qui praesertim quaestor in eadem provincia post me quaestorem fuisset. 
quo ego adiumento sperabam hanc a me posse ==molestiam== demoveri, id mihi erat adversarium maxime; **nam** *illi* \[multo] mihi \[hoc facilius] **remisissent** **si** istum non **nossent**, aut **si** iste apud eos quaestor non fuisset. 

# Translation
I was troubled heavily and harshly, judges, in this situation I deveoted myself, either that hope failed those people who sought action and help from me, or that I, who had dedicated myself to defending others from entering youghly imprudence, compelled by situation and duty, was drawn to accusing others. I said they had Q. Caecilius as the advocate, the very one who had been quaestor in the province after me, with whose help I hoped the trouble could be removed from me - it was undesirable to me to the greatest extent. For they would have returned to me so much more easily if they do not know him, or if he was not quaestor among them.

# Grammar

## 1. nam illi multo mihi hoc facilius remisissent si istum non nossent, aut si iste apud eos quaestor non fuisset. 
### 语法树
```
主句 nam … remisissent（内含过去反事实条件句）
├─ 主语 illi
├─ 谓语 remisissent
├─ 与格 mihi
├─ 宾语 hoc
├─ 程度状语 multo
├─ 方式状语 facilius
└─ 条件从句（两个并列 protasis）
   ├─ si istum non nossent
   │  ├─ 宾语 istum
   │  └─ 谓语 nossent
   └─ aut si iste … non fuisset
      ├─ 主语 iste
      ├─ 表语 quaestor
      ├─ 处所状语 apud eos
      └─ 谓语 fuisset
```
### 细部用法
> 译文：因为，要是他们不认识他，或者他要是不曾在他们那里当过财务官，他们本来会对我这件事宽容得多。
