# Original Text
mancipes a civitatibus pro frumento pecuniam exegerunt. quid? hoc Verre praetore factum est solum? non, sed etiam quaestore Caecilio. quid igitur? daturus es huic crimini quod et potuisti prohibere ne fieret et debuisti, an totum id relinques? ergo id omnino Verres in iudicio suo non audiet quod, cum faciebat, quem ad modum defensurus esset non reperiebat. atque ego haec quae in medio posita sunt commemoro: sunt alia magis occulta furta, quae ille, ut istius, credo, animos atque impetus retardaret, benignissime cum quaestore suo communicavit.

# Text Analysis
==mancipes== a civitatibus pro frumento pecuniam exegerunt. quid? hoc Verre praetore factum est solum? non, sed etiam quaestore Caecilio. quid igitur? daturus es huic crimini quod et potuisti prohibere ne fieret et debuisti, an totum id relinques? ergo id omnino Verres in iudicio suo non audiet quod, cum faciebat, quem ad modum defensurus esset non ==reperiebat==. atque ego haec quae in medio posita sunt commemoro: sunt alia magis occulta furta, quae ille, \[ut istius, credo, animos atque impetus retardaret], benignissime cum quaestore suo communicavit.

# Translation
The contractors took away money from cities in place of grains. what? is this only done with Verres being the praetor? no, but also when Caecilius was the quaestor. therefore what? you'll give to this crime ~~both what~~ ***which*** you're ***both*** able and responsible to prevent from happening, or will you leave this thing altogether? thereby Verres in his own case does not ~~dare~~ ***hear*** it entirely because, he does not find to which mode he will be defended when doing it. and I remember what are positioned in the middle: there're other more concealled thefts, which he, ~~as~~ ***in order to delay the mind and impulse of this person*** ~~his~~, I believe, ~~delays the minds and attacks~~, in the most friendly way ~~after~~ he has communicated with his own quaestor. 

粮食承包商们向各城邦征收了折抵粮食的现钱。怎么？这种事只在维列斯任裁判官时才发生吗？不，凯基利乌斯任财务官时也照样发生了。那么，你打算就这项罪名提出指控吗——尽管你本来既能、也本应阻止它发生？还是要把这整件事一概放过？所以，维列斯在自己的审判中根本不会去听这件事，因为他在做的时候就想不出该怎样为自己辩护。而我在这里所说的，还只是那些摆在明面上的事；另外还有一些更隐蔽的盗窃，我想，他为了压一压此人（凯基利乌斯）的心气与冲劲，极为“慷慨”地同自己的财务官分享了。

# Vocabulary
- mancipēs：manceps, -ipis m.（第三变格法），“承包人、包税人、公开拍卖的买主”；此处为复数主格，作 exegerunt 的主语，指替国家承包粮食供应的承包人，向各城市收取款项以代替谷物。参见 [[manceps, -ipis m.]]。
- reperiēbat：reperiō, reperīre, repperī, repertus 4.，“发现、找到；找到（办法）”；此处为未完成时主动直陈第三人称单数，主语为 Verres，quem ad modum ... esset 为间接疑问从句作宾语。参见 [[reperiō, reperīre, repperī, repertus 4.]]。
