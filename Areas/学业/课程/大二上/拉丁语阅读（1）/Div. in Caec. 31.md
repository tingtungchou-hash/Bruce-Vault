# Original Text
Caecili, quid facies? utrum hoc tantum crimen praetermittes an obicies? si obicies, idne alteri crimini dabis quod eodem tempore in eadem provincia tu ipse fecisti? audebis ita accusare alterum ut quo minus tute condemnere recusare non possis? sin praetermittes, qualis erit tua ista accusatio, quae domestici periculi metu certissimi et maximi criminis non modo sponsionem, verum etiam mentionem ipsam pertimescat? 

# Text Analysis
Caecili, quid facies? utrum hoc tantum crimen ==praetermittes== an obicies? si obicies, idne alteri crimini dabis quod eodem tempore in eadem provincia tu ipse fecisti? audebis ita accusare alterum ut quo minus tute condemnere ==recusare== non possis? sin praetermittes, ==qualis== erit tua ista accusatio, quae \[domestici periculi metu] certissimi et maximi criminis non modo ==sponsionem==, verum etiam mentionem ipsam pertimescat? 

# Translation
Caecilius, what are you going to do? are you going to let go or ~~throw away~~ ***reveal*** such a grave crime? if you are indeed going to ~~throw~~ ***reveal*** it ~~away~~, are you going to give to another crime (Verres') what at the same time in the same province you yourself had done? will you dare to accuse another one that you can't refuse in? but if you let it go, of what sort is your accusation, which, for the worry of ~~domestic danger~~ ***dangers done to itself, fears most certain and greatest crime not only the sponse, but even truly the calling to mind itself***?

“凯基利乌斯，你打算怎么办？这桩如此重大的罪行，你是要放过，还是要揭发？如果你揭发，难道你要把自己在同一时期、同一行省亲手做过的事，算作别人的罪状吗？你竟敢这样控告别人，以致自己也无法推脱被定罪的结局吗？但如果你放过它，你这场所谓的控告又算什么呢？它因惧怕危及自身，不仅畏惧那项正式保证金，甚至连提起那桩最确凿、最重大的罪行都感到害怕。”
