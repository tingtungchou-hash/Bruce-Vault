# Original Text
Vtrum, Q. Caecili, hoc dices, me non Siculorum rogatu ad causam accedere, an optimorum fidelissimorumque sociorum voluntatem apud hos gravem esse non oportere? si id audebis dicere, quod C. Verres, cui te inimicum esse simulas, maxime existimari vult, Siculos hoc a me non petisse, primum causam inimici tui sublevabis, de quo non praeiudicium, sed plane iudicium iam factum putatur, quod ita percrebruit, Siculos omnis actorem suae causae contra illius iniurias quaesisse.

# Translation
Whether you, Quintus Caecilius, say that I approach this case not by the request of the Sicilians, or that it is not proper for the will of the best and loyalest allies to be important among these things? If you dare say this, which Gaius Verres, whom you liken yourself an enemy of, wishes the most to be thought, that is that the Sicilians had not asked this from me, at the very first you will let go the case of your enemy, about which not a preceding judgement but a judgement now openly conducted is assumed, which is so prevalent that the advocate of their own case against injustice of that one.
