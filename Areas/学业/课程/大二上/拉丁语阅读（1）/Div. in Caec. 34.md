# Original Text
haec tu scis ad me esse delata; quae si velim proferre, facile omnes intellegent vobis inter vos non modo voluntatem fuisse coniunctam, sed ne praedam quidem adhuc esse divisam. quapropter si tibi indicium postulas dari quod tecum una fecerit, concedo, si id lege permittitur; sin autem de accusatione dicimus, concedas oportet iis qui nullo suo peccato impediuntur quo minus alterius peccata demonstrare possint.

# Text Analysis
haec tu scis ad me esse delata; quae si velim proferre, facile omnes intellegent vobis inter vos non modo voluntatem fuisse coniunctam, sed **ne** ==praedam== **quidem** adhuc esse divisam. quapropter si tibi ==indicium== postulas dari quod tecum una fecerit, concedo, si id lege permittitur; ==sin== autem de accusatione dicimus, concedas oportet iis qui nullo suo ==peccato== ==impediuntur== quo minus alterius peccata demonstrare possint.

# Translation
This you know is reported to me; which if I wish to leak forth, easily all know that not only had willingness been connected among you but also that the spoil is not even divided yet till this time. because of which, if you demand the information to be given to you which he did with him together, I concede, if it is allowed by law; if we talk about the accusation however, you concede that it is proper to them who are hampered by nothing mistaken of theirs, in a way that they are able to demonstrate it is mistaken of another one.  

你知道，这些事情已经有人报到我这里来了。如果我愿意把它们抖出来，所有人都不难明白：你们彼此之间不仅意志一致，而且连赃物至今都还没有瓜分。因此，如果你要求把‘他与你共同做过的事’作为告发材料交给你，我同意——只要法律允许；但如果我们谈的是由谁提起控诉，那你就应当让给那些没有因自身罪过而受阻、因而能够揭露别人罪过的人。
