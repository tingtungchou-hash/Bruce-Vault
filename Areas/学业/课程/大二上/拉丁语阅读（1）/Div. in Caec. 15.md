# Original Text
at enim cur a me potissimum hoc praesidium petiverunt? si esset dubium petissent necne, dicerem cur petissent: nunc vero, cum id ita perspicuum sit ut oculis iudicare possitis, nescio cur hoc mihi detrimento esse debeat, si id mihi obiciatur, me potissimum esse delectum. 

# Text Analysis
at enim cur a me potissimum hoc praesidium petiverunt? si esset dubium petissent necne, dicerem cur petissent: nunc vero, cum id ita perspicuum sit ut oculis iudicare possitis, nescio cur hoc mihi detrimento esse debeat, si id mihi obiciatur, me potissimum esse delectum. 

# Translation
or indeed because from me they had persued this protection? if there had been doubt whether they sought or not, I tell why they sought: now indeed, since it is so transparent that you're able to indicate to eyes, I don't know why this should be of account to me, if it is thrown to me, I am pleased to the greatest extent.
