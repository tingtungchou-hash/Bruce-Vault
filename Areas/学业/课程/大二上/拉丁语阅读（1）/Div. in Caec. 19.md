# Original Text
quis ergo est qui neget oportere eorum arbitratu lege agi quorum causa lex sit constituta? Sicilia tota si una voce loqueretur, hoc diceret: 'quod auri, quod argenti, quod ornamentorum in meis urbibus, sedibus, delubris fuit, quod in una quaque re beneficio senatus populique Romani iuris habui, id mihi tu, C. Verres, eripuisti atque abstulisti; quo nomine abs te sestertium miliens ex lege repeto.' si universa, ut dixi, provincia loqui posset, hac voce uteretur: quoniam id non poterat, harum rerum actorem quem idoneum esse arbitrata est ipsa delegit.

# Text Analysis
quis ergo est qui neget oportere eorum arbitratu lege agi quorum causa lex sit constituta? Sicilia tota si una voce loqueretur, hoc diceret: 'quod auri, quod argenti, quod ornamentorum in meis urbibus, ==sedibus==, delubris fuit, quod \[in una quaque re] beneficio senatus populique Romani iuris habui, id mihi tu, C. Verres, ==eripuisti== atque ==abstulisti==; quo nomine abs te sestertium miliens ex lege repeto.' si universa, ut dixi, provincia loqui posset, hac voce uteretur: quoniam id non poterat, harum rerum actorem quem idoneum esse arbitrata est ipsa delegit.

# Translation
who therefore is a person who deny that it is proper to be legally implemented, in accordance with the assempution of them for whose sake the law is set up? The entire Sicily, if it complained with one voice, they would say this: "what gold, what silver, what ornaments had been in my cities, seats, and shrines, which I had thanks to one certain thing, that is, the good deeds of law of the Senate and People of Rome, this you, Gaius Verres, plucked and even took away from me; in this name I seek to restore from you millions of sesterces with the aid of law." if the entire province, as I said, had been able to speak, they would have used this voice: since it couldn't, it chose itself the advocate of these things whom people assumed to be ideal. 

那么，谁会否认：应当依照那些法律为之而制定的人的意愿，依法进行诉讼呢？整个西西里若能以一个声音说话，它就会这样说：“凡在我的城邦、居所、神庙中曾有过的金、银、饰物，凡我在每一件事上凭元老院与罗马人民的恩惠而享有的一切权利，这一切都是你，盖乌斯·维勒斯，从我手中夺走并掠去的；为此，我依法向你追索一亿塞斯特斯。”如果整个行省，正如我所说，能够开口说话，它就会发出这样的声音；既然它不能，它便亲自选定了它认为适格的人，来担任这些事务的诉讼代理人。 

# Vocabulary

1. sedibus（sēdēs, sēdis f. 的夺格复数）
	- 词形：sēdēs, sēdis f.（第三变格法；属格复数作 sedum，别体 sedium）；此处 sedibus ＝ 夺格复数，与 urbibus、delubris 并列，同受介词 in 支配，表处所
	- 词源：sēdēs 出 sedeō（“坐、坐落”），本义“坐处、座位”；同族 sella（“座椅”）、sedeō、sedīle
	- 义：(1) 本义，that on which one sits——bench, chair, throne（坐具、座椅、宝座）；(2) 转义（本篇所取），a place where one stays——seat, dwelling-place, residence, habitation, abode, temple（住所、居所、栖身之地、神庙）
	- 本句：quod auri, quod argenti, quod ornamentorum in meis urbibus, sedibus, delubris fuit——“凡在我的城邦、居所、神庙中曾有过的金、银、饰物”。sedibus 取转义 (2) “住所、居所”，与 delubris（神庙）并举；三个夺格复数连缀，涵盖公私各处，为下文“尽被你掳掠”张本
	- 警示：夺格复数 sedibus 与主格单数 sēdēs 形近，须凭 in ＋ 夺格辨其格数；亦勿与 sedeō 的虚拟式 sedīs（“你或坐”）相混
	- 关联：同义 domicilium, locus, habitatio, domus；同族 sedeō, sella, sedīle

2. ēripuistī（ēripiō 的完成时主动直陈第二人称单数）
	- 词形：ēripiō, ēripere, ēripuī, ēreptum 3（第三变格法 -iō 类）；此处 ēripuistī ＝ 完成时主动直陈，第二人称单数，“你夺走了”
	- 词源：ē-（＝ ex-，“出”）＋ rapiō（“抓、夺、掠”），本义“（自……）抢出、夺走”
	- 义：(1) to snatch, tear, or pull out; to snatch away, take away（抢出、拽出、夺走）；(2) 转义 to take away, snatch away（剥夺——宾语常为 iūs, lībertās, dolor, timor 等抽象物）；(3) 与 sē 连用，sē ēripere“脱身、逃脱”
	- 本句：id mihi tū, C. Verres, ēripuistī atque abstulistī——“这一切都是你，盖乌斯·维勒斯，从我处夺去并卷走的”。ēripuistī（从我手中抢走）与 abstulistī（掳掠而运去）近义连用，递进加重指控
	- 警示：ēripiō 属 -iō 类第三变格法，完成时为 ēripuī（非 *ēripsī），-p- 音出 rapiō 词干，勿比照 capiō（cēpī）类推
	- 关联：同义 rapiō, auferō, adimō, dēmō, tollō, diripiō；本篇第 18 段 ēreptae（“被夺去的”，分词）同源

3. abstulistī（auferō 的完成时主动直陈第二人称单数）
	- 词形：auferō, auferre, abstulī, ablātum（不规则动词，ferō 的复合）；此处 abstulistī ＝ 完成时主动直陈，第二人称单数，“你运走了、卷走了”
	- 词源：ab-（“离开、去”）＋ ferō（“携、载”）；完成时借用 ferō 的异根词干 tul-（cf. tulī），ab- 的完成形作 abs-，故拼作 abstulī，字面“携之而去”
	- 义：(1) to take or bear off or away, carry off, withdraw, remove（带走、携去、移去）；(2) 更强义 to take by force, rob, steal（强夺、劫掠、窃取）；(3) 引申 to destroy, consume, kill（消灭、耗尽、致死），to leave off, desist（抛开、停止）
	- 本句：L&S 于 auferō 条即引本句为证——quod auri, quod argenti, quod ornamentorum in meis urbibus fuit, …, ēripuistī atque abstulistī（Cic. Div. in Caecil. 5, 19，即本课第 19 段）——“城中所藏的金、银、饰物，尽被你掳掠而去”。abstulistī 承 ēripuistī，一“夺”一“运”，合写维勒斯劫掠后又转移赃物
	- 警示：auferō 为 ferō 的不规则复合，完成时 abstulī（非 *abferī），过去分词 ablātum；ab- 在完成形中作 abs-，勿按规则动词类推而误拼 *abtulī
	- 关联：同义 tollō, rapiō, ēripiō, diripiō, adimō；同族 ferō, tulī, lātus（异根变化），cf. auferre 之完成 abstulī
