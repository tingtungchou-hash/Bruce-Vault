# Original Text 
\[1] si quis vestrum, iudices, aut eorum qui adsunt, forte miratur me, qui tot annos in causis iudiciisque publicis ita sim versatus ut defenderim multos, laeserim neminem, subito nunc mutata voluntate ad accusandum descendere, is, si mei consili causam rationemque cognoverit, una et id quod facio probabit, et in hac causa profecto neminem praeponendum mihi esse actorem putabit. 

# Text Analysis 
**si** quis <= vestrum, iudices, aut {quis} eorum <= qui adsunt, ==forte== **miratur** me, 
	<= (**qui** tot annos \[in causis iudiciisque publicis] ita **sim** versatus **ut** **defenderim** multos, **laeserim** neminem), 
\[subito nunc] \[mutata voluntate] \[ad accusandum] descendere, 
**is**, **si** (mei consili {<consilium}) => causam rationem**que** **cognoverit**, 
\[una] *et id quod facio* **probabit**, et \[in hac causa] \[==profecto==] neminem ==praeponendum mihi== esse actorem **putabit**. 

# Translation 
If anyone of you, judges, or anyone of those who are present, ***happens to*** marvel~~s~~ ~~hard~~ at that I, who is engaged in cases and public jurisdiction for that many years in such a way that I had defended many while attacked nobody, now suddenly with changed will descend into accusation, then he, if he had knew the reason and reasoning of my decision, he will both approve in one go what I do and think that in this case, ~~nothing~~ ***no one*** is actually ~~laid forth by~~ ***preferred to*** me as an advocate

# Vocabulary 

1. forte（adv.）
	- 词源：fors, fortis f.（"偶然、机运"）的夺格，固定用如副词；亦见 fors（名词用法，"或许"，多用于诗体）
	- 义：by chance, by accident, casually, accidentally（常与 casu、temere、fortuna 连用）
	- 本句：si quis vestrum, iudices, aut eorum qui adsunt, forte miratur me...——"若你们中有人，法官们，或在场者中有人，碰巧对我……感到不解"
	- 警示：勿与 fortis（"勇敢的、强壮的"）的夺格 forte（"以强力"）相混；亦勿与 fortasse / forsitan（"也许"，表推测）相混——forte 说的是偶然，不是推测

2. profectō（adv.）
	- 词源：prō + factō（factum 的夺格），字面"就既成之事而言"；L&S 归为 particle of affirmation, confirmation, and declaration
	- 义：assuredly, certainly, undoubtedly, really, indeed（加强肯定语气的虚词）
	- 本句：et in hac causa profecto neminem praeponendum mihi esse actorem putabit——"而且他必定会认为，在这桩案子里没有人应当被置于我之上而任起诉人"
	- 警示：勿与 proficīscor 的完成时分词 profectus（"已出发的"）相混；与 re verā / reāpse（"实际上"）不同，profectō 表说话人的确信，不含"与表面相反"的对照义

3. praepōnendum mihi（praepōnō 的动形词 + 与格）
	- 词形：praepōnō, praepōnere, praeposuī, praepositum 3（prae- "在前" + pōnō "放置"）
	- 义：(1) 置于前、放在……之上；(2) 更看重、宁取（prefer，近 praeferō）；(3) 任命为……之长（set over as commander or governor，如 aliquem provinciae praepōnere）
	- 本句：neminem praeponendum mihi esse actorem——putabit 引导的间接引语（acc. + inf.）：neminem ... actorem 为宾格主语与其表语，praeponendum esse 为动形词表被动义务，mihi 为与格（比较对象）。译："（他必定会认为）没有人应当被置于我之上、充任（本案的）起诉人"
	- 警示：mihi 是与格，不是"被我"；praepōnō 的比较结构为 praepōnere aliquem alicui（把某人置于某人之上），故 mihi 正与 neminem 相对
