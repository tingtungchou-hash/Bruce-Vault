# Original Text
\[2] Cum quaestor in Sicilia fuissem, iudices, itaque ex ea provincia decessissem ut Siculis omnibus iucundam diuturnamque memoriam quaesturae nominisque mei relinquerem, factum est uti cum summum in veteribus patronis multis, tum non nullum etiam in me praesidium suis fortunis constitutum esse arbitrarentur. quare nunc populati atque vexati cuncti ad me publice saepe venerunt, ut suarum fortunarum omnium causam defensionemque susciperem. me saepe esse pollicitum, saepe ostendisse dicebant, si quod tempus accidisset, quo tempore aliquid a me requirerent, commodis eorum me non defuturum. 

# Text Analysis 
**Cum** quaestor in Sicilia **fuissem**, iudices, ita**que** \[ex ea provincia] **decessissem** 
**ut** \[Siculis omnibus] iucundam diuturnamque memoriam <= (quaesturae nominisque mei) **relinquerem**, 
factum est (**uti** ==cum== *summum* in veteribus patronis multis, ==tum== non *nullum* \[etiam in me] *praesidium* suis fortunis constitutum esse **arbitrarentur**). 
**quare** \[nunc] populati atque ==vexati== ==cuncti== \[ad me publice saepe] **venerunt**, 
**ut** (suarum fortunarum omnium) => causam defensionemque **susciperem**. 
me saepe esse ==pollicitum==, saepe ostendisse **dicebant**, 
**si** quod tempus accidisset, \[quo tempore] ==aliquid a me requirerent==, commodis eorum me non defuturum. 

# Translation 
Since I had been a quaestor in Sicily, judges, and had left that province in such a way that I had left to the Sicilians pleasant and long-standing memory of my quaestorship and my name, it had so happened that people thought since defence was given for their fortunes, much in their many old patrons, but no less in me. For which reason, the plundered and crushed now always all came to me publicly, in order that I take the case and defence of all their fortunes. They said that I often promised, had often shown myself, and would not be absent from their interests, if the time had come at which they requires something from me.  

# Vocabulary

1. cum ... tum（关联连词组合）
	- 词源：cum 为连词，古体拼写作 quom，与关系/疑问词干 quo- 同源；tum 为指示副词 "那时、然后"，与 tam、tot、tantisper 同族（词干 *to-）。二者本各自独立，搭配使用后固化为 "既……又……" 的关联结构
	- 义：单用 cum 时表时间 "当……的时候"；作 cum ... tum 时表并列递进，"既……又……"（both ... and；not only ... but also）。第二项亦可由 tum vero、tum etiam 加强
	- 本句：factum est uti cum summum in veteribus patronis multis, tum non nullum etiam in me praesidium suis fortunis constitutum esse arbitrarentur——"结果是，人们认为，对于他们的财产而言，既在许多旧日的庇护人身上有着最大的保障，甚至在我身上也有一份不小的保障"
	- 省略：第二分支承前省略了重复的 praesidium（brachylogy），故 non nullum 单独出现时仍须以 praesidium 补足理解
	- 警示：此处 cum 是连词，不是介词 cum（+ 夺格，"和……一起"），也不是引导时间/原因/让步从句的 cum（那须从句谓语用虚拟式）。识别要点：介词 cum 后接夺格名词，关联结构 cum 之后必有 tum 呼应

2. vexātī（vexō 的完成被动分词，阳性复数主格）
	- 词形：vexō, vexāre, vexāvī, vexātum 1
	- 词源：L&S 归为 vehō（"运送、载运"）的反复动词（frequentative），本义 "来回颠簸"，引申为 "骚扰、蹂躏"
	- 义：harassed, harried, ravaged, plundered, distressed（"被骚扰的、被蹂躏的"）
	- 本句：quare nunc populati atque vexati cuncti ad me publice saepe venerunt——"因此，如今被劫掠、被蹂躏的全体人，屡次公开地来到我这里"
	- 警示：vexati 是完成被动分词，此处与 populati 并列，作名词化形容词修饰 cuncti；不是完成时被动陈述式（那须与 sunt 等连用）。勿与 vehemens（"激烈的"）相混

3. cūnctī（cunctus -a -um 的阳性复数主格）
	- 词形：cūnctus, -a, -um；多见于复数 cūnctī（人）、cūncta（事物）
	- 词源：一般认为由 coniunctus（"连接在一起的"）缩略而来（L&S: contr. from coniunctus）
	- 义：all together, all, the whole body of, entire——强调 "作为整体的全部"（the whole collectively）
	- 本句：... populati atque vexati cuncti ad me publice saepe venerunt——"被劫掠、被蹂躏的全体（西西里人）"
	- 警示：与 omnēs（"所有的"，逐一计数）有别：cuncti 着眼整体，omnes 着眼个体总和。勿与异相动词 cūnctor, cūnctārī（"拖延、迟疑"）相混——cuncti 是形容词，cunctantur 才是那个动词

4. pollicitum（polliceor 的完成被动分词，阳性单数宾格）
	- 词形：polliceor, pollicērī, pollicitus sum 2（异相动词 deponent）
	- 词源：por-（= prō，"向前"）+ liceor（"出价、竞买"），字面 "向前出价"，引申为 "允诺、承诺"（L&S 将 polliceor 系于 por- 与 liceor）
	- 义：to promise, offer, hold out, undertake（"承诺、许诺"）；分词 pollicitum = "（已）承诺的"
	- 本句：me saepe esse pollicitum, saepe ostendisse dicebant——"他们说我常常承诺、常常表明（过）"。此处 esse pollicitum 为完成时不定式，作 dicebant 的间接陈述
	- 警示：polliceor 为异相动词，完成时以被动形式表主动义（pollicitus sum）。pollicitum 与中性主格/宾格同形，勿据此误判为中性——此处应按宾格主语 me（阳性）断为阳性宾格单数。勿与 polluō（"污染"）、pollūceō（"献祭"）相混

5. aliquid a mē requīrerent（requīrō 的未完成主动虚拟式，第三人称复数）
	- 词形：requīrō, requīrere, requīsīvī, requīsītum 3
	- 词源：re-（"再、回"）+ quaerō（"寻找、求取"），本义 "再寻求"，引申为 "索求、追问、调查"
	- 义：to seek again; to seek, ask for, demand, require（"索求、要求"）
	- 本句：si quod tempus accidisset, quo tempore aliquid a me requirerent——"如果出现了某个时机，在那个时机他们要向我索求什么"。quo tempore 引出的关系从句以 tempus 为先行词；aliquid（中性单数宾格）为不定代词 "某物、某事"，a me（a + 夺格）表来源
	- 警示：requirerent 用未完成主动虚拟式，因整句处于 dicebant 所引间接陈述之内，与 accidisset 同属次时序列，虚拟式表示设想中的情形。勿与 requiēscō（"休息"）相混

# Grammar

## 1. factum est uti cum summum in veteribus patronis multis, tum non nullum etiam in me praesidium suis fortunis constitutum esse arbitrarentur

### 语法树

```
主句 factum est
└─ 主语从句 uti … arbitrarentur
   ├─ 谓语 arbitrarentur
   └─ 间接引语（宾格+不定式）
      ├─ 主语 praesidium
      ├─ 谓语 constitutum esse
      ├─ 与格 suis fortunis
      └─ 定语（并列）
         ├─ cum summum
         │  └─ 领域 in veteribus patronis multis
         └─ tum non nullum
            └─ 领域 etiam in me
```
