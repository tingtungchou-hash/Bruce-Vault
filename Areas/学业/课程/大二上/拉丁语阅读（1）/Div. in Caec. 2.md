# Original Text
\[2] Cum quaestor in Sicilia fuissem, iudices, itaque ex ea provincia decessissem ut Siculis omnibus iucundam diuturnamque memoriam quaesturae nominisque mei relinquerem, factum est uti cum summum in veteribus patronis multis, tum non nullum etiam in me praesidium suis fortunis constitutum esse arbitrarentur. quare nunc populati atque vexati cuncti ad me publice saepe venerunt, ut suarum fortunarum omnium causam defensionemque susciperem. me saepe esse pollicitum, saepe ostendisse dicebant, si quod tempus accidisset, quo tempore aliquid a me requirerent, commodis eorum me non defuturum. 

# Text Analysis 
**Cum** quaestor in Sicilia **fuissem**, iudices, ita**que** \[ex ea provincia] **decessissem** 
**ut** \[Siculis omnibus] iucundam diuturnamque memoriam <= (quaesturae nominisque mei) **relinquerem**, 
factum est (**uti** cum summum in veteribus patronis multis, tum *non nullum* \[etiam in me] *praesidium* suis fortunis constitutum esse **arbitrarentur**). 
**quare** \[nunc] populati atque ==vexati== ==cuncti== \[ad me publice saepe] **venerunt**, 
**ut** (suarum fortunarum omnium) => causam defensionemque **susciperem**. 
me saepe esse ==pollicitum==, saepe ostendisse **dicebant**, 
**si** quod tempus accidisset, \[quo tempore] ==aliquid a me requirerent==, commodis eorum me non defuturum. 

# Translation 
Since I had been a quaestor in Sicily, judges, and had left that province in such a way that I had left to the Sicilians pleasant and long-standing memory of my quaestorship and my name, it had so happened that people had thought that at that time not no defence was taken even by me for their fortunes, with in many old patrons 

in many old patrons, then not no help even against me 

For which reason, the plundered and crushed now always all came to me publicly, 
in order that I take the case and defence of all their fortunes. 
They said that I often promised, had often shown myself, and would not be absent from their interests, if the time had come at which they requires something from me.

## 1. factum est uti cum summum in veteribus patronis multis, tum non nullum etiam in me praesidium suis fortunis constitutum esse arbitrarentur

### 语法树

```
主句 factum est
└─ 主语从句 uti … arbitrarentur
   ├─ 谓语 arbitrarentur
   └─ 间接引语（宾格+不定式）
      ├─ 主语 praesidium
      ├─ 谓语 constitutum esse
      ├─ 与格 suis fortunis
      └─ 定语（并列）
         ├─ cum summum
         │  └─ 领域 in veteribus patronis multis
         └─ tum non nullum
            └─ 领域 etiam in me
```
