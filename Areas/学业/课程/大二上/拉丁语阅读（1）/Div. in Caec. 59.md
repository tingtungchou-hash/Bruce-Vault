# Original Text
quodsi ne iniuriae quidem, quae tibi ab illo facta sit, causa remanet, quid habes quod possis dicere quam ob rem non modo mihi, sed cuiquam anteponare? nisi forte illud, quod dicturum te esse audio, quaestorem illius fuisse. quae causa gravis esset, si certares mecum uter nostrum illi amicior esse deberet: in contentione suscipiendarum inimicitiarum ridiculum est putare causam necessitudinis ad inferendum periculum iustam videri oportere.

# Text Analysis
quodsi ne iniuriae quidem, quae tibi ab illo facta sit, causa remanet, quid habes quod possis dicere \[quam ob rem] non modo mihi, sed cuiquam anteponare? \[nisi forte] illud, quod dicturum te esse | audio, quaestorem illius fuisse. quae causa gravis esset, \[si ==certares== mecum] uter nostrum illi amicior esse deberet: in contentione suscipiendarum inimicitiarum ridiculum est (putare causam necessitudinis \[ad ==inferendum== periculum] ==iustam== videri oportere).

# Translation
if not even the reason of injury which was done to you by him remain, what do you have that you're able to say for what reason you're preferred not only to me, but to anyone? unless by chance ***(it is) that one point which*** I hear ~~what~~ you'll say, that you had been his quaestor. this reason is great, if you fight with me, ***for which*** one of us should be more friendly to that one: in the contention of undertaking inimicity it is rediculous to think that the reason of personal bond should seem ~~lawful~~ ***justified*** for the sake of bringing forth danger. 

而如果连所谓他侵害你的理由都不复存在，你还有什么可说，能说明为什么不仅该排在我前面，甚至该排在任何人前面？除非也许是你我听说你要说的那一点——你曾是他的财务官。这个理由本来会很有分量，如果是我们俩在争谁应当与他更亲近的话；但在争相承担敌意的较量中，认为亲近关系的理由就应当被视为足以正当化施加危险，这是荒谬的。

# Vocabulary

1. certo（动词）
	- 词形：*certō, certāre, certāvī, certātum*，第一变格法动词；本句 *certares* 即 *certārēs*，第二人称单数未完成时主动虚拟式（现在词干 *certā-* 加 *-rē-* 再加人称词尾 *-s*）
	- 词源：L&S 将其与 *cernō*“筛分、分辨、判定”系联（*certo, āvi, ātum, 1, cerno*），本义“以较量来判定”，故引申为“争斗、竞赛”；同族 *certāmen*“较量”、*certātim*“争先地”
	- 义：争斗、较量、竞争（to fight, struggle, contend, vie）；亦指言辞上的争辩、争执
	- 本句：*sī certārēs mēcum, uter nostrum illī amīcior esse dēbēret*——“如果你我是在争谁该与他更亲近”；与主句 *quae causa gravis esset* 同为未完成时虚拟式，构成反事实/潜在条件句
	- 提示：形式警示——勿与副词 *certō*“确定地、无疑”（源自 *certus*）相混，二者拼写全同；此处有 *-rē-* 为虚拟式标志，陈述式则应作 *certābās*
2. infero（动词）
	- 词形：*inferō, inferre, intulī, illātum*，*ferō* 的不规则复合动词；本句 *inferendum*，动形容词（gerundive）或动名词（gerund）的中性单数宾格
	- 词源：*in-*“向内、向……之上”加 *ferō*“携带、搬运”；完成时 *intulī*、目的分词 *illātum* 中的 *in-* 因后接 *l* 而同化为 *il-*
	- 义：带入、搬入；施加、加于（bring upon, inflict）。*inferre periculum alicui*“给某人带来危险”，*inferre bellum*“发动战争”，*inferre signa*“进攻”
	- 本句：*ad inferendum periculum*——“为了施加危险”。通常解作动名词，*inferendum* 支配宾语 *periculum*；若解作与 *periculum* 一致的动形容词（“为使危险被施加”），语义无别，二者皆表目的
	- 提示：完成时 *intulī* 不规则，勿写作 *inferāvī*；勿与 *īnferus*“下面的”、*īnferī*“下界亡灵”相混
3. iustus（形容词）
	- 词形：*iūstus, -a, -um*，第一/二变格法形容词；本句 *iustam* 即 *iūstam*，阴性单数宾格，与 *causam* 一致
	- 词源：*iūs, iūris* n.“法、权利”加形容词后缀 *-tus*，字面“合乎法的”
	- 义：公正的、合法的、正当的（just, lawful, rightful）；用于事物时指“有根据的、站得住脚的、正当有效的”，如 *causa iūsta*“正当理由”
	- 本句：*causam necessitūdinis ad inferendum periculum iūstam vidērī oportēre*——“认为亲近关系这一理由，竟可被视为足以正当化施加危险”；*iūstam* 为 *vidērī* 的表语，与 *causam* 保持性、数、格一致
	- 提示：L&S 以 *causa iūsta*（*Cic. Phil. 2, 22, 53*）为“正当理由”的书证；注意 *iūs* / *iūstus* 的 *ū* 为长音
