## what is a partitio? 
- a partitio should ==pinpoint the area of disagreement between opposing parties== and / or state the topics that the speaker intends to address ... many of Cicero's speeches do not contain a partitio proper (Watson 2025:139) 
- **divisio, partitio, confirmatio** 
	- divisio: division，分析哪些事情已经达成一致，哪些仍有争议
	- partitio: partition / statement of divisions，分题，划分并声明演讲将要处理的各点
	- confirmatio: supporting argumentation，正面提出论据以支持己方主张的部分
	- Cic. Inv. 1.31 una pars est, quae, quid cum adversariis conveniat et quid in controversia relinquatur, ostendit; 
	- Quint. Instit. Orat. 7.4.31 - 32 multa controversiarum genera in qualitatem cadunt 
	- Quint. Instit. Orat. 7.4.33 - 34: how Cicero forms his partitio ==gerundive + est + indirect questions==
