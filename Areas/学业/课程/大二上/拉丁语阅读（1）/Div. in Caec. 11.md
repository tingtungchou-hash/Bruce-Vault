# Original Text
in hac causa, iudices, tametsi utrumque esse arbitror perspicuum, tamen de utroque dicam, et de eo prius quod apud vos plurimum debet valere, hoc est de voluntate eorum quibus iniuriae factae sunt; quorum causa iudicium de pecuniis repetundis est constitutum. Siciliam provinciam C. Verres per triennium depopulatus esse, Siculorum civitates vastasse, domos exinanisse, fana spoliasse dicitur. adsunt, queruntur Siculi universi; ad meam fidem, quam habent spectatam iam et cognitam, confugiunt; auxilium sibi per me a vobis atque a populi Romani legibus petunt; me defensorem calamitatum suarum, me ultorem iniuriarum, me cognitorem iuris sui, me actorem causae totius esse voluerunt.

# Text Analysis
in hac causa, iudices, ==tametsi== utrumque esse arbitror perspicuum, tamen de utroque dicam, et de eo prius quod apud vos plurimum debet valere, hoc est de voluntate eorum quibus iniuriae factae sunt; quorum causa iudicium de pecuniis repetundis est constitutum. Siciliam provinciam C. Verres per triennium depopulatus esse, Siculorum civitates ==vastasse==, domos ==exinanisse==, fana spoliasse dicitur. adsunt, queruntur Siculi universi; ad meam fidem, quam habent spectatam iam et cognitam, confugiunt; auxilium sibi per me a vobis atque a populi Romani legibus petunt; me defensorem calamitatum suarum, me ultorem iniuriarum, me ==cognitorem== iuris sui, me actorem causae totius esse voluerunt.

# Translation
In this case, judges, though each side from my point of view is clear, still I say about each side and on what should thrive especially among you previously - that is, about the will of those to whom acts injustice have been inflicted, for the sake of whom the legal investigation on bribery has been set up. Gaius Verres is said to have plundered the province of Sicily for three years, to have ravaged the Sicilian cities, to have emptied households, to have robbed shrines. Present are the Sicilians - and all of them complain; towards my good faith they have now seen and known they seek shelter; from me and you and even the laws of the Roman People they seek help for themselves; they wish me to be the defender of their catastrophe, the revenger of injustice, the attorney of their rights, and the advocate of their case. 

# Vocabulary

1. tametsi（conj.，让步连词）
	- 词形：tametsi（亦作 tamen etsi、tamenetsi），为 tamen + etsi 的缩合
	- 词源：tamen（“然而”）＋ etsi（et + si，“即使”），字面“然而即使”；古典散文尤爱以 tametsi … tamen 作框式呼应
	- 义：although, though, notwithstanding that（“虽然、尽管”）
	- 本句：tametsi utrumque esse arbitror perspicuum, tamen de utroque dicam——“虽然我认为两边都已清楚，我仍要就两边都讲一讲”。tametsi 引让步从句（arbitror 用直陈式），主句由 tamen 接住
	- 警示：本课程第 8 课已就 tametsi … tamen 的呼应详述，可互参；tametsi 多接直陈式（接虚拟式极罕见），且常带 tamen，tamen 不可省

2. vastō, vastāre, vastāvī, vastātum 1（此处 vastasse，完成时不定式）
	- 词形：vastō, -āre, -āvī, -ātum 1；完成时不定式 vastasse ＝ vastāvisse
	- 词源：与 vastus（“空旷的、荒芜的”）同族，本义“使空旷、使无人烟”；派生现代意大利语 guastare、法语 gâter（“损坏”）
	- 义：(1) 本义 to make empty or vacant, desert（使空旷、荒废）；(2) 引申 to lay waste, desolate, ravage, devastate（使荒芜、蹂躏、洗劫）
	- 本句：Siculorum civitates vastasse——“（维勒斯）蹂躏了西西里的城邦”。与 domos exinanisse、fana spoliasse 三连，与前面的 depopulatus esse 同作 dicitur 所引不定式
	- 警示：L&S 指出 vastō 常与 populor、vexō 近义；此处 vastasse … exinanisse … spoliasse 构成递进排比（城邦—家宅—神庙），译时宜保住层次

3. exināniō, exinānīre, exinānīvī, exinānītum 4（此处 exinanisse，完成时不定式）
	- 词形：exināniō, -īre, -īvī（或 -iī）, -ītum 4；完成时不定式 exinanisse
	- 词源：ex-（“出、尽”）＋ inānis（“空的”），字面“把……掏空”；同族 inānis、inānia（“空话”）
	- 义：(1) to empty, make empty（使空、清空）；(2) 引申 to strip, despoil, drain（掏空、掠尽、耗尽）
	- 本句：domos exinanisse——“把家宅掏空”。L&S 即以此句为书证（Cic. Div. in Caecil. 4, 11），与 vastasse（城邦）、spoliasse（神庙）并列，谓维勒斯将民家洗劫一空
	- 警示：此处取“掠尽”之引申义，非字面“清空”；与同段 vastō 分工：vastō 重“使荒芜”，exināniō 重“掏空财物”

4. cognitōr, cognitōris m.（第三变格法；此处 cognitorem，宾格单数）
	- 词形：cognitōr, -ōris m.；动词 cognōscō, cognōscere, cognōvī, cognitum 3
	- 词源：由 cognōscō（“认识、查明”）派生，-tor 表施事者，本指“已熟谙案情之人”，遂为法律术语
	- 义：(1) 法律义 an advocate, attorney（诉讼代理人）——以当事人名义出庭、由当事人在庭前正式授予之人（cognitor est qui litem alterius suscipit coram ab eo cui datus est）；(2) 泛指 a defender, protector（维护者）；(3) 另有“证人、保证人”义（指证明其人身份者）
	- 本句：me cognitorem iuris sui——“（他们愿）我作其权利的代理人”。与前后的 me defensorem、me ultorem、me actorem 构成四个 me + 表语宾语的排比，层层递进
	- 警示：cognitōr 是严格的法律术语，须与 advocātus（“出庭声援者”，不主持诉讼）、prōcūrātor 区分；阿斯科尼乌斯（Asconius）正是就本篇（Div. in Caecil. 4, 11）注释此词，L&S 亦引本篇为例
