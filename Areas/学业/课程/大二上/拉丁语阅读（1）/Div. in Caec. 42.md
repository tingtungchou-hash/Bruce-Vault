# Original Text
iam nunc mente et cogitatione prospicio quae tum studia hominum, qui concursus futuri sint, quantam exspectationem magnitudo iudici sit adlatura, quantam auditorum multitudinem infamia C. Verris concitatura, quantam denique audientiam orationi meae improbitas illius factura sit. quae cum cogito, iam nunc timeo quidnam pro offensione hominum, qui illi inimici infensique sunt, et exspectatione omnium et magnitudine rerum dignum eloqui possim.

# Text Analysis
iam nunc mente et cogitatione prospicio *quae tum studia* hominum, *qui concursus* futuri sint, *quantam exspectationem* magnitudo iudici sit ==adlatura==, *quantam* auditorum *multitudinem* infamia C. Verris concitatura, *quantam* denique audientiam orationi meae *improbitas* illius factura sit. quae cum cogito, iam nunc **timeo** ==quidnam== \[pro offensione hominum], <= (qui illi inimici infensique sunt), et exspectatione omnium et magnitudine rerum dignum eloqui possim.

# Translation
now I see with mind and cognitive faculty what great passion of human beings, what tumults are going to happen, how much expectation the magnitude of judges is going to rage, what multitude of auditors the infame of C. Verres is going to incite, how much hearing the weckedness will make to my oration. since I know this, now I fear how in front of offence of people who are unfriendly and hostile to him, I am able to speak worthy of both of the expectation of all and the magnitude of the things

我现在就已凭心智和思虑预见：届时人们将有何等热情，将有何等样的人群聚集，案件的重大将带来多大的期待，盖乌斯·维勒斯的恶名将招来多么庞大的听众，最后，他的卑劣将给我的陈词带来多大的关注。每当我想到这些，我现在就已恐惧：面对那些与他为敌、对他满怀敌意的人们的憎恶，面对所有人的期待和事情的重大，我究竟能讲出什么与之相称的话来。

# Vocabulary

1. adferō, adferre, attulī, adlātum v. tr.
	- 词形：文中 adlātūra 是 adferō 的将来时主动分词（participium futūrī actīvī）阴性主格单数，由 supine 词干 adlāt- 加 -ūra 构成；在间接疑问句 quantam exspectātiōnem magnitūdō iūdicī sit adlātūra 中，与主语 magnitūdō（阴性单数）性、数、格一致，与 sit 构成将来迂说（periphrastic future），表“将要带来”
	- 词源：ad-（“向、朝”）+ ferō（“带、拿、运送”）；ferō 系列的完成时 attulī（不规则），supine adlātum（亦作 allātum，为 ad- 与 l- 的 dl/ll 同化变体）。同族 referō（带回）、cōnferō（集中、比较）、offerō（呈上、提供）、differō（分散；拖延）
	- 义：(1) 带来、携带到、运到（bring to, carry to）；(2) 招致、引起、造成（bring about, cause），如 exspectātiōnem adferre“带来期待”；(3) 提供、给予、施加（offer, impart）
	- 本句：quantam exspectātiōnem magnitūdō iūdicī sit adlātūra——“案件的重大将带来多大的期待”；sit adlātūra 为间接疑问中的将来迂说，相当于 futūrum sit ut magnitūdō exspectātiōnem adferat
	- 警示：adlātūra 是将来主动分词（“将要……”），勿与完成分词 adlāta（“已被带来／带过”）相混；sit adlātūra 整体作谓语，不能读作“是……的”。adlātum 与 allātum、adferō 与 afferō 仅是同化拼写之别，非两个词；阴性 -ūra 呼应 magnitūdō

2. quis, quae, quid pron. interr.
	- 词形：文中 quidnam ＝ quid（疑问代词 quis, quid 的中性单数宾格）加附加疑问小品词 -nam（enclitic，加强疑问语气）；整体为中性单数宾格，作 ēloquī 的宾语，受同格 dignum 修饰
	- 词源：quis, quae, quid 为疑问代词，与关系代词 quī, quae, quod 同源而功能有别；-nam 为疑问强调小品词，另见 quisnam、ubinam、quōnam
	- 义：究竟什么？到底是什么？（what, pray?）——带追问、惊讶或强调的“究竟／到底”
	- 本句：timeō quidnam … dignum … ēloquī possim——“我恐惧：……我究竟能讲出什么相称的话来”；timeō 后接间接疑问句（quidnam … possim，虚拟式），quidnam 作 ēloquī（“说出、表达”）的宾语
	- 警示：quidnam 是疑问代词，不是关系代词 quod；-nam 只加强语气、不改变意义。ēloquī 为异态动词（ēloquor, ēloquī, ēlocūtus sum），形被动而义主动；勿与副词 quam（“多么、比”）相混
