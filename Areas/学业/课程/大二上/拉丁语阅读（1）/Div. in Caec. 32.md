# Original Text
emptum est ex senatus consulto frumentum ab Siculis praetore Verre, pro quo frumento pecunia omnis soluta non est. grave est hoc crimen in Verrem, grave me agente, te accusante nullum; eras enim tu quaestor, pecuniam publicam tu tractabas, ex qua, etiamsi cuperet praetor, tamen ne qua deductio fieret magna ex parte tua potestas erat. huius quoque igitur criminis te accusante mentio nulla fiet: silebitur toto iudicio de maximis et notissimis illius furtis et iniuriis. mihi crede, Caecili, non potest in accusando socios vere defendere is qui cum reo criminum societate coniunctus est.

# Text Analysis
emptum est ex senatus consulto frumentum ab Siculis praetore Verre, pro quo frumento pecunia omnis ==soluta== non est. grave est hoc crimen in Verrem, grave me agente, te accusante nullum; eras enim tu quaestor, pecuniam publicam tu tractabas, ex qua, etiamsi cuperet praetor, tamen \[ne qua deductio fieret] \[magna ex parte] tua potestas erat. huius quoque igitur criminis te accusante mentio nulla fiet: ==silebitur== toto iudicio de maximis et notissimis illius furtis et iniuriis. mihi crede, Caecili, non potest in accusando socios vere defendere is qui cum reo criminum societate coniunctus est.

# Translation
Grains are taken from the Sicilians by Verres the praetor according to the Senatorial decree, in place of which money is not ~~released~~ ***paid***. this crime is heavy at disadvantage of Verres - heavy with me as the advocate, while nothing with you as the advocate; you were indeed the quaestor, you handled public money out of which even if the praetor desires, still it was your power that such theft away not happend. Therefore, you being the advocat, no mention of this crime is going to happen as well: also it is silenced to the entire court on the greatest and most notable thefts and injuries of that one. believe me, Caecilius, he who is connected to the accused through bonds of crime is not able to truly defend the allies in the accusation. 

根据元老院决议，总督维勒斯向西西里人购买了粮食，而这批粮食的全部价款并未付清。这项罪名由我起诉时，对维勒斯来说是严重的；由你起诉时，则完全不算数。因为当时你是财务官，掌管着公款；这笔钱，即便总督想作任何扣除，能否不作扣除，很大程度上也取决于你的权力。所以，若由你起诉，这一罪行同样不会被提及：在整个审判过程中，对他那些最重大、最臭名昭著的盗窃与不法行为，都将保持沉默。相信我吧，凯基利乌斯：一个与被告因共同犯罪而结为一体的人，在起诉时不可能真正为我们的盟邦辩护。

# Vocabulary
- soluta：solvō, solvere, solvī, solūtus 3.，“解开、松开；偿付、清偿”；此处为完成被动分词 solūtus, -a, -um 的阴性单数主格，与 pecunia 一致，构成完成被动式 pecunia omnis soluta non est，“全部款项并未付清”。注意 solūtus 亦可作形容词“松开的、无忧的”，此处取“偿付”义。
- silebitur：sileō, silēre, siluī 2.（无 supine），“沉默、不作声；对……保持沉默”；此处为第三人称单数将来时被动直陈式，作无人称被动，主语不出现；silebitur ... de furtis et iniuriis 意为“对……将保持沉默”。注意 sileō 属第二变位，将来时被动词尾为 -bitur，其被动一般只作无人称用法。
