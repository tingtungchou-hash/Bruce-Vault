# Original Text
de te, Caecili,--iam me hercule hoc extra hanc contentionem certamenque nostrum familiariter tecum loquar,--tu ipse quem ad modum existimes vide etiam atque etiam, et tu te collige, et qui sis et quid facere possis considera. Putasne te posse de maximis acerbissimisque rebus, cum causam sociorum fortunasque provinciae, ius populi Romani, gravitatem iudici legumque susceperis, tot res tam gravis, tam varias voce, memoria, consilio, ingenio sustinere? 

# Text Analysis
de te, Caecili,--iam ==mehercule== hoc extra hanc contentionem certamenque nostrum familiariter tecum loquar,--tu ipse quem ad modum existimes vide ==etiam atque etiam==, et tu te collige, et qui sis et quid facere possis considera. Putasne te posse de maximis acerbissimisque rebus, \[cum causam sociorum fortunasque provinciae, ius populi Romani, gravitatem iudici legumque susceperis], tot res tam gravis, tam ==varias== | voce, memoria, consilio, ingenio sustinere? 

# Translation
about you, Caecilius, - now, ~~~me being Hercles~~~ ***I swear by Hercules***, let me thus speak friendly with you beyond this contest and decisive competition of ours, - see yourself in what way you estimate ~~even and even~~, ***once and over again***, ~~with Verres being your college~~ ***collect your mind***, and do consider both who you are and what you're able to do. do you think that about the greatest and most harsh things, you're able to sustain so many heavy and diverse things with voice, memory, deliberation, and ability, when you undertake the case of the Sicilians, the fortunes of the province, the law of the Roman People, the gravity of court and law? 

至于你，凯基利乌斯——凭赫拉克勒斯起誓，我现在要撇开我们之间这场争夺与较量，以朋友的口吻同你说话——你自己要一遍又一遍地审视自己究竟是怎样想的，把心神收拢起来，认真考虑你是什么人、又能做什么。你以为，当你承担起同盟者的讼案、行省的命运、罗马人民的法权、法庭与法律的威严时，面对那些最重大、最惨痛的事情，你能凭嗓音、记忆、判断、才思，撑得住这么多、这么沉重、这么纷繁的事务吗？

# Vocabulary

1. mehercule（亦作 me hercule、mehercules）
	- 词形：固定叹语（interiectiō），不变格；由宾格代词 me + 神名 Hercules 构成，动词（如 iuvet“愿……助我”）省略
	- 词源：字面“愿海格立斯助我”（me Hercules iuvet），属以神名起誓的套语；与 edepol（凭波鲁克斯）、ecastor（凭卡斯托尔）同类。罗马习俗中男子多凭海格立斯起誓，女子多用 Castor
	- 义：起誓语，“凭海格立斯起誓”“说真的”“我敢说”；语气比 per deōs 轻，带口语色彩，常见于喜剧、书信与演说
	- 本句：iam mehercule hoc extra hanc contentiōnem certāmenque nostrum familiāriter tēcum loquar——“说真的，我现在要撇开我们之间这场争夺与较量，以朋友的口吻同你说话”。mehercule 为括号内的“私下进言”打上真诚的标记
	- 警示：me 是宾语，不能解作“我是海格立斯”；翻译时通常不必译出神名，可作“说真的”“凭良心讲”。此处是修辞姿态：familiariter loqui 之后接的仍是对凯基利乌斯的攻击

2. etiam atque etiam
	- 词形：副词性短语：etiam + atque + etiam，字面“又而且又”
	- 词源：etiam 由 et + iam 构成（“且已经”，引申“还、甚至”）；atque 为“而且、如同”的连接词。三词叠用构成强调
	- 义：(1) 一再、反复（again and again）；(2) 越来越（more and more）；(3) 恳切地、迫切地（earnestly）
	- 本句：vide etiam atque etiam——“一遍又一遍地审视”“再三掂量”。与下文的 collige、consīderā 连成一串命令式，语气逐步加强
	- 警示：勿与 atque etiam（“而且甚至”）相混，语序不同；此为固定搭配，不能逐词硬译

3. varius, -a, -um
	- 词形：第一/二变格法形容词；此处 variās 为阴性宾格复数，与 rēs（第五变格法，宾格复数 rēs）性数格一致；副词 variē，名词 varietās
	- 词源：词源不确定，或与 varix（“曲张的血管、蜿蜒”）相关，或与 vārus（“弯曲的、外翻的”）同源；核心义为“杂色的、斑驳的”，引申为“多样的、多变的”
	- 义：(1) 杂色的、斑驳的（of various colours, mottled）；(2) 多样的、形形色色的（varied, diverse）；(3) 多变的、不可靠的（changeable, fickle）
	- 本句：tot rēs tam gravīs, tam variās ... sustinēre——“撑得住这么多、这么沉重、这么纷繁的事务”。varius 与 gravis 并列修饰 rēs，分别从“单一/纷繁”与“轻/重”两个维度写案件的负担
	- 警示：varius 不等于英语 various 的简单对译；本义“杂色”使其含“种类混杂、头绪繁多”之意。此处 variās 是形容词，勿与动词 variō, -āre（“使变化”）相混
