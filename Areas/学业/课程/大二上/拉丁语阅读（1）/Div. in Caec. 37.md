# Original Text
de te, Caecili,--iam me hercule hoc extra hanc contentionem certamenque nostrum familiariter tecum loquar,--tu ipse quem ad modum existimes vide etiam atque etiam, et tu te collige, et qui sis et quid facere possis considera. Putasne te posse de maximis acerbissimisque rebus, cum causam sociorum fortunasque provinciae, ius populi Romani, gravitatem iudici legumque susceperis, tot res tam gravis, tam varias voce, memoria, consilio, ingenio sustinere?

# Translation
about you, Caecilius, - now, ~~~me being Hercles~~~ ***I swear by Hercules***, let me thus speak friendly with you beyond this contest and decisive competition of ours, - see yourself in what way you estimate even and even, and with Verres being your college, do consider both who you know and what you're able to do. do you think that about the greatest and most harsh things, you're able to sustain so many heavy and diverse things with voice, memory, deliberation, and ability, when you undertake the case of the Sicilians, the fortunes of the province, the law of the Roman People, the gravity of court and law? 

至于你，凯基利乌斯——凭海格立斯起誓，我现在要撇开我们之间这场争夺与较量，以朋友的口吻同你说话——你自己要一遍又一遍地审视自己究竟是怎样想的，把心神收拢起来，认真考虑你是什么人、又能做什么。你以为，当你承担起同盟者的讼案、行省的命运、罗马人民的法权、法庭与法律的威严时，面对那些最重大、最惨痛的事情，你能凭嗓音、记忆、判断、才思，撑得住这么多、这么沉重、这么纷繁的事务吗？
