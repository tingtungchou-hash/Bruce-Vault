# Original Text
deinde, ut opinor, habet alienum, hunc tamen a subselliis; qui quid in dicendo posset numquam satis attendi, in clamando quidem video eum esse bene robustum atque exercitatum. in hoc spes tuae sunt omnes; hic, si tu eris actor constitutus, totum iudicium sustinebit. ac ne is quidem tantum contendet in dicendo quantum potest, sed consulet laudi et existimationi tuae, et ex eo quod ipse potest in dicendo aliquantum remittet, ut tu tamen aliquid esse videare. Vt in actoribus Graecis fieri videmus, saepe illum qui est secundarum aut tertiarum partium, cum possit aliquanto clarius dicere quam ipse primarum, multum submittere, ut ille princeps quam maxime excellat, sic faciet alienus; tibi serviet, tibi lenocinabitur, minus aliquanto contendet quam potest. 

# Text Analysis
deinde, ut opinor, habet alienum, hunc tamen a ==subselliis==; qui quid in dicendo posset numquam satis attendi, in clamando quidem video eum esse bene robustum atque exercitatum. in hoc spes tuae sunt omnes; hic, si tu eris actor constitutus, totum iudicium sustinebit. ac ne is quidem tantum contendet in dicendo quantum potest, sed consulet laudi et existimationi tuae, et ex eo (quod ipse potest) in dicendo aliquantum remittet, ut tu tamen aliquid esse videare. Vt in actoribus Graecis fieri videmus, saepe illum qui est secundarum aut tertiarum partium, cum possit aliquanto clarius dicere quam ipse primarum, multum ==submittere==, ut ille princeps *quam* maxime ==excellat==, *sic* faciet alienus; tibi serviet, tibi lenocinabitur, minus aliquanto contendet quam potest. 

# Translation
then as I see, he has another one, one from the low benchers still; since I never paid enough attention to what he is able in speaking, I see that in clamouring indeed he is very robust and vexed. your hopes are all in this one; this one, if you will be chosen as the advocate, will undertake the entire court. and not even he will contend as mush as he can, but he will both take care of your praise and existimation and will send back in terms of speaking a little bit from him what he is able to do, so that you are seen as something instead. as we see to happen in the Greek advocates, often that who is among the second or third parts, ~~since~~ ***althoug*** he is able to speak more clearly to an extent, still ~~undertakes~~ ***reduces*** much so that the first man excels as much as another one makes him; he serves you, he act to the interest of you, he contends a little bit less than he actually can. 

就算如此，他本人不算什么，也做不了什么；但他带着一些训练有素、能言善辩的副控告人一同前来，是有所准备的。这毕竟还算一回事，尽管还不够；因为在任何事情上，在控告中居首位的人都应当是最完备、最有准备的。然而我看，卢基乌斯·阿普莱乌斯是居次的副控告人，此人论年纪并非新手，论法庭经验和历练却还是新手。接着，据我看来，他还有一个外人，不过此人是拍卖台那边的出身；他演说有多大能耐，我从未仔细留意过，至于叫嚷，我倒是看出他相当强壮、训练有素。你的全部希望就寄托在此人身上；如果你被指定为控告人，整个审判就要靠他来支撑。而且就连他，也不会在演说上使出全力，而会顾惜你的名声和声望，在力所能及之处稍稍收敛，好让你至少显得像个人物。正如我们在希腊演员身上常见的那样：常常那个演第二或第三角色的人，明明能比演主角的人说得更响亮，却大大收敛自己，好让那位主角尽量出彩；这个外人也会这样做：他会侍奉你，会奉承你、衬托你，会比他自己能做到的程度稍稍少用力一些。
