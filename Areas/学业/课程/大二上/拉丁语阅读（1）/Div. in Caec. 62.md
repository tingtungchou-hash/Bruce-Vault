# Original Text
quam ob rem si iure posses eum accusare, tamen, cum is tibi parentis numero fuisset, id pie facere non posses; cum vero neque iniuriam acceperis et praetori tuo periculum crees, fatearis necesse est te illi iniustum impiumque bellum inferre conari. etenim ista quaestura ad eam rem valet, ut elaborandum tibi in ratione reddenda sit quam ob rem qui quaestor eius fueris accuses, non ut ob eam ipsam causam postulandum sit ut tibi potissimum accusatio detur. neque fere umquam venit in contentionem de accusando qui quaestor fuisset, quin repudiaretur.

# Text Analysis
quam ob rem si iure posses eum accusare, tamen, cum is tibi parentis numero fuisset, id pie facere non posses; cum vero neque iniuriam acceperis et praetori tuo periculum ==crees==, fatearis necesse est te illi iniustum impiumque bellum inferre conari. etenim ista quaestura ad eam rem valet, ut elaborandum tibi in ratione reddenda sit quam ob rem qui quaestor eius fueris accuses, non ut ob eam ipsam causam postulandum sit ut tibi potissimum accusatio detur. neque fere umquam venit in contentionem de accusando qui quaestor fuisset, quin ==repudiaretur==.

# Translation
for this reason even if you're able to accuse him lawfully, still, given that he had been to you like a parent, you're not able to conduct it piously. since indeed you had received no injury and created danger for your praetor, you have to confess it is necessary that you strive to inflict an unjustified as well as impious war to him. and indeed that quaestorship is strong to this thing, that you should work hard to give back a reason for which you accuse the one who'd been your quaestor, not that it should be demanded for this case itself that the accusation be given to you especially. nor approximately ever has ~~he~~ ***one who'd been the quaestor*** gone into connection about accusing ~~who'd been the quaestor~~, ~~which was refused~~ ***without being refused***. 

因此，即便你有权控告他，然而既然他曾以父辈的身份待你，你这样做便绝谈不上虔敬；而既然你并未受到任何伤害，反倒给自己的行政长官招致危险，你就必须承认，你是在试图对他发动一场不义且不敬的战争。事实上，你这段财务官经历所起的作用，是让你在陈述理由时必须竭力说明：你既是他的财务官，为何还要控告他；而并非让你仅凭这一条就有资格要求把控告权优先授予你。况且，凡是曾做过某人财务官的人，一旦卷入控告权的争夺，几乎从来都被拒绝。

# Vocabulary

1. creo（动词）
	- 词形：*creō, creāre, creāvī, creātum*，第一变格法动词；本句 *crees* 即 *creēs*，第二人称单数现在时主动虚拟式
	- 词源：古体作 *cereō*（瓦罗《论拉丁语》）；L&S 将其与梵语 *kar-, kri-*“做、造”系联
	- 义：(1) 产生、创造、生育；(2) 制造、造成、引起（*perīculum creāre* 带来危险）；(3) 政治术语：选举、任命（*cōnsulēs creāre* 选举执政官）
	- 本句：*cum ... praetōrī tuō perīculum creēs*——“既然你在给你的长官招致危险”；*creēs* 处于 *cum* 从句中，虚拟式表示原因或情境
	- 提示：形式警示——*creēs*（虚拟式，*ē* 长）勿与 *creās*（陈述式，*ā* 长）相混；*creāre* 的“创造”义偏“使产生、造就”，政治语境中则指“选任”
2. repudio（动词）
	- 词形：*repudiō, repudiāre, repudiāvī, repudiātum*，第一变格法动词；本句 *repudiaretur* 即 *repudiārētur*，第三人称单数未完成时被动虚拟式
	- 词源：由 *repudium*“休妻、解除婚约；拒绝”转来
	- 义：休弃、离婚；拒绝接受（遗产、条件等）；泛指拒绝、摒弃、唾弃（reject, refuse, spurn）
	- 本句：*neque ferē umquam vēnit in contentiōnem dē accūsandō quī quaestor fuisset, quīn repudiārētur*——“几乎从来没有做过财务官的人卷入控告权之争而不被拒绝的”；*quīn* 引出否定阻碍/结果从句，*repudiārētur* 为被动“被拒绝”
	- 提示：*quīn* 从句一律用虚拟式，此处未完成时表示过去反复发生的情形；参见 [[Div. in Caec. 61#Vocabulary|第 61 课词汇]] 中 *repudiō* 条
