# Original Text
si tibi, Q. Caecili, hoc Siculi dicerent: 'te non novimus, nescimus qui sis, numquam te antea vidimus; sine nos per eum nostras fortunas defendere cuius fides est nobis cognita,' nonne id dicerent quod cuivis probare deberent? nunc hoc dicunt, utrumque se nosse; alterum se cupere defensorem esse fortunarum suarum, alterum plane nolle. 

# Text Analysis
si tibi, Q. Caecili, hoc Siculi dicerent: 'te non novimus, nescimus qui sis, numquam te antea vidimus; ==sine== nos per eum nostras fortunas defendere cuius fides est nobis cognita,' nonne id dicerent quod ==cuivis== probare deberent? nunc hoc dicunt, utrumque se nosse; alterum se cupere defensorem esse fortunarum suarum, alterum plane nolle.

# Translation
if to you, Q. Caecili, the Sicilians said this: "we wanted not you, we didn't know who you are, we have never seen you before; let us defend our fortunes through the one of whose bond is recognised by us," wouldn't they say what they should prove to anyone? they now say this, that they had not known neither side, but that they desire the other to be the defender of their own fortune, while openly do not wish the other to be. 

# Vocabulary

1. sine（sinō 的现在时命令式第二人称单数）
	- 词形：sinō, sinere, sīvī, situm 3（主动）；此处 sine ＝ 现在时主动命令式，第二人称单数（复数作 sinite）
	- 词源：L&S 谓其词源不明（etym. dub.），本义“让、置、放”，此义仅存于过去分词 situs 及复合词 pōnō（＝po-sinō）；其余皆转义“容许、听凭”
	- 义：(1) to let, suffer, allow, permit, give leave（让、容许、听凭）；常接宾语从句、虚拟式，或独立使用，偶接 ut 或“宾格＋不定式”；(2) 命令式 sine（复数 sinite）＋虚拟式／宾格＋不定式 ＝“让……吧”。同义 permittō, patior, tolerō, ferō
	- 本句：sine nōs per eum nostrās fortūnās dēfendere——“让我们借他之手来维护我们的身家吧”。sine 为命令式，支配宾格 nōs ＋ 不定式 dēfendere（accūsātīvus cum īnfīnītīvō）
	- 警示：命令式 sine 与介词 sine（“无”，接夺格，如 sine dubiō）同形异词，须凭搭配分辨——此处后随宾格 nōs＋不定式，故必为命令式；亦勿误作 sī＋-ne 的疑问小词
	- 关联：同义 permittō, patior, ferō, tolerō；命令式单数 sine／复数 sinite；完成时 sīvī、过去分词 situs（与名词 situs“位置”同形异源）

2. cuivis（quīvīs 的与格单数）
	- 词形：quīvīs, quaevīs, quidvīs（＝quodvīs），不定代词；夺格作 quīvīs（Ter. Ad. 2, 3, 1）；此处 cuivis ＝ 与格单数
	- 词源：quī（关系／疑问词干）＋ -vīs（＝ vel sīs／velīs“你愿意”，强调后附成分），本义“你乐意挑谁（哪个）都行”
	- 义：who or what you please, any whatever, any one, anything（随便哪个、任何人／任何物都行）——语气强于 quisquam、ullus，强调“无一例外”
	- 本句：nonne id dicerent quod cuivis probare deberent——“难道他们说的不正是任何一个人都该能被说服的道理吗”。cuivis 为 probāre 的与格间接宾语（probāre aliquid alicui“向某人证明某事”），义“向随便任何人（都成立）”
	- 警示：cuivis 为与格单数，勿与同族 quīlibet, quālibet, quodlibet（亦表“随便哪个”）相混；亦须辨形近的 quamvīs（副词“尽管、无论多么”）与 quōvīs（“往任何方向”）
	- 关联：同族 quīlibet, quālibet, quodlibet；近义 quisque, quisquam, ullus, ūnusquisque；强调后缀 -vīs 亦见 quamvīs, quōvīs
