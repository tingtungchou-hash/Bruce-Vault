# Original Text
huic ego homini iam ante denuntio, si a me causam hanc vos agi volueritis, rationem illi defendendi totam esse mutandam, et ita mutandam ut, meliore et honestiore condicione quam qua ipse vult uti, imitetur homines eos quos ipse vidit amplissimos, L. Crassum et M. Antonium, qui nihil se arbitrabantur ad iudicia causasque amicorum praeter fidem et ingenium adferre oportere. nihil erit quod me agente arbitretur iudicium sine magno multorum periculo posse corrumpi.

# Text Analysis
huic ego homini iam ante **denuntio**, \[si a me causam hanc vos agi volueritis], rationem illi defendendi totam esse mutandam, et ita mutandam ut, \[meliore et honestiore ==condicione== quam qua ipse vult uti], ==imitetur== homines eos quos ipse vidit amplissimos, L. Crassum et M. Antonium, qui nihil se arbitrabantur ad iudicia causasque amicorum praeter fidem et ingenium adferre oportere. nihil erit quod me agente arbitretur iudicium sine magno multorum periculo posse corrumpi.

# Translation
to this man I remind before, that if you had been willing ~~to take this case from me~~ ***that this case is conducted by me***, the reason of his defending that should be totally changed, and changed in such a way that, better and with more respected ~~agreement~~ ***condition*** than how he himself wishes to use, he imitates those people whom he saw as the greatest, Lucius Crassus and Marcus Antonius, who think it is proper that they carry forward nothing other than faith and talent to courts and cases of friends. it will not happen that one thinks the court to be able to be corrupted without great peril of many with me being the advocate. 

我现在事先向此人声明：如果你们愿意让我来承办此案，那么他整套辩护方案都必须彻底改变，而且要改成这样：以一种比他本人所愿采用的更好、更体面的条件，去效仿他亲眼见过的最显赫的人物，即卢基乌斯·克拉苏斯和马尔库斯·安东尼乌斯；这两人认为，在法庭审判中、在为朋友办理案件时，除了诚信和才干，不应带进任何别的东西。这样，他就没有任何理由认为，在我承办此案时，可以收买法庭而不使许多人面临巨大危险。

# Vocabulary

- condicio, condicionis, f. — 条件、地位、处境。本句为 ablative singular `condicione`，与 `meliore et honestiore` 连用：“以更好、更体面的条件/地位”。
- imitor, imitari, imitatus sum — 模仿、效仿。本句 `imitetur` 为第三人称单数现在时虚拟式（deponent），在 `ita ... ut` 从句中表示“他应当效仿”。

# Grammar

## nihil est quod 句式

- nihil est quod... = 没有理由……
- quid est quod...? = 为什么……？/ 有什么理由……？
- nihil est cur... = 没有理由……
- est quod... = 有理由……
- nihil habeo quod... = 我没有理由……

例：

- Nihil est quod timeas. = 你没有什么可害怕的。
- Quid est quod me accusas? = 你为什么指责我？
- Nihil est quod putem te mentiri. = 我没有理由认为你在撒谎。
