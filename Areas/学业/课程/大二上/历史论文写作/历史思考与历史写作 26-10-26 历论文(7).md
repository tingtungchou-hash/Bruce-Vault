# 历史思考与历史写作
- 历史的结果还是写作，写作不仅是把思考表达出来，而且还是让自己知道自己思考了什么。写作显示对过去的事实知道什么，对这些事实之间的关系产生了疑问，提出自己的问题，并尝试回答
- 写作显示对已有历史知识之间的重组，这种重组是为了回答提出的问题。所有的重组都应该反映新的思考和疑惑
## 历史地思考的方法
- establish historical significance 
- use primary source evidence 
- identify continuity and change 
- analyze cause and consequence 
- take historical perspectives 
- understand the ethical dimensions of historical interpretations 
## 在阅读中思考过去
- 史料告诉我们什么（5W1H）
- 史料没有告诉我们什么（reading between lines）
- 史料是谁写的、是为谁写的
- 我有什么疑惑、怀疑和问题
## 历史论文写作十诫
- THOU SHALT begin with an outline that builds the entire paper around central ideas 
	- have a thesis and subtopics 
		- categorise information 
		- ask interpretive questions 
		- facts always support the main idea 
- THOU SHALT avoid self-conscious discussion 
	- avoid use of first person 
		- I will demonstrate 
		- now let us turn to 
		- now we see 
		- I think that 
- THOU MAY covet other writers' ideas but THOU SHALT NOT steal them 
- THOU SHAL strive for clarity above cuteness 
- REMEMBER thy paragraph and develop a main idea 
- THOU SHALT use quotations sparingly and judiciously 
- THOU SHALT not relegate essential information to thy footnotes
