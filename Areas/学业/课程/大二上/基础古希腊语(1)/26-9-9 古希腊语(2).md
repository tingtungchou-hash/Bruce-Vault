# Accentuation 
## accent marks 
- The Attic Greek has a **tonal accent / pitch accent**. But around the 5th century AD, tonal accent gradually turned into stress accent. 
- The practive of marking accents was initiated by literary scholars in **Alexandria** around **200 BC**, and the marking system we use today was first devised in **9th-century Byzantium** when a whole-scale transliterary process from mascule letters to miniscule letters took place. 
## division of syllables 
Rules: 
- There's one vowel per syllable
	- ἀκηκόαμεν => ἀ κη κό α μεν
- A single consonant by itself (not a cluster) goes with the following vowel 
	- ἐωράκαμεν => ἐ ω ρά κα μεν
- Two consecutive vowels that do not form a diphthong are divided 
	- ἐθεασάμεθα => ἐ θε α σά με θα
	- Ἠσαΐας => Ἠ σα ΐ ας
- A consonant cluster that cannot be *pronounced* is divided, and the first consonant goes with the preceding vowel; while a consonant cluster that can be pronounced goes with the following vowel   
	- ἀρχῆς => ἀρ χῆς  
	- ἔμπροσθεν => ἔμ προ σθεν
	- Χριστός => Χρι στός 
	- γραφή => γρα φή
## Contonation and Mora
- mora is the standard length value of a sound 
- contonation is the combination of the rise of pitch and the necessary return of it 
	- ==There can be only one mora at most after the contonation==
		- ἀνγέλου: the contonation is έ↑λου↓, and because the ending ου takes more than 1 morae, it is impossible for the acute to be kept on the antepenult, thus is is moved to penult 
		- ἄνγελος: the contonation is ἄν↑γε↓, and the ending ος takes up only 1 mora 
		- οἶκος: the contonation is οἶ↑↓, and the ending ος takes up only 1 mora

## Ultima, Penult, and Antepenult 
ἄν - θρω - πος 
Antepenult - Penult - Ultima
According to the meaning of the marks and the rules as explained in [[26-9-9 古希腊语(2)#Contonation and Mora|Contonation and Mora]]: 
- acute can appear on U, P, and A
- grave can appear only on U 
	- (because grave is derived from acute when written with other words)
- circumflex can appear on U, P, but not A 
	- (if the circumflex appears on A, there'd be at least 2 morae after the contonation)
