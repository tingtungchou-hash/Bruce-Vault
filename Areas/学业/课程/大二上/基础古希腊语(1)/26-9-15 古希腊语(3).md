# Parts of Speech 
Catalogories of words according to their function in the sentence (nouns, verbs, pronouns, articles, adverbs, prepositions, conjunctions, etc.) 
## Noun (ὄνομα/nomen) 
- proper noun 
- grammatical gender: in Greek, masculine, feminine, neuter
- inflection of nouns: **declension** 
- number: in Greek, singular, dual, plural 
- case: indicates the grammatical function of the noun (nominative, genitive, dative, accusative, vocative) 
	- ὁ ἄνθρωπος ἄρτον ἐσθίει 
	- ἄρτον ὁ ἄνθρωπος ἐσθίει (√) 
	- ὁ ἄρτος ἄνθρωπον ἐσθίει (×) 
## Preposition (πρόθεσις/praepositio) 
- expresses the relation between a noun or pronoun on the one hand and another word on the other hand 
- prepositions are accompanied by the oblique cases: cases except the nominative (gen. dat. acc.)
