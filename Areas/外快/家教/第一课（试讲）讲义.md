本课语料分两块。

精读部分取自 [[书虫·牛津英汉双语读物(第3级全)]] 中的《铁路少年》（The Railway Children）第一章 The beginning of things，切成两段。原文与译文均照抄，一字未改。本章后半段（The New House，搬家与抵达乡下的房子）留作下一课的精读材料，本课不读，正好给他留一个悬念。

应试部分是一篇原创短文，仿中考阅读 A 篇体裁，为的是把两段精读里的核心词块在考试文体里再滚一遍。凡是精读里教过的重点词，这篇里都有它的位置。

## 一、聊天：了解你的偏好（15 分钟）

开头先说清楚：今天不打分、不批评、不留作业本，就是一起读一个故事，顺便让我知道从哪里开始教你最省力。

聊天问题，按顺序问，可以顺着聊开：

1. 你以前上过哪些英语课？有没有哪个老师你比较喜欢？为什么？
2. 有没有哪个老师你不太喜欢？是哪里让你不舒服？
3. 你更喜欢哪种上课方式：老师多讲一点、你多做一点题、还是边聊边学？
4. 一节课上多久你会开始觉得累？
5. 平时喜欢看什么？电影、动漫、打球、游戏、小说，都行。
6. 故事的话，你喜欢哪一类：搞笑日常、悬疑、冒险、科幻、还是真人真事？
7. 你觉得英语最难的是哪一块：单词、语法、阅读，还是背课文？
8. 如果英语课能改一样东西，你最想改什么？

教师提示：这一节有两个目的，一是建立关系，二是探题材偏好，为后面的选材做准备。他说的每一句都记下来。同时观察他回答的长短、有没有抬头、会不会主动补充，这些进课后报告。今天的故事是家庭变故加悬念路线，如果他说更喜欢别的类型，记下来，下一课就换。

记录位：

- 以前上过的课与感受：
- 偏好的授课方式：
- 兴趣与题材偏好：
- 自认最难的板块：
- 其他：

## 二、热身闯关（13 分钟）

教师提示：提前说明这是两关小游戏，不是考试。答错不扣分，答对记一分。

### 第一关 单词雷达（8 分钟）

教师提示：我念英文，他说中文意思。说不出的直接跳过，不追问、不提示。三层分别记数。

第一组

because / before / different / favorite / important / usually / together / answer / question / weekend

第二组

subject / habit / healthy / exercise / interest / decide / describe / prefer / borrow / arrive

第三组

suggest / mention / realize / improve / achieve / receive / discuss / celebrate / introduce / develop

得分：第一组 ___ / 10　第二组 ___ / 10　第三组 ___ / 10

教师提示：判读标准。第一组错 3 个以上，说明小学基础词有硬缺口；第二组错 5 个以上，说明课内词没跟上；第三组全错属正常，能认出一半说明上限不低。

### 第二关 开口关（5 分钟）

教师提示：让他逐个朗读，不看音标。看的是过程不是对错：会不会按音节拆词（beau-ti-ful）、遇到长词是不是直接说不会、会不会把学过的规则迁移到生词上。

beautiful / comfortable / necessary / interesting / probably / different / favorite / restaurant / birthday / Wednesday

记录：能拆词 ___ / 10　直接放弃 ___ / 10

教师提示：若基本不会拆词，说明他背单词一直靠整体记忆，这是词汇量上不去的根因，也是后续课程的第一个抓手。这一点务必写进课后报告。

## 三、精读一 The Beginning of Things（24 分钟）

教师提示：开读之前只说一句：这是一家五口，住在伦敦，有三个孩子。不要剧透后面发生了什么——这一段结尾停在“敲门”，是留给他的钩子。

教师提示：每段精读走同样五步。第三步只讲他勾出来的词，没勾的一律不讲。这既省时间，也让他知道勾词是有用的。

### 3.1 跟读（4 分钟）

教师提示：我读一句，他跟读一句。不做任何讲解，先让耳朵和嘴过一遍。

The beginning of things

They were not railway children at the beginning. They lived with their father and mother in London. There were three of them. Roberta—she was always called Bobbie, and was the oldest. Next came Peter, who wanted to be an engineer when he grew up. And the youngest was Phyllis, who was always trying to be good.

Mother was almost always at home, ready to play with the children, or to read to them. And she wrote stories, then read them to the children after tea.

These three lucky children had everything that they needed. Pretty clothes, a warm house, and lots of toys. They also had a wonderful father who was never angry, and always ready to play a game.

They were very happy. But they did not know how happy until their life in London was over, and they had to live a very different life indeed.

The awful change came suddenly.

*　　*　　*

It was Peter's birthday, and he was ten years old. Among his presents was a toy steam engine, and it quickly became Peter's favourite toy. But after three days, the engine went BANG! Peter was very unhappy about his broken toy. The others said he cried, but Peter said his eyes were red because he had a cold.

When Father came home that day, Peter told him the sad story about his engine, and Father looked at it very carefully. Mother and the children waited.

'Is there no hope?' said Peter.

'Of course there's hope!' said Father, smiling. 'I'll mend it on Saturday, and you can all help me.'

Just then, someone knocked at the front door. A few moments later, Ruth—the maid—came in. 'There are two gentlemen to see you,' she said to Father.

'Now, who can they be?' said Father.

### 3.2 粗读勾词（4 分钟）

教师提示：让他自己读一遍，用笔勾出不认识或影响理解的词。不要提示，不要替他判断哪个词重要。

### 3.3 单词讲解（7 分钟）

教师提示：只讲他勾出来的。下面这些是预估会被勾的，按需取用。

- at the beginning 起初
- grow up 长大
- be ready to do sth. 乐意做某事
- play a game 做游戏
- come suddenly 来得突然
- go BANG 砰的一声响
- be unhappy about 为……不高兴
- have a cold 感冒
- look at sth. carefully 仔细查看
- Is there no hope? 没希望了吗？
- mend 修好
- knock at the front door 敲前门
- come in 进来
- a few moments later 过了一会儿

### 3.4 自主串文义（5 分钟）

教师提示：让他用自己的话把这段讲一遍，中文就行。讲不顺的地方不要直接给答案，说“我们一起回去看那句”，带他回原文找。这一步是这节课最重要的训练。

### 3.5 阅读题（4 分钟）

1. How many children were there in the family, and who was the oldest?
2. What happened to Peter's toy engine?
3. 猜词：文中 awful 最可能的意思是（　）A. 美好的　B. 糟糕的　C. 突然的
4. 预测：有人敲门，来了两位先生。你觉得接下来会发生什么？

答题记录：1 ___　2 ___　3 ___　4 ___

教师提示：参考译文。起初，他们并不是铁路少年。他们和父母一起住在伦敦。他们兄弟姐妹三个，分别是：罗伯塔——大家常叫她博比，年龄最大。彼得排行老二，他长大后想当工程师。老三叫菲利斯，她一向很乖巧。妈妈几乎一直待在家里，或者陪孩子们玩，或者读故事给他们听。她还自己编写故事，在茶后读给孩子们听。这三个幸运的孩子拥有他们想要的一切。漂亮的衣服，温暖的家，还有好多好多的玩具。他们还有一个难得的好爸爸，他从不发脾气，而且经常陪孩子们做游戏。他们生活得非常幸福。但直到他们在伦敦的生活结束以后，他们才真正明白了那段生活有多么 美好，以后他们不得不过一种截然不同的生活。这个糟糕的变化降临得十分突然。那天是彼得的生日，他已经10岁了。在他收到的生日礼物中，有一个玩具蒸汽火车头，这个火车头很快就成了他最喜欢的玩具。但过了三天，这个火车头突然“砰”地一声坏掉了！彼得为此闷闷不乐。家里的其他人都说彼得哭了，可彼得声称他的眼睛变红是因为他感冒了。那天，爸爸回来的时候，彼得把这个不幸的消息告诉了他。爸爸仔细地检查了那个火车头。妈妈和孩子们等在旁边。“没希望修好了吗？”彼得问。“当然有希望！”爸爸微笑着说，“星期六我给你修好，你们都可以给我帮帮忙。”就在这个时候，有人敲他家的前门。几分钟之后，鲁思——家里的女佣——走了进来。“有两位先生想要见您。”她对爸爸说。“现在这个时候，会是谁呀？”爸爸说。

教师提示：第 4 题没有标准答案，让他大胆猜。猜什么都行，重点是让他对下一段产生期待。猜完不要揭晓。

## 四、精读二 Bad News（24 分钟）

### 4.0 滚动复习（1 分钟）

教师提示：我说中文，他说英文。只考上一篇的词块：起初 / 长大 / 做游戏 / 感冒 / 修好 / 敲门。

教师提示：先让他猜一句“那两位先生是谁”，再接下文，把上一段的钩子收掉。

### 4.1 跟读（4 分钟）

'Try to be quick, dear,' said his wife. 'It's nearly time for the children to go to bed.'

But the two men stayed and stayed. Father's voice got louder and louder in the next room, but the children and Mother could not hear what was said. Then Ruth came back and spoke to Mother.

'He wants you to go in, ma'am,' she said. 'I think he's had bad news. Be ready for the worst.'

Mother went into the next room, and there was more talking. Soon after, the children heard Ruth call a taxi, then there was the sound of feet going outside and down the steps.

Mother came back, and her face was white.

'It's time to go to bed,' she said to the children. 'Ruth will take you upstairs.'

'But, Father—' began Phyllis.

'Father's had to go away on business,' said Mother. 'Now, go to bed, darlings.'

Bobbie whispered, 'It wasn't bad news, was it?'

'No, darling,' said Mother. 'I can't tell you anything tonight. Please go now .'

*　　*　　*

Mother went out early the next morning, and it was nearly seven o'clock before she came home. She looked ill and tired, and the children asked her no questions.

Mother drank a cup of tea, then she said, 'Now, my darlings, I want to tell you something. Those men did bring bad news last night. Father will be away for some time, and I'm very worried.'

'Is it something to do with the Government?' asked Bobbie. The children knew that Father worked in a Government office.

'Yes,' said Mother. 'Now don't ask me any more questions about it. Will you promise me that?.'

The children promised.

Everything was horrible for some weeks. Mother was nearly always out. Ruth, the maid, went away. Then Mother went to bed for two days, and the children wondered if the world was coming to an end.

### 4.5 阅读题（4 分钟）

1. Why did Mother's face become white?
2. What did Mother tell the children about Father?
3. Why did Mother ask the children to promise not to ask more questions?
4. 猜词：文中 whispered 的意思是（　）A. 大声喊　B. 低声说　C. 哭

答题记录：1 ___　2 ___　3 ___　4 ___

教师提示：参考译文。“尽量快点儿，亲爱的，”他妻子说，“孩子们就该上床睡觉了。”但是，那两个人待了很久。隔壁房间里，爸爸讲话的声音越来越高，不过妈妈和孩子们还是听不清他在说些什么。后来，鲁思回来跟妈妈说话。“夫人，他想让您过去一下，”鲁思说，“我想可能是不好的消息。做好最坏的准备吧。”妈妈进了隔壁那个房间，又是一番交谈。过了一会儿，孩子们听到鲁思叫了一辆出租车，随后传来了有人下楼出门的脚步声。妈妈回来了，面无血色。“该上床睡觉了，”她对孩子们说，“鲁思会带你们上楼的。”“但是，爸爸——”菲利斯首先说。“爸爸要出差，”妈妈说，“现在，上床睡觉吧，亲爱的。”博比小声说：“该不是什么坏消息吧，是吗？”“不是，亲爱的，”妈妈说，“今晚我什么也不能告诉你们，请现在 就去睡觉吧。”第二天一早，妈妈就出门了，直到将近晚上7点才回来。她看上去很不舒服，很疲惫，孩子们什么都没敢问。妈妈喝了一杯茶，随后她说：“我亲爱的孩子们，现在我想告诉你们一些事情。昨晚那两个人的确带来了坏消息。爸爸要离开我们一段日子，我很担心。”“是跟政府有关的事情吗？”博比问。因为孩子们知道，爸爸在一个政府部门工作。“是的，”妈妈说，“别再问这件事了，你们能答应我吗？”孩子们都答应了。随后的几个星期里，情况变得一团糟。妈妈几乎不着家。鲁思，就是家里的女佣，也走了。然后，妈妈有两天卧床不起，孩子担心是不是世界末日就要来了。

教师提示：第 3 题是推断题，答案指向妈妈在保护孩子——她知道的事比说出来的多。他能说出“不想让孩子担心”就够。

教师提示：这一段读完，故事停在“世界末日就要来了”，正好收住。本章剩下的部分（妈妈宣布搬家、火车、乡下的房子）留到下一课，不要提前讲。

## 五、应试文章 A New Start（22 分钟）

教师提示：这一节是考试模拟，不是精读，流程和前面反过来：先限时做题，再讲。目的是让他体验一次真实的考试节奏，同时把前两段学的词在考试文体里再碰一遍。

教师提示：发下去之前不要说“这是考试”，说“我们做一篇短的，十分钟，看看你能对几个”。计时十分钟，中途不提示、不查词、不查手机。他卡住了就让他猜——考试的时候也只能猜。

### 5.1 限时阅读（10 分钟）

A New Start

At the beginning, Kate's family lived in a big house in London. Her father worked in a company in the city. Every morning he got on the train at seven, and every evening he came home at eight. On weekends, the family played games together, or went to the park. Kate was twelve. She wanted to be a teacher when she grew up, and she thought her life would never change.

Then, one evening in October, two men came to their house. They stayed for two hours and talked with Kate's parents. Kate and her brother could hear their father's voice in the next room, but they could not hear the words.

Later, Kate's mother came upstairs. Her face was white, but she tried to smile. 'Your father has had some bad news,' she said. 'He has to go away on business. Don't worry. Go to sleep now, darlings.'

But Kate was worried. Two weeks later, her mother told them the truth. The company was in trouble, and the family could not keep the big house. Their old life came to an end. They would move to a small house in the country, near a railway line.

Kate did not want to go. She did not want to leave her school or her friends. That night, she could not sleep.

On the last morning, the family stood in the empty house. The lights went out one by one. Kate's brother began to cry. Kate looked at her mother — tired, but still trying to smile — and promised to help.

Then they got on the train. Kate looked out of the window and watched her old life go past.

### 5.2 讲题（8 分钟）

1. How did Kate's father go to work every morning?
A. By car.　B. By bus.　C. By train.　D. On foot.

2. What did Kate's mother tell Kate that evening?
A. That the family would move to the country.
B. That Kate's brother was ill.
C. That they would buy a new house.
D. That her father had to go away on business.

3. Why did Kate's mother say "Don't worry"?
A. Because she did not want the children to be afraid.
B. Because there was really nothing wrong.
C. Because the family had a lot of money.
D. Because Kate's father would come home soon.

4. 猜词：文中 empty 最可能的意思是（　）A. 空的　B. 温暖的　C. 拥挤的　D. 明亮的

5. What is the best title for this passage?
A. Kate's School Life　B. A Change in Kate's Family　C. A Trip by Train　D. Kate's Best Friend

答案与解析

1. C　细节。第一段 Every morning he got on the train at seven。
2. D　细节。第三段 He has to go away on business。注意 A 是两周以后妈妈才说出的事实，不是那天晚上说的，这是干扰项。
3. A　推理。妈妈说“别担心”的时候，真话还没有说出口。B、C 与后文事实相反，D 文中没有依据。
4. A　猜词。下文 The lights went out one by one 和 Kate's brother began to cry 说明房子已经空了、人就要走了。
5. B　主旨。文章写的是一个家庭从伦敦的大房子搬到乡下的全过程。A、C、D 都只是文中的一个细节。

教师提示：第 2 题是这篇里最值得讲的一题。A 和 D 都“在文中出现过”，区别只在时间。中考阅读的干扰项最常用这个套路——把后文的信息挪到前文的位置。这一题讲透，比多做十道题有用。
