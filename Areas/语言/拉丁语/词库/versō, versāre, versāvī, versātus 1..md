# versō, versāre, versāvī, versātus 1.

## 基本信息
- **语言**: 拉丁语
- **词性**: v. trans. (第一变位法，vertō 的反复动词)
- **词源**: frequentative of *vertō* ("to turn often or violently")

## 释义
1. Lit.: to turn, wind, twist, or whirl about often or violently
   a. *Sīsyphus versat saxum* = Sisyphus turns the stone
   b. *terram versāre* = to turn the soil, hoe
   c. *turdōs in ignī versāre* = to turn thrushes on the fire
2. Trop.:
   a. To turn, twist, bend: *versāre suam nātūram* = to twist one's nature
   b. To disturb, vex, agitate, toss about
      - *fortūna utrumque versāvit* = fortune tossed both of them this way and that (BG V.44.14)
   c. To turn over in the mind, ponder, consider
      - *multās rēs in corde versāre* = to turn many things over in one's heart
   d. *hūc et illūc versāre* = to toss to and fro

## 复习提示
- 本课出现: *sic fortūna ... utrumque versāvit* — "thus fortune tossed both men" (BG V.44.14)
- **区分 versō (主动) vs versor (异态)**: *versō* = "翻来覆去、摆布"（及物，带宾语）；*versor* = "身处、忙于"（不及物，带 *in* + 夺格）
- ⑭ *versāvit* 是整个故事的高潮句——命运如波浪般来回推搡两个竞争者，为道德化评论定调
- Caesar 用 *versō* 而非 *vertō*：反复动词携带"反复、来回"的语义，更贴合命运无常的主题
