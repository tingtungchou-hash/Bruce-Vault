# excitō, excitāre, excitāvī, excitātus 1.

## 基本信息
- **语言**: 拉丁语
- **词性**: v. (第一变位，*exciō* 的频率体)
- **词源**: *ex-* (出/向上) + *ciō/cieō* (唤起、发动); 频率体 *excitō* = 反复唤起/竖起

## 释义

1. to call out or forth, to wake or rouse up (字面义)
   a. *aliquem ē somnō excitāre* — to wake someone from sleep (Cic. Rep. 6.12)
   b. *ferās excitāre* — to rouse/scare up wild beasts (Cic. Off. 3.17.68)
   c. *aliquem ab īnferīs excitāre* — to summon someone up from the underworld (Cic. Font. 12.26)

2. to raise, erect, build, construct (建筑/工程语境)
   a. *turrēs excitāre* — to erect towers (Caes. B.G. 5.40; id. B.C. 1.25)
   b. *turrēs admodum CXX excitantur incrēdibilī celeritāte* — **本课用法**: about 120 towers are erected with incredible speed (Caes. B.G. 5.40)
   c. *sepulcrum ē lapide excitāre* — to build a tomb from stone (Cic. Leg. 2.27.68)
   d. *ignem excitāre* — to kindle a fire (Caes. B.G. 7.24.4)

3. to raise up, arouse, awaken, excite, stimulate (比喻义)
   a. *animōs excitāre ad laetitiam* — to stir up spirits to joy (Caes. B.G. 7.79.3)
   b. *aliquem ad bellum excitāre* — to incite someone to war (Caes. B.G. 3.10.3)
   c. *rīsum excitāre* — to provoke laughter (Cic. Phil. 3.9.21)

4. to found, cause, occasion
   a. *laudēs excitāre* — to establish a reputation (Cic. Sest. 2.5)

## 复习提示
- 本课用义项 2 (建筑/工程语境)，不是"唤醒"
- 本课搭配 *turrēs excitantur* 是被动语态: "塔楼被竖立起来"
- *excito* 作为 *exciō* 的频率体，暗示动作的反复或持续 (一次次地竖起塔楼)
