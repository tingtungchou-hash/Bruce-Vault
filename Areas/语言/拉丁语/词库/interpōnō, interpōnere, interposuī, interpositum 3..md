# interpōnō, interpōnere, interposuī, interpositum 3.

## 基本信息
- **语言**: 拉丁语
- **词性**: v. a. (第三变位)
- **词源**: inter- (between, among) + pōnō (to put, place)。字面义 "to place between"。

## 释义

1. **To put, place, lay, or set between or among, to interpose, insert**
   a. 空间上：*pīlae inter mūrōs interpōnuntur* — "pillars are placed between the walls" (Caesar BC 2.15)
   b. 时间上：留出间隔 — *spatium ad recreandōs animōs interpōnere* — "to allow an interval for refreshing spirits" (Caesar BC 3.74)
   c. 话语中：插入言语 — *nē 'inquam' et 'inquit' saepius interpōnerētur* — "lest 'I say' and 'he says' be inserted too often" (Cicero)

2. **Trop., to interpose, introduce, put forward**
   a. 作为借口/障碍：*nūllā interpositā dubitātiōne* — "with no hesitation interposed" (Caesar)
   b. **Se interpōnere** — "to interfere, intermeddle, engage oneself in"

3. **To pledge, give (one's word)** (核心义项)
   a. ***Fidem interpōnere*** — "to pledge one's word/credit": *fidem suam in eam rem interpōnere* — "to pledge one's word on this matter" (Caesar BG 5.36)
   b. *iūre iūrandō interpositō* — "with an oath having been interposed"

## 关联词汇
- [[pōnō, pōnere, posuī, positum 3.]] — to put, place
- [[inter (prep. + acc.)]] — between, among
- [[fidēs, fideī f.]] — faith, trust, pledge, word

## 复习提示
- **Caesar 经典句式**：*inque eam rem sē suam fidem interpōnere* — "and on this matter he pledged his own word" (BG 5.36)
- **关键习语**：`fidem interpōnere` 是 Caesar 谈判/外交场景的核心表达，不可逐字译为 "insert faith"，应译为 "pledge one's word"
- `-que` 附着在介词 `in` 后形成 `inque`，注意识别
