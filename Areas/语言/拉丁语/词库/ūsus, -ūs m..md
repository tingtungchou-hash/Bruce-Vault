# ūsus, -ūs m.

## 基本信息
- **语言**: 拉丁语
- **词性**: n. m. (第四变格法)
- **词源**: 来自 [[Resources/lewis-short-json-master/ls_U.json|Lewis & Short]]: *ūtor* 的抽象名词 (如同 *cantus* : *cantō*)，核心义为"使用、实践"

## 释义
1. use, using, employment (使用)
	1. Hī, cum est ūsus atque aliquod bellum incidit [[collegecaesar.14jan25.pdf#page=50&selection=6,27,12,7|Caesar]]
2. usefulness, utility, benefit, advantage (效用)
3. need, want, necessity (需要)
4. occasion, opportunity (机会)
5. practice, experience, skill (实践经验)
6. familiarity, intimacy, intercourse (交往)
