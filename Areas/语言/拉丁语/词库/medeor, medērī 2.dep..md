# medeor, medērī 2.dep.

## 基本信息
- **语言**: 拉丁语
- **词性**: v. dep. (第二变位法，异态动词)
- **词源**: root *madh-* (to be wise; cf. Zend *madha* = healing art, Greek *mathos*, also *medicus*, *re-medium*)

## 释义
1. Lit., to heal, cure, be good for or against a disease
   a. With dat.: *morbo mederi* = to cure a disease
   b. With *contra* + acc.: *contra serpentium ictus mederi* = to heal snake bites
2. Trop., to remedy, relieve, amend, correct, restore
   a. *rei publicae mederi* = to heal/remedy the state
   b. *inopiae frumentariae mederi* = to relieve the grain shortage
      - **本课用法**: *sese mederi posse existimavit* = "he thought that he could remedy (it)"

## 复习提示
- 本课出现: *mederi* (inf.) with *inopiae* (dat.) (BG V.24.6)
- **异态动词**: 形式被动，含义主动
- **支配与格**: *medeor* + dat. = "heal/remedy X"
- *sese mederi posse* = 间接陈述中的不定式，主语 *sese* = *se* (Caesar 自己)
