# moror, morārī, morātus sum 1.dep.

## 基本信息
- **语言**: 拉丁语
- **词性**: v. dep. (第一变位法，异态动词)
- **词源**: from *mora* ("delay")

## 释义
1. Neutr., to delay, tarry, stay, wait, remain, linger, loiter
   a. *in provincia morari* = to tarry in a province
   b. *paulum morari* = to delay a little
   c. *quid moror?* = "why do I linger?" / "why make a long story?"
   d. *haud multa moratus* = without delaying long
   e. *non moror* = "I have no objection" (idiomatic)
2. Act., to delay, retard, detain, hinder
   a. *iter morari* = to delay a march
   b. *hostem morari* = to delay the enemy
   c. To delight, amuse, entertain, fix the attention of
3. *nihil morari* (+ acc.): not to value, not to care about
   - *nihil moror* = "I don't care" / "I have nothing against it"

## 复习提示
- 本课出现: *morari constituit* — "he decided to linger" (BG V.24.8)
- **异态动词**: 形式被动，含义主动
- 常见 Caesar 搭配: *in Gallia morari* = "to linger/remain in Gaul"
- 与 *maneo* 区分: *moror* = linger/delay (暂时性), *maneo* = stay/remain (持久性)
