# dēficiō, dēficere, dēfēcī, dēfectus 3

## 基本信息
- **语言**: 拉丁语
- **词性**: v. 3 (dē- + faciō)
- **词源**: dē- "向下、离开" + faciō "做"；字面义为"不做、撤出"，见 [[Resources/lewis-short-json-master/ls_D.json|Lewis & Short]]

## 释义
1. to fail, give out, run short (中性：缺乏、不足)
	1. cum eius generis cōpia dēfēcit [[collegecaesar.14jan25.pdf#page=50&selection=299,23,305,3|Caesar]]
2. to forsake, desert, abandon (及物：背叛、脱离)
3. to revolt, rebel (政治：反叛)
4. to grow weak, faint (身体：衰竭)
5. to come to an end, cease (时间：终止)
