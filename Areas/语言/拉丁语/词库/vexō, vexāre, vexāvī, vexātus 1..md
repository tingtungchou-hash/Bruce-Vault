# vexō, vexāre, vexāvī, vexātus 1.

## 基本信息
- **语言**: 拉丁语
- **词性**: v. (第一变位法)
- **词源**: < *vehō*（载运）；本义「载运途中颠簸、摇晃」——凡被载而颠簸者即 *vexārī*

## 释义

1. Lit., to shake, jolt, toss in carrying; hence, in gen., to move violently, to shake, agitate（载运时颠簸、摇晃；转指剧烈搅动；罕用）— syn. quatiō
2. Transf., in gen., to injure, damage, molest, annoy, distress, plague, trouble, maltreat, abuse, vex, harass, disquiet, disturb, torment（损害、蹂躏、侵扰、折磨、困扰——本词主要义）— syn. angō, cruciō, vastō
   a. Physically: *agrōs vexātōs et exinānītōs ā Verre* — 被维勒斯搜刮一空的税田；*Siciliam vexāre* — 蹂躏西西里
   b. Mentally: *aliquem probrīs maledictīsque* — 以辱骂恶言折磨某人
   c. P. perf. subst. *vexāta, -ōrum* n. — 受伤的部位、损伤
