# dēscendō, dēscendere, dēscendī, dēscēnsus 3

## 基本信息
- **语言**: 拉丁语
- **词性**: v. 3 (dē- + scandō)
- **词源**: dē- "向下" + scandō "攀爬"；见 [[Resources/lewis-short-json-master/ls_D.json|Lewis & Short]]

## 释义
1. to descend, come/go down, climb down (空间上的下降)
2. to dismount, land (下马/下船)
3. to penetrate, sink into (渗透)
4. to stoop, condescend, resort to (比喻：降低到某种行为)
	1. sed cum eius generis cōpia dēfēcit, etiam ad innocentium supplicia dēscendunt. [[collegecaesar.14jan25.pdf#page=50&selection=299,17,308,8|Caesar]]
