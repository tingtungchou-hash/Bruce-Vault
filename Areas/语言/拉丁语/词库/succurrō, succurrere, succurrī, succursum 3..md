# succurrō, succurrere, succurrī, succursum 3.

## 基本信息
- **语言**: 拉丁语
- **词性**: v. (第三变位法)
- **词源**: *sub-*（在下）+ *currō*（跑）

## 释义

1. to run under（跑到底下）
2. to run or hasten to the aid or assistance of one, to help, aid, assist, succor（跑下来援助；帮助、解救）——主要义，常接与格
   a. of things, to be useful for, good against（对……有效、可治）
   b. impers. pass. *succurritur*（无人称被动）
3. to come into the mind, occur to one（想起、浮现于脑际）
