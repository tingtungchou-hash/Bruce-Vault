# pertineō, pertinēre, pertinuī, (pertentus) 2.

## 基本信息
- **语言**: 拉丁语
- **词性**: v. intr. (第二变位法)
- **词源**: `per-` + `teneo` (to hold); 字面义 "to hold through, to stretch out"

## 释义

1. **To stretch out, reach, extend to a place**; to arrive at a place
   a. Construed with `ad` + acc., or with adverbs
   b. Rarely with `in` or `per` + acc.

2. **Trop.** — To reach, extend (like Greek *dihēkein*)
   a. *eadem bonitas etiam ad multitudinem pertinet* — the same goodness extends also to the multitude (Cic.)
   b. *caritas patriae per omnes ordines pertinebat* — love of country pervaded all ranks (Liv.)

3. **To belong, relate, concern, pertain to**, have reference to, affect
   a. *illa res ad meum officium pertinet* — that matter concerns my duty (Cic.)
   b. *quod ad plures pertinebat* — because the matter pertained to many (Caes. B.G. 5.25) -- 指此事牵涉到多数人，不是 "tends to happen to the masses"

4. **To belong, be the right of** (of a right, as opposed to possession)
   a. *ad quem jure regnum pertinet* — to whom the kingdom rightfully belongs (Liv.)

5. **To apply to, be applicable to, suit**
   a. *ad quem suspicio maleficii pertineat* — on whom suspicion should fall (Cic.)

## 复习提示
`pertineo ad` 是 Caesar 高频用法，表示 "关系到/涉及/属于"。`quod ad plures pertinebat` = "因为这关系到很多人"（是对具体事件的判断，不是普遍规律）。
