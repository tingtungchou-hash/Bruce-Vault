# percutiō, percutere, percussī, percussus 3.

## 基本信息
- **语言**: 拉丁语
- **词性**: v. trans. (第三变位法 -iō 变体)
- **词源**: *per-* + *quatiō* ("to strike through and through")

## 释义
1. To strike through and through, to thrust or pierce through
   a. *gladiō percussus* = pierced by a sword
   b. Transf.: to slay, kill (*aliquem secūrī percutere* = to behead someone)
2. To strike, beat, hit, smite
   a. Lit.: *iānuam manū percutere* = to knock on a door
   b. To stamp, coin money
   c. To play a musical instrument
3. Trop.: to strike, shock, affect deeply
   a. *percutere animum* = to strike the mind, make an impression
   b. To cheat, deceive, impose upon

## 关联词汇
- [[quatiō, quatere, ---, quassus 3.]] — to shake, strike
- [[percellō, percellere, perculī, perculsus 3.]] — to strike down, overturn
- [[trāiciō, trāicere, trāiēcī, trāiectus 3.]] — to throw across, pierce through

## 复习提示
- 本课出现: `quō percussō et exanimātō` — 独立夺格 ("with this man struck down and killed", BG V.44.6)
- 区分: *trāiciō* 偏"投掷刺穿"（⑥用了 *trāicit*），*percutiō* 偏"击打致死"
- 注意 -iō 变体的变位: percussī (完成时), percussus (目的分词)
