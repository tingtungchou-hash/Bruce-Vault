# prōpulsō, prōpulsāre, prōpulsāvī, prōpulsātus 1

## 基本信息
- **语言**: 拉丁语
- **词性**: v. 1 (prōpellō 的反复体)
- **词源**: prō + pulsō

## 释义
1. to repulse 
2. to drive back/off 
3. to ward off 
	1. aut ipsī iniūriās inferrent aut illātās prōpulsārent [[Note - Day 1 Class 15#^propulso|Caes.]]
4. to repel 
5. to avert
