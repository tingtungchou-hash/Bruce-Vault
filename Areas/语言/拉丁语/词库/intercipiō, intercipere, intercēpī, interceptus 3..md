# intercipiō, intercipere, intercēpī, interceptus 3.

## 基本信息
- **语言**: 拉丁语
- **词性**: v. (第三变位，-iō 动词)
- **词源**: *inter-* (在……之间) + *capiō* (拿、抓); 字面义"从中间拿走"

## 释义

1. to seize on the passage, to intercept (核心军事义)
   a. *litterās intercipere* — to intercept letters (Cic. Att. 1.13.2)
   b. *commeātūs intercipere* — to cut off supplies (Liv. 36.3)
   c. *hostēs intercipere* — to cut off/separate the enemy (Tac. H. 4.75)
   d. *nōnnūllī mīlitēs... repentīnō equitum adventū interciperentur* — **本课用法**: some soldiers were intercepted by the sudden arrival of the cavalry (Caes. B.G. 5.39)

2. to interrupt, hinder, cut off, preclude
   a. *medium iter intercipere* — to block the middle of the route (Liv. 25.39.2)
   b. *hostīlēs ingressūs intercipere* — to block the enemy's approaches (Tac. A. 15.3)

3. to take away, rob, steal
   a. *aliquid ab aliquō intercipere* — to take something away from someone (Liv. 3.71)

4. of death: to snatch away, carry off
   a. *sī mē fāta intercēpissent* — if the fates had cut me off (Quint. prooem. 1.6)

## 复习提示
- 复合 *capiō* 动词中，*intercipiō* 强调"中途截取、阻断通道"
- 本课出现的 *interciperentur* 是未完成时虚拟式被动，在 *ut* 从句中表示目的/结果
- 与 *obsideō* (围攻) 搭配时：道路先被封锁 (*obsessīs viīs*)，然后信使被截 (*intercipiuntur*)
