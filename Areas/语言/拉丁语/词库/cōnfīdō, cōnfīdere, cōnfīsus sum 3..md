# cōnfīdō, cōnfīdere, cōnfīsus sum 3.

## 基本信息
- **语言**: 拉丁语
- **词性**: v. (第三变位法，半异相动词；完成时作被动形 *cōnfīsus sum*)
- **词源**: *com-*（加强）+ *fīdō*（信赖）

## 释义

1. to trust confidently in something, confide in, rely firmly upon, believe, be assured of（信赖、确信）
   a. constr. with the abl.：*corporis firmitāte cōnfīdere*
   b. with acc. and inf.（最常用）：*cōnfīdō tē ventūrum esse*
   c. with dat.（尤用于人称）：*sibi cōnfīdere*（自信）；*virtūtī mīlitum cōnfīdere*
   d. with *dē*, *ut*, or absol.
2. part. *cōnfīdēns, -entis*（自信的；多含贬义「狂妄、放肆」）；adv. *cōnfīdenter*
