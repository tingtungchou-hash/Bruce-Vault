# citō (adv.)

## 基本信息
- **语言**: 拉丁语
- **词性**: adv.（源自形容词 *citus* 的中性夺格，< *cieō* "使动"）
- **词源**: *cieō*（使动、激起）→ 完成分词 *citus*（被驱动的、快速的）→ 副词 *citō*（快速地）

## 释义
1. Quickly, speedily, soon
   a. *quam citō!* (Ter.) — "how quickly!"
   b. *sat citō sī sat bene* — "soon enough if well enough"（Cato 的名言）
   c. *citō absolvere, tardē condemnāre* (Cic.) — "to acquit quickly, to condemn slowly"
   d. With negative = not easily: *haud citō* (Ter.) — "not readily"

2. **比较级 *citius*** — more quickly, sooner
   a. *dictō citius* — "quicker than a word" / "in an instant"
   b. *citius ... quam* — "more quickly than"
   c. Without negative = rather, sooner: *citius dīxerim* (Cic.) — "I would sooner say"

3. **最高级 *citissimē*** — most quickly
   a. *citissimē* (Caes. *BG* 4.33) — "very speedily"

## 关联词汇
- [[cieō, ciēre, cīvī, citus 2.]] — to move, stir, rouse（基础动词）
- [[citō, citāre, citāvī, citātus 1.]] — to put in motion; summon, cite（反复体动词）

## 复习提示
- 词族线索：*cieō*（使动）→ *citus*（快）→ *citō*（快速地）→ *concitus*（被激起的）、*excitus*（被唤醒的）、*incitus*（迅速的）
- 常见固定搭配：*quam cito*（多快）、*sat cito*（够快）、*dictō citius*（转瞬间）
- 与 *celeriter* 区分：*citō* 侧重"动作本身的快速"，*celeriter* 侧重"在短时间内的完成"
