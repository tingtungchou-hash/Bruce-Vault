# conferciō, confercīre, ---, confertum 4.

## 基本信息
- **语言**: 拉丁语
- **词性**: v. a. (第四变位，缺陷动词，无完成时系统)
- **词源**: con- (together) + farciō (to stuff, cram)。同源词：`farciō` (to stuff), `frequēns` (crowded)。

## 释义

1. **To stuff or cram together, to press close together**（限定动词形式罕见，但完成分词 `confertus` 为古典常用）
   a. 完成分词 `confertus, -a, -um` — **pressed close, crowded, thick, dense** (与 `rārus` 相对)
   b. 军事用语（最常见）：*confertī proeliārentur* — "they fought in close order" (Caesar BG 5.16)；*confertissimī hostēs* — "the enemy most densely packed" (Caesar)
   c. 带夺格：*confertus ingentī turbā* — "crammed full with a huge crowd" (Livy)

## 关联词汇
- [[farciō, farcīre, farsī, fartum 4.]] — to stuff, cram
- [[cōnferō, cōnferre, contulī, collātum (irreg.)]] — to bring together (different root)

## 复习提示
- 限定动词形式在散文中极为罕见，实际遇到的几乎总是完成分词 `confertus`（作形容词用）
- **Caesar 经典句式**：*ab tantā multitūdine coniecta tēla confertī vitāre poterant* — "crowded together, they were able to avoid the missiles thrown by such a great multitude" (BG 5.35)
- 军事语境中 `confertus` 描述密集阵型，是理解 Caesar 战术描写的关键词
