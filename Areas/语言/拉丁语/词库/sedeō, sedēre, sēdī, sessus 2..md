# sedeō, sedēre, sēdī, sessus 2.

## 基本信息
- **语言**: 拉丁语
- **词性**: v. (第二变位)
- **词源**: 梵语词根 *sad-* (坐); 希腊语 *ἕζομαι* (hezomai, 坐), *ἕδος* (hedos, 座位); 拉丁语同族词: *sēdēs*, *īnsidiae*, *sēdāre*, *sella*; 英语同源: *sit*, *seat*

## 释义

1. (字面义，极高频) to sit, be seated
   a. *hī stant ambō, nōn sedent* — these two are standing, not sitting (Plaut. Capt. prol. 2)
   b. *in subselliīs sedēre* — to sit on the benches (Plaut. Poen. prol. 5)
   c. *in equō sedēre* — to sit on a horse (Cic. Verr. 2.5.10)
   d. *solio sedēre* — to sit on the throne (Ov. M. 6.650)
   e. (晚期拉丁语，被动) *equī sedentur* — horses are ridden (Spart. Hadr. 22)

2. (特定用法) of magistrates, judges: to sit in council, in court, on the bench
   a. *iūdex sedet* — the judge sits/is in session (Liv. 40.8)
   b. *sedēre prō tribūnālī* — to sit on the tribunal (Prop. 1.10.9)

3. to continue sitting, sit still, remain, tarry, abide; (含贬义) to sit idly, linger, loiter
   a. *tōtōs diēs domī sedēre* — to sit at home all day long (Plaut. Aul. 1.1.34)
   b. *in vīllā tōtōs diēs sedēre* — to linger in the villa all day (Cic. Att. 12.44.2)
   c. *sedēmus dēsidēs domī* — we sit idly at home (Liv. 3.68)

4. (军事语境) to sit, i.e. remain encamped, keep the field (常指长期围而不攻)
   a. *sedendō expugnāre urbem* — to take the city by sitting (i.e. by siege/blockade) (Liv. 2.12)
   b. *ad Suessulam sedēre* — to remain encamped at Suessula (Liv. 7.37)
   c. 谚语: *Rōmānus sedendō vincit* — "The Roman conquers by sitting" (Varr. R.R. 1.2.2 — 源于 Fabius Cunctator 的拖延战术)

5. (比喻义) to sink or settle down, subside
   a. *nebula sedet dēnsiōr campō quam montibus* — fog settles thicker on the plain than on the mountains (Liv. 22.4)
   b. *sēdēre mīnae* — the threats subsided (Sil. 10.624)

6. (比喻义) to sit fast, hold firm, be fixed, settled; (衣服) to fit
   a. *toga sedet* — the toga fits/sits well (Quint. 11.3.140)
   b. *sī mihi nōn animō fīxum immōtumque sedēret* — if it were not fixed and immovably settled in my mind (Verg. A. 4.15)
   c. *bellum sedet* — war is resolved upon (Flor. 2.15.4)
   d. *sedet* + 不定式 — it is resolved that... (Stat. Th. 1.324)

## 复习提示
- 谚语 *Rōmānus sedendō vincit* 是理解罗马军事文化的关键——"坐得住"被视为一种美德/战略
- *obsideō* 的含义可以直接从 *sedeō* 推导: *ob-* + *sedeō* = "坐在对面" → "围困、封锁"
- 义项 4 (军事"坐守") 与义项 3 (贬义"闲坐") 共享同一个词但情感色彩完全相反——取决于行为者是 Fabius 式的谋略还是单纯的懒惰
