# grātus, -a, -um adj.

## 基本信息
- **语言**: 拉丁语
- **词性**: adj. (第一/第二变格)
- **词源**: 来自 [[Resources/lewis-short-json-master/ls_G.json|Lewis & Short]]: 与梵语 *har-jami* (爱、渴望)、希腊语 χαίρω (欢喜)、χάρις (恩惠) 同源

## 释义
1. grateful, thankful (被动义：感受到恩惠的)
2. pleasant, agreeable, welcome (主动义：令人愉悦的)
	1. Supplicia eōrum quī in furtō aut in latrōciniō aut aliquā noxiā sint comprehēnsī grātiōra dīs immortālibus esse arbitrantu [[collegecaesar.14jan25.pdf#page=50&selection=266,2,299,15|Caesar]]
3. beloved, dear, acceptable

## 关联词汇

- [[grātulor, -ārī]] — to congratulate, rejoice
- [[grātuītus, -a, -um]] — free, gratuitous
- [[ingrātus, -a, -um]] — ungrateful, unpleasant (反义词)
