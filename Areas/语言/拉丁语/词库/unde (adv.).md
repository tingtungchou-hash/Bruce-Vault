# unde (adv.)

## 基本信息
- **语言**: 拉丁语
- **词性**: adv. (关系副词 / 疑问副词)
- **词源**: 与 `inde` (thence)、`unde` (whence) 构成地点副词序列。源自关系/疑问词干 + 地点后缀 -de。

## 释义

1. **From which place, whence** (关系用法，表地点来源)
   a. 与 `inde` 或 `eodem` 形成关联呼应：*eodem, unde erant profecti* — "to the same place from which they had set out"
   b. 独立用法（直接/间接疑问）：*Unde is?* — "Where do you come from?"
2. **Transferred** (从地点扩展到来源/原因/手段)
   a. 表来源、原因、手段：*unde mihi argentum est?* — "From where do I have money?" / "How do I have money?"
   b. 法律用语：*unde petitur* — "the defendant" (lit. "from whom demand is made")
   c. 不定式组合 *unde unde* = *undecumque* — "from wherever, whencesoever"

## 关联词汇
- [[inde (adv.)]] — thence, from there
- [[ubi (adv.)]] — where (static location)

## 复习提示
- **Caesar 经典句式**: *in eum locum unde erant egressi* — "to the place from which they had gone out" (BG 5.35)
- 区分：`unde` (from where, 来源) vs. `ubi` (where, 静态位置) vs. `quo` (to where, 方向)
