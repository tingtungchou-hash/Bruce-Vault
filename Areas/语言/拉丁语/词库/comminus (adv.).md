# comminus (adv.)

## 基本信息
- **语言**: 拉丁语
- **词性**: adv.
- **词源**: *con-* + *manus* ("hand-to-hand")

## 释义
1. In close contest, hand to hand (with sword, etc.); opp. *ēminus* (at long range, from a distance)
   a. *gladiō comminus rem gerere* = to fight hand-to-hand with the sword (BG V.44.11)
   b. *comminus gladiīs pugnāre* = to fight at close quarters with swords
   c. *neque ēminus hastīs aut comminus gladiīs ūterētur* = nor did he use spears at a distance or swords up close (Cic.)
2. In general: nigh at hand, near to, near, in close proximity (post-Aug.)
   a. *comminus vīsō armātōrum agmine* = seeing the armed column at close range
   b. Of time: immediately, without interruption (provincial usage)
3. Trop.: at close quarters, in person
   a. *nunc comminus agāmus* = now let us fight hand to hand (let's get to the point) (Cic.)

## 复习提示
- 本课出现: *gladiō comminus rem gerit* — "he fights hand-to-hand with the sword" (BG V.44.11)
- 军事术语核心词对: *comminus* (近身) vs *ēminus* (远程)
- ⑪ 中 *comminus* 修饰 *rem gerit*：Vorenus 不是在远处投掷，而是冲上去近身肉搏
