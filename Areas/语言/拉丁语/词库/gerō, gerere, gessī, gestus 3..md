# gerō, gerere, gessī, gestus 3.

## 基本信息
- **语言**: 拉丁语
- **词性**: v. trans. (第三变位法)
- **词源**: root *gas-* (to come, go); caus. sense "to cause to come" → "to bear"

## 释义
1. Lit.: to bear, carry, wear
   a. *vestem gerere* = to wear clothing
   b. *uterum gerere* = to be pregnant
   c. *aliquid prae sē gerere* = to show, manifest, exhibit
2. Trop.: to bear, have, entertain (feelings, attitudes)
   a. *animum fortem gerere* = to have a brave spirit
   b. *inimīcitiās gerere cum aliquō* = to have enmity with someone
   c. *persōnam gerere* = to play a part, assume a role
3. **To conduct, manage, carry on, wage** (key sense)
   a. ***rem gerere*** = to conduct business; in military context: to fight hand-to-hand
      - *gladiō comminus rem gerit* = he fights hand-to-hand with the sword (BG V.44.11)
   b. *bellum gerere* = to wage war
   c. *rēs gestae* = deeds, exploits, achievements, history
   d. *magistrātum gerere* = to hold a magistracy
   e. *sē gerere* (+ adv.) = to behave, conduct oneself
   f. *mōrem alicui gerere* = to humor someone, gratify someone's wishes
4. Of time: to pass, spend
   a. *aetātem gerere* = to pass one's life

## 复习提示
- 本课出现: *gladiō comminus rem gerit* — "he fights hand-to-hand with the sword" (BG V.44.11)
- *rem gerere* 是惯用语 (idiom)，字面"做事情"，军事语境中 = "近身作战"
- 注意不要按字面翻译为 "did the thing"——英文翻译应为 "fights" 或 "engages"（历史现在时）
