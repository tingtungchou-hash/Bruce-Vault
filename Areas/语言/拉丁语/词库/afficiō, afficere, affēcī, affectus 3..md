# afficiō, afficere, affēcī, affectus 3

## 基本信息
- **语言**: 拉丁语
- **词性**: v. 3 (ad- + faciō 复合词)
- **词源**: ad "向" + faciō "做"；字面义为"对某人做某事"，见 [[Resources/lewis-short-json-master/ls_A.json|Lewis & Short]]

## 释义
1. to affect, influence (acc. + abl. of manner) — 对身心施加影响
	1. quī sunt adfectī graviōribus morbīs [[collegecaesar.14jan25.pdf#page=50&selection=110,0,125,1|Caesar]]
2. to treat, handle (以某种方式对待)
3. to manage, accomplish
4. to weaken, exhaust (affectus 作分词)

## 关联词汇

- [[affectus, -ūs m.]] — state of mind, emotion, affection
- [[cōnficiō, cōnficere, cōnfēcī, cōnfectus 3]] — to finish, exhaust
- [[perficiō, perficere, perfēcī, perfectus 3]] — to accomplish, perfect
