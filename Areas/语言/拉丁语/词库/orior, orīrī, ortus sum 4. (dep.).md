# orior, orīrī, ortus sum 4. (dep.)

## 基本信息
- **语言**: 拉丁语
- **词性**: v. dep. (第四变位法，异态动词；部分形式按第三变位)
- **词源**: 可能与希腊语 `ornumi` (to stir up) 同源; 印欧词根 *h₃er- (to rise, set in motion)

## 释义

1. **To rise, arise, spring up, start, begin, originate**
   a. **Of persons**: to rise, bestir oneself, get up; to be born, spring from
   b. **Of heavenly bodies**: to rise, become visible, appear
      - *orto sole* — at sunrise (Hor.)
      - *orta luce* — at daybreak (Caes.)
   c. **Of events**: to arise, break out, begin
      - *initium repentini tumultus ac defectionis ortum est* — the beginning of a sudden uprising and rebellion arose (Caes. B.G. 5.26)
      - *bellum oritur* — war breaks out

2. **To spring from, descend from, be born of**
   a. *a nobis ortus* — descended from us
   b. *ex eadem familia ortus* — sprung from the same family

3. Derived forms:
   a. *oriens, -entis* — the rising sun; the East, the Orient
   b. *ortus, -us* m. — a rising; origin, birth

## 复习提示
BG 5.26: *ortum est* = "arose" (自发产生), 不是 "was raised" (被某人举起)。注意 `orior` 是异态动词，完成时主动形式 `ortus sum` 译为主动义。
