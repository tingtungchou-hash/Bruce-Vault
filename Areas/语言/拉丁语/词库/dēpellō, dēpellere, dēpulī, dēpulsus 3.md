# dēpellō, dēpellere, dēpulī, dēpulsus 3.

## 基本信息
- **语言**: 拉丁语
- **词性**: v. a. (第三变位)
- **词源**: *dē-* + *pellō* (to drive)

## 释义

1. (字面) to drive out, drive away, remove, expel
   a. *dēpellere aliquem dē locō* — 将某人逐出某地
   b. *dēpellere morbum* — 驱除疾病 (见 Caes. *B.G.* 6.17 — **本课用法**)
   c. *dēpellere aliquem urbe* — 将某人逐出城市 (流放)
   d. *dēpellere famem* — 驱走饥饿

2. (军事) to dislodge an enemy from his position
   a. *dēpellere hostem locō* — 将敌人赶出阵地
   b. *dēpellere praesidia* — 驱逐守军

3. (引申) to deter, divert, dissuade from
   a. *dēpellere aliquem dē sententiā* — 使某人改变主意
   b. *dēpellere perīcula* — 消除危险
