# flāgitium, -ī n.

## 基本信息
- **语言**: 拉丁语
- **词性**: n.（第二变格法）
- **词源**: < *flāgitō*（急切要求、纠缠）；本义「炽热的欲望、情欲之热」

## 释义

1. Lit., an eager or furious demand, importunity, urgency（急切的要求、纠缠；后奥古斯都时期，罕用）
2. Transf., esp., a shameful or disgraceful act done in the heat of passion; a burning shame, disgraceful thing（因情欲冲动而行的可耻之事、奇耻大辱）— syn. scelus, nefas, facinus, maleficium, peccatum, delictum, crimen
3. In gen., any shameful or disgraceful act or thing, without the accessory idea of passion（泛称一切可耻之事、丑行、荒谬之举）

## 关联词汇
