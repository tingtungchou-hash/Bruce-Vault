# concidō, concidere, concidī 3.

## 基本信息
- **语言**: 拉丁语
- **词性**: v. intrans. (第三变位法)
- **词源**: *con-* + *cadō* ("to fall together, fall down")

## 释义
1. To fall together, fall down, tumble to the ground
   a. Of buildings/towers: *turris concidit* = the tower collapsed
   b. Of persons: *in locum dēiectus inferiōrem concidit* = thrown into a lower spot, he falls (BG V.44.12)
2. To fall down faint or lifeless; to fall in battle
   a. *in proeliō concidere* = to fall in battle
   b. *concidit et sonitum arma dedērunt* = he fell and his armor clashed
3. Trop.: to lose strength, to be overthrown, to fail, decay, perish
   a. *concidunt ventī* = the winds subside
   b. *senātūs auctōritās concidit* = the Senate's authority collapsed
   c. *fidēs concidit* = credit failed

## 关联词汇
- [[cadō, cadere, cecidī, cāsus 3.]] — to fall
- [[concīdō, concīdere, concīdī, concīsus 3.]] — to cut to pieces (from *caedō*, with long ī — different word!)
- [[occidō, occidere, occidī, occāsus 3.]] — to fall down, set (sun); perish

## 复习提示
- 本课出现: *in locum dēiectus inferiōrem concidit* — "thrown into a lower spot, he fell" (BG V.44.12)
- **长音区分**: *concidō* (短 i, from *cadō*) = "倒下、摔倒" vs. *concīdō* (长 ī, from *caedō*) = "切碎"
- 这是一个非常重要的区分——两个词外形极像但来源和含义完全不同
- ⑫ *dēiectus* + *concidit*: 先被丢下去 (dēiciō 的被动分词) → 再摔倒 (主动)
