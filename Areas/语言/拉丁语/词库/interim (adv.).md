# interim (adv.)

## 基本信息
- **语言**: 拉丁语
- **词性**: adv.
- **词源**: `inter` + 古宾格 `im`（来自 `is`）; 字面义 "between that"

## 释义

1. **Meanwhile, in the meantime** (core meaning, = *interea*)
   a. *interim ab omnibus legatis ... certior factus est* — meanwhile he was informed by all the legates (Caes. B.G. 5.25)
   b. *tu hic ante aedes interim speculare* — you meanwhile keep watch here before the house (Plaut.)

2. **For a time, for a while** (post-Augustan)
   a. *ut uno interim contenti simus exemplo* — so that we may be content with one example for the moment (Quint.)

3. **However, nevertheless**
   a. *interim velim mihi ignoscas* — nevertheless I would like you to forgive me (Cic.)

4. **Sometimes** (post-Augustan)
   a. *interim ... interim* — sometimes ... sometimes

## 关联词汇
- [[intereā (adv.)]] — meanwhile (near-synonym)
- [[inter (prep. + acc.)]] — between, among

## 复习提示
最简单清晰的用法就是 "meanwhile"。BG 5.25.5: Caesar 派 Plancus 前往 Carnutes，**与此同时** (*interim*) 收到了各路将领已到达冬营的汇报。
