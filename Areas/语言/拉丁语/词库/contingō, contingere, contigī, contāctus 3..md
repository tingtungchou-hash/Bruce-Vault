# contingō, contingere, contigī, contāctus 3.

## 基本信息
- **语言**: 拉丁语
- **词性**: v. trans. & intrans. (第三变位)
- **词源**: *con-* + *tangō* (to touch); 字面义为 "全面触及"

## 释义

1. Lit.: to touch on all sides, take hold of, seize
   a. *fūnem manū contingere* — 用手抓住绳子
   b. *terram ōsculō contingere* — 亲吻大地

2. to be near, neighboring, or contiguous; to border upon, reach, extend to
   a. *turrī adactā et contingente vāllum* — 塔被推向并触碰城墙 (Caes. *B.G.* 5.43 — **本课用法**)
   b. *Helviī, quī fīnēs Arvernōrum contingunt* — 厄尔维人，其领地与阿维尔尼接壤 (Caes. *B.G.* 7.7)

3. with idea of motion: to reach, arrive at, attain to (poet.)
   a. *optātam mētam cursū contingere* — 疾驰中抵达渴望的终点
   b. *Ītaliam contingere* — 抵达意大利

4. to touch with pollution, to pollute, stain (esp. in perf. pass. part.)
   a. *equī nullō mortālī opere contāctī* — 未被凡人触碰过的马

5. to be connected with, related to, concern
   a. *aliquem sanguine ac genere contingere* — 与某人有血缘和族系关联

6. **of occurrences (极高频)**: to happen to one, befall, fall to one's lot (w. dat.)
   a. *cui tam subitō tot contigerint commoda* — 谁竟能如此突然地获得这么多好处 (Ter.)
   b. *nōn cuivīs hominī contingit adīre Corinthum* — 并非每个人都能去科林斯 (Hor.)
   c. impers. *mihi contingit* + inf. — "我有幸/得以..."

## 复习提示
- 本课用字面义 (touching the rampart)，但更高频的是义项 6 (happen/befall + dat.)
- *contigit ut* 和 *mihi contingit* + inf. 是常见套话
- 两个截然不同的义项 (触碰 vs. 发生) 需根据语境判断
