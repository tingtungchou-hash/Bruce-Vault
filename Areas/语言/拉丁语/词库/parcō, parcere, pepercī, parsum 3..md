# parcō, parcere, pepercī, parsum 3.

## 基本信息
- **语言**: 拉丁语
- **词性**: v. intr. (第三变位，接与格)
- **词源**: 源自 *sparco；同源词：Greek *σπαρνός* (rare)；English *spare*。与 paucus (few)、parvus (small) 可能同根。

## 释义

1. **Lit., to act sparingly, be sparing with respect to a thing, to spare** (接与格)
   a. 事物作宾语（古典用法）：*sumptuī parcere* — "to spare expense" (Cicero); *operae parcere* — "to spare one's labor"
   b. 独立使用：*parcendō tolerāre* — "to endure by sparing (i.e., rationing)" (Caesar BG 7.71)
   c. 带宾格（古语/诗歌）：*pecūniam parcere* — "to spare money" (Plautus)

2. **To spare persons, to have mercy upon, forbear to injure or punish** (接与格)
   a. *sibi mīlitibusque parcere* — "to spare himself and the soldiers" (Caesar BG 5.36 — 本课用法)
   b. *parcere omnibus* — "to spare all" (Cicero)
   c. *parcere hostibus* — "to spare the enemy"

3. **To abstain or refrain from doing a thing; to forbear, leave off, desist, stop, cease**
   a. 带不定式（非古典散文）：*parcite, ovēs, nimium prōcēdere* — "refrain, O sheep, from advancing too far" (Vergil)
   b. 带 `ab` + 夺格：*ā caedibus et ab incendiīs parcere* — "to desist from slaughter and burning" (Livy)

## 复习提示
- **核心语法**：`parcō` 接与格（不是宾格！）— *sibi mīlitibusque parcat*（"spare him and the soldiers"），与格 `sibi` 和 `mīlitibus`
- **完成时**：Cicero 和 Caesar 一律用 `pepercī`（重叠完成时），不用 `parsī`
- **Caesar 经典句式**：*rogātum ut sibi mīlitibusque parcat* — "to ask that he spare him and his soldiers" (BG 5.36)
