# certior fio (idiom)

## 基本信息
- **语言**: 拉丁语
- **词性**: 动词惯用短语
- **词源**: `certus` (certain) 的比较级 `certior` + `fio` (to become, be made)

## 释义

1. **To be informed, to learn, to be made aware of**
   a. 字面义: "to be made more certain"
   b. *certior factus est* — he was informed (Caes. B.G. passim)
   c. *certior fieri de aliqua re* — to be informed about something
   d. *certior factus est in hiberna perventum* — he was informed that they had reached winter quarters (Caes. B.G. 5.25)

2. Active form: **certiorem facere aliquem** — to inform someone
   a. *Caesarem certiorem faciunt* — they inform Caesar

## 关联词汇
- [[certus, -a, -um adj.]] — certain, fixed, determined
- [[fiō, fierī, factus sum]] — to become, be made, happen
- [[certē (adv.)]] — certainly, surely

## 复习提示
这是 Caesar 最常用的 "被告知" 表达之一，全书反复出现。注意主语是被通知的人: *Caesar certior factus est* = "Caesar was informed"（主语是 Caesar，不是无人称的 "it was made clear"）。

语法上，`certior` 是形容词比较级作主语补足语，`factus est` 是 `fio` 的完成时。后面的内容用间接陈述（acc. + inf.）表达。
