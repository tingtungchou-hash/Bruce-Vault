# cōnstō, cōnstāre, cōnstitī, cōnstātus 1.

## 基本信息
- **语言**: 拉丁语
- **词性**: v. n. (第一变位)
- **词源**: con- + stō (to stand)

## 释义

1. To stand together, stand with some person or thing.

2. Lit. (very rare): constant, conserunt sermones inter se drapetae,Plaut. Curc. 2, 3, 11.
   a. With cum and abl. (cf. consisto, II. B. 3.): considerabit, constetne oratio aut cum re aut ipsa secum,Cic. Inv. 2, 14, 45: sententiā non constare cum superioribus et inferioribus sententiis, etc.,Auct. Her. 2, 10, 14.

3. With the access. idea of firmness, to stand firm, to remain immovable, unchanging, steadfast, to abide, last, endure, persevere, etc. (very freq. in all perr. and styles).
