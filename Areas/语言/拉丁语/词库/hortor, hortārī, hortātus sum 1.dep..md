# hortor, hortārī, hortātus sum 1.dep.

## 基本信息
- **语言**: 拉丁语
- **词性**: v. dep. (第一变位，异态动词)
- **词源**: for *horitor*, cf. *horior* (古形式)

## 释义

1. to urge strongly, incite, encourage, exhort
   a. with acc. pers.: *aliquem hortārī* — to urge/encourage someone
   b. *hortārī ut/nē* + subj. — to urge that/that not (see Caes. *B.G.* 5.38: *hortātur nē... occāsiōnem dīmittant* — **本课用法**)
   c. *hortārī ad/in aliquid* — to urge to something
   d. with inf. (rare): *hortārī sequī* — to urge to follow

2. (in partic., milit.) to exhort soldiers before battle
   a. *suōs hortātus signum dat* — having exhorted his men, he gives the signal (Caes. *B.G.* 3.19)

3. (of inanimate things) to prompt, incite: *multae rēs eum hortābantur* — many things urged him on

## 复习提示
异态动词，-tur 结尾但意义主动。*hortātur nē... dīmittant* 中 *nē* + 虚拟式为否定祈愿/目的从句，非恐惧从句。
