# anteferō, anteferre, antetulī, antelātus irreg.

## 基本信息
- **语言**: 拉丁语
- **词性**: v. trans. (不规则，ferō 复合词)
- **词源**: *ante-* + *ferō* ("to bear before")

## 释义
1. To bear or carry before (rare; Tac.)
2. To place before, prefer, give preference to
   a. *aliquem alicui anteferre* = to prefer someone to someone
   b. Pass.: *omnī honōre antelātus* = preferred in every honor
3. To anticipate, consider beforehand (rare; Cic.)

## 关联词汇
- [[ferō, ferre, tulī, lātus irreg.]] — to bear, carry
- [[auferō, auferre, abstulī, ablātus irreg.]] — to take away
- [[cōnferō, cōnferre, contulī, collātus irreg.]] — to bring together
