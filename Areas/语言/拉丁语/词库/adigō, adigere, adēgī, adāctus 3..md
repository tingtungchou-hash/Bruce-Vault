# adigō, adigere, adēgī, adāctus 3.

## 基本信息
- **语言**: 拉丁语
- **词性**: v. trans. (第三变位)
- **词源**: *ad-* + *agō* (to drive); 字面义为 "驱向/推向某处"

## 释义

1. to drive, bring, or take a person or thing to a place (与 *ad*, *in* 或地点副词连用)
   a. *pecore ... adacto* — 牲畜被赶到一起 (Caes. *B.G.* 7.17)
   b. *turrī adactā et contingente vāllum* — 塔被推向并触及城墙 (Caes. *B.G.* 5.43 — **本课用法**)
   c. *tigna fistucis adigere* — 用夯槌把木桩打进去 (Caes. *B.G.* 4.17)

2. of weapons: to drive home, plunge, thrust
   a. *ut telum adigi non posset* — 以至于武器无法刺入 (Caes. *B.G.* 3.51)
   b. *ferrum iugulō adigere* — 用剑刺入喉咙

3. Fig.: to drive or force someone into a situation or state of mind
   a. *aliquem ad insāniam adigere* — 把某人逼疯
   b. *aliquem ad mortem adigere* — 逼某人赴死

4. t.t. *adigere aliquem ad iūs iūrandum* (或 *iūre iūrandō* / *sacrāmentō*): to put someone on oath, to swear someone in
   a. *omnibus iūs iūrandum adactīs* — 所有人都被要求宣誓 (Caes. *B.G.* 7.67)

## 复习提示
- 夺格独立中极高频: *turrī adactā*, *pecore adactō* ——见一次记牢
- *ad-* 前缀 + *agō* = 驱向，与 *cōgō* (驱到一起→强迫)、*redigō* (驱回→使变成) 对比记忆
- 本课 *turrī adactā et contingente vāllum*：塔被推到并触碰城墙
