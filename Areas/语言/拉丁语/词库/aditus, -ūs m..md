# aditus, -ūs m.

## 基本信息
- **语言**: 拉丁语
- **词性**: n. m. (第四变格法)
- **词源**: *adeō*（走向、接近）的动作名词（verbal noun）；源自 *ad-* + *itus* (< *eō*)

## 释义
1. A going to, approach, access（接近的动作或可能性）
   a. 字面：*quōrum abitū aut aditū* (Lucr.) = "by their going away or coming near"
   b. *aditus ad eum difficilior* (Cic.) = "access to him is rather difficult"
   c. 比喻 — 接近某人（社交/政治层面）的权利或机会：
     - *aliquem sermōnis aditum ... habēre* (Caes. *BG* 5.41.1) = "to have some access for conversation (with someone)"
     - *facilēs aditūs ad eum prīvātōrum* (Cic.) = "easy access to him for private citizens"
     - *homō rārī aditūs* (Liv.) = "a man rarely accessible"
2. （转义，具体化）The place through which one approaches; an entrance, avenue, passage
   a. *aditus īnsulae mūnītī* (Cic.) = "the approaches to the block were fortified"
   b. *prīmō aditū vestibulōque prohibēre* (Cic.) = "to keep them from the first approach and the entrance"
   c. 比喻 — 切入点、门径：*aditus ad causam* (Cic.) = "an approach to the case"; *quārtus aditus ad initia rērum* (Varro) = "a fourth approach to the origins of things"

## 复习提示
- 本课出现：BG 5.41.1 *aliquem sermōnis aditum causamque amīcitiae ... habēbant* — "they had some access for conversation and some basis for friendship"
- 核心区分：*aditus* 的 (1) 抽象义 = "接近的机会/权利" vs. (2) 具体义 = "入口/通道" — BG 语境下几乎总是抽象义
- 结构要点：*aditus* + gen.（...的门路）+ *ad* / *in* + acc.（通往...）
