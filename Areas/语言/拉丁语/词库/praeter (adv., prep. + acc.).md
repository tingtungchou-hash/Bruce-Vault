# praeter (adv., prep. + acc.)

## 基本信息
- **语言**: 拉丁语
- **词性**: adv. / prep. + acc. (不变词)
- **词源**: *prae* + demonstrative suffix *-ter* (as in *inter*, *subter*, *propter*)

## 释义
1. (adv.) Past, by, beyond
   a. Comparatively: before, beyond, above, more than
   b. Exceptionally: except, excepting, unless, save
2. (prep. + acc.) Past, by, before, along
   a. Lit. of place: *praeter castra copias transducere* = to lead troops past the camp
   b. Trop.: over, beyond; against, contrary to; aside from
      - *praeter spem* = beyond hope
      - *praeter consuetudinem* = contrary to custom
   c. Comparatively: beyond, above, more than
      - *praeter ceteros* = above the rest
   d. In addition: besides, in addition to
   e. Exception: besides, except
      - *omnibus sententiis praeter unam* = by all votes except one
