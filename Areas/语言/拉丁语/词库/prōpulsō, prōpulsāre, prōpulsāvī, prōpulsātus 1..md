# prōpulsō, prōpulsāre, prōpulsāvī, prōpulsātus 1

## 基本信息
- **语言**: 拉丁语
- **词性**: v. 1 (prōpellō 的反复体/frequentative)
- **词源**: prō- "向前" + *pulsō* "推打" (反复体)；[[Resources/lewis-short-json-master/ls_P.json|Lewis & Short]] 释为 *prōpellō* 的反复体

## 释义
1. to drive back, repel, repulse (击退)
	1. aut ipsī iniūriās inferrent aut illātās prōpulsārent [[collegecaesar.14jan25.pdf#page=50&selection=19,5,38,4|Caesar]]
2. to ward off, avert, keep off (挡开，用于比喻)
	1. *periculum* — 抵御危险
	1. *suspicionem ā sē* — 消除对自己的怀疑

## 关联词汇

- [[pulsō, pulsāre, pulsāvī, pulsātus 1]] — to push, strike (反复体)
- [[repellō, repellere, reppulī, repulsus 3]] — to drive back
