# perturbō, perturbāre, perturbāvī, perturbātus 1.

## 基本信息
- **语言**: 拉丁语
- **词性**: v. trans. (第一变位)
- **词源**: *per-* (thoroughly, completely) + *turbō* (to disturb); 字面义为 "彻底扰乱/全面搅乱"

## 释义

1. Lit.: to throw into complete confusion or disorder, to confuse, disturb thoroughly
   a. *omnia perturbāre* — 把所有事搅得一团糟 (Ter.)
   b. *prōvinciam perturbāre* — 使行省陷入混乱 (Cic.)
   c. *aetātum ōrdinem perturbāre* — 打乱年龄次序 (Cic.)
   d. *reliquōs (mīlitēs) incertīs ōrdinibus perturbaverunt* — 使其余士兵阵脚大乱 (Caes. *B.G.* 4.32)
   e. *aciem perturbāre* — 彻底打乱阵线 (Sall.)

2. Trop.: to disturb mentally, discompose, embarrass, confound
   a. *mentēs animōsque perturbat timor* — 恐惧搅乱了心神 (Caes. *B.G.* 1.39)
   b. *clāmōre perturbārī* — 被喊声吓得慌乱
   c. *dē reī pūblicae salūte perturbārī* — 为国家安危心绪不宁 (Cic.)
   d. *magnō animī mōtū perturbātus* — 心神极度不安

3. (part. adj.) *perturbātus, -a, -um*: troubled, disordered, embarrassed
   a. *cīvitātem perturbātam trādidistis* — 你们交出了一个乱成一团的城邦 (Cic.)
   b. *perturbātior metū* — 被恐惧搅得更加慌乱 (Cic.)

## 复习提示
- *per-* (彻底) + *turbō* (扰乱) = 不仅扰乱，而是"彻底翻个底朝天"
- Caesar 中 *perturbō* 常用于描写军队心理崩溃: *mentēs animōsque perturbat timor* (*B.G.* 1.39)
- 强度等级: *turbō* (扰乱) < *perturbō* (彻底扰乱) < *conturbō* (全面崩溃/破产)
