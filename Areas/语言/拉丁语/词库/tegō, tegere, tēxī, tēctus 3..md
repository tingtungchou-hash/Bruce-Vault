# tegō, tegere, tēxī, tēctus 3.

## 基本信息
- **语言**: 拉丁语
- **词性**: v. trans. (第三变位法，-ō 型)
- **词源**: 同源词：希腊语 στέγω（覆盖）、τέγος/στέγος（屋顶）；梵语 sthag-（隐藏）；德语 decken（覆盖）；英语 thatch（茅草）

## 释义
**字面 — 覆盖**
1. To cover, clothe, envelop
   a. *corpus palliō tegere* — to cover the body with a cloak
   b. *caput galeā tegere* — to cover the head with a helmet
   c. **Greek-style acc. construction**（诗歌中）：*prīmā tēctus lanūgine mālās* — "his cheeks covered with first down"
   d. 本课用法：*quae mōre Gallicō strāmentīs erant tēctae* (Caes. *BG* 5.43.1) — "which were covered with thatch in the Gallic manner"
2. To cover, hide, conceal
   a. *fugientem silvae tēxērunt* (Caes.) — "the woods hid him as he fled"
   b. *latibulīs sē tegere* — to hide oneself in hiding places
3. To shelter, protect, defend
   a. *portus ab Āfricō tegēbātur* (Caes.) — "the harbor was sheltered from the south-west wind"
   b. *aliquem cōnservāre et tegere* — to preserve and protect someone

**比喻**
1. To cloak, veil, conceal, keep secret
   a. *nōmen tyrannī hūmānitāte tegere* (Nepos) — "to cloak the name of tyrant with kindness"
   b. *turpia facta ōrātiōne tegere* (Sall.) — "to cover shameful deeds with speech"
2. To defend, protect, guard
   a. *aliquid excūsātiōne amīcitiae tegere* (Cic.) — "to shield something with the excuse of friendship"
