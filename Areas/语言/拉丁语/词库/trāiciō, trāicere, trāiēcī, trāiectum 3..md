# trāiciō, trāicere, trāiēcī, trāiectum 3.

## 基本信息
- **语言**: 拉丁语
- **词性**: v. a. (第三变位 -iō 动词)
- **词源**: trāns- (across, through) + iaciō (to throw)。Caesar 一律拼写为 `trānsiciō`。

## 释义

1. **To throw across, to cause to cross** (将人/物转移过去)
   a. 军队/辎重："to transport, ship across, lead over" — *exercitum flūmen trāicere* — "to transport the army across the river"
   b. *sē trāicere* — "to cross over (oneself)"
   c. *oculōs trāicere* — "to cast one's eyes around" (poetic)

2. **To pass through, make a way through**
   a. 军事："to break through" — *mediam trāicere aciem* — "to break through the middle of the battle line"
   b. **To strike through, stab through, pierce, transfix** — *aliquem pīlīs trāicere* — "to pierce someone with javelins" (Caesar BG 7.82)
   c. 本课用法：*utrumque femur trāgulā trāicitur* — "he is pierced by a tragula in each thigh" (BG 5.35)

3. **To cross over, pass over** (不及物/带地点宾格)
   a. *flūmen trāicere* — "to cross the river"
   b. *mare trāicere* — "to cross the sea"

## 复习提示
- 现在时被动 `trāicitur` 形式辨识要点：trā- + -ic- (来自 iac-) + -i- (现在时词干元音) + -tur (被动)
- 注意区分义项 1（运输/转移）和义项 2b（刺穿），完全不相关但共用一个词
