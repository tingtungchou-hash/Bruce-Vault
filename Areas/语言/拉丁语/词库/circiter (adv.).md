# circiter (adv. & prep. + acc.)

## 基本信息
- **语言**: 拉丁语
- **词性**: adv. & prep. + acc.
- **词源**: 来自 `circus` (circle); 字面义 "round about"

## 释义

1. **Adv.** — of time, designating nearness to a fixed point, about, near, approximately
   a. *diebus circiter quindecim* — about fifteen days (Caes. B.G. 2.2)
   b. *hora diei circiter quarta* — about the fourth hour of the day (Caes. B.G. 4.23)
   c. *circiter triennium* — about three years (Plaut.)

2. **Adv.** — of place (rare), round about, on every side

3. **Prep. + acc.** — (rare) of place or time: about, near
   a. *loca haec circiter* — somewhere about here (Plaut.)

## 关联词汇
- [[circum (prep. + acc.)]] — around
- [[circus, -i m.]] — circle, ring

## 复习提示
**高频混淆警告**: 不要与 `celeriter` (quickly) 混淆。`circiter` = "大约" (时间/数量), `celeriter` = "迅速地" (方式)。

In BG 5.26: *Diebus circiter XV* = "About 15 days," not "Quickly within 15 days."
