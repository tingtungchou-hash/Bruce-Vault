# adeō, adīre, adiī, aditus 4.

## 基本信息
- **语言**: 拉丁语
- **词性**: v. trans. / intr. (第四变位法)
- **词源**: *ad-*（朝、向）+ *eō*（去）→ "走向、接近"

## 释义
**字面 — 走向、接近**
1. To go to, approach (a person or place)
   a. With *ad* + acc.: *ad mē adīre* = "to approach me"
   b. With *in* + acc.: *in iūs adīre* = "to go to law / to court"
   c. With acc. alone: *castra adīre* = "to approach the camp"; *municipia adīre* = "to visit the towns"
   d. 绝对用法：*adeō, cōnsistunt* = "they approach, they halt"
2. To address, apply to, consult (approaching for a purpose)
   a. *aliquem adīre* = "to approach someone (to ask/consult)"
   b. *adīre deōs / arās / ōrācula* = "to approach the gods / altars / oracles"（as a suppliant）
   c. *adīre librōs Sibyllīnōs* = "to consult the Sibylline Books"
3. To attack, assail（逼近 + 敌意）: *audet adīre virum* (Verg.) = "he dares to approach/attack the man"

**比喻 — 着手、承担**
1. To undertake, set about, undergo, submit to
   a. *perīculum adīre* = "to undergo danger"
   b. *labōrem adīre* = "to undertake a task"
   c. *hērēditātem adīre* = "to enter upon an inheritance"（法律术语）
   d. *adīre ad rem pūblicam* = "to enter public life"
2. 习语：*adīre manum alicui* = "to deceive / make sport of someone"（Plautus 用语）

## 关联词汇
- [[eō, īre, iī, itus]] — to go（基础动词）
- [[adeō (adv.)]] — so, so much, indeed（源自同一词形但语义分化）
- [[aditus, -ūs m.]] — approach, access, entrance（动作名词）

## 复习提示
- 本课相关：BG 5.41.1 *aliquem sermōnis aditum ... habēre* → *aditus* 是 *adeō* 的动作名词 — "拥有某种交谈的门路（接近交谈的通道）"
- 核心多义性：物理接近 → 社交接近（拜访/咨询）→ 敌意接近（攻击）→ 比喻承担（冒险/任务）
