# vallum, -ī n.

## 基本信息
- **语言**: 拉丁语
- **词性**: n. n. (第二变格法)
- **词源**: collective of *vallus* (stake, pale); the line of palisades about an intrenchment

## 释义

1. an earthen wall or rampart set with palisades, a palisaded rampart, intrenchment, circumvallation
   a. *castra vallō fossāque mūnīre* — to fortify the camp with a rampart and ditch (Caes. *B.G.* 2.5)
   b. *aquilam intrā vallum prōicere* — to throw the eagle within the rampart (Caes. *B.G.* 5.37 — **本课用法**)
   c. *oppidum vallō et fossā cingere* — to surround a town with a rampart and ditch

2. (transf., in gen.) a wall, rampart, fortification, natural barrier
   a. *vallum Alpium* — the rampart of the Alps; *vallum dentium* — the rampart of the teeth; *vallum aristārum* — the rampart of the beard (of wheat)

## 复习提示
**形近词辨析**：*vallum*（壁垒、栅栏，n.） vs *vallēs/vallis*（山谷，f.）。仅差一个 -ēs，今天翻译中混淆导致严重误译（"threw into the valley" 应为 "threw within the rampart"）。
