# praestō (adv.)

## 基本信息
- **语言**: 拉丁语
- **词性**: adv. (源自 `praestus`, 为 `prae` 的最高级形式)
- **词源**: `prae` (before) 的最高级形式; 字面义 "in the foremost place"

## 释义

1. **At hand, ready, present, here** — usually with `esse`
   a. *praesto esse alicui* — to be at hand for someone, to be ready to help/meet someone
   b. *hic praesto est* — he is here at hand
   c. *Sabino Cottaeque praesto fuissent* — they had met/been at hand for Sabinus and Cotta (Caes. B.G. 5.26)

2. In partic.: *praesto esse* — to present oneself in a hostile manner, to resist, oppose
   a. *si quis mihi praesto fuerit cum armatis hominibus* — if anyone should oppose me with armed men (Cic.)

## 关联词汇
- [[praestō, praestāre, praestitī, praestitus 1.]] — to excel, surpass; to perform, fulfill (动词)
- [[praestolor, praestolārī, praestolātus sum 1.]] — to wait for, expect

## 复习提示
`praesto esse` + dat. 的核心含义是 "在场/待命，准备应对某人"。在 BG 5.26 中，Ambiorix 和 Catuvolcus 在边境上 "迎接/应对" 了 Sabinus 和 Cotta——是军事上的 "接应/对峙"，不是部落间的友好会面。

注意区分: `praesto` (adv.) + `esse` 与动词 `praesto, -are` (to excel/perform) 是不同词条。
