# coorior, coorīrī, coortus sum 4. dep.

## 基本信息
- **语言**: 拉丁语
- **词性**: v. dep. intr. (第四变位法，异态动词)
- **词源**: *con-*（共同、加强）+ *orior*（升起、出现）→ "to arise together / burst forth"

## 释义
1. To come forth, stand up, arise, appear, rise, break forth
   a. *bellum coortum est* (Caes.) — "war broke out"
   b. *sēditiō coorta est* (Liv.) — "a sedition arose"
   c. *rīsus omnium cum hilāritāte coortus est* (Nep.) — "laughter of all broke out with cheerfulness"

2. **Of natural phenomena**（Caesar 极常用）— to arise, break out, begin
   a. *tempestās coorta est* (Caes.) — "a storm arose"
   b. **本课用法**：*maximō coörtō ventō* (Caes. *BG* 5.43.1) — "a very great wind having arisen"
   c. *ventus coortus est* (Caes.) — "a wind arose"

3. **Of a hostile rising** — to rise up, break forth
   a. *Rōmānī ... coortī pugnam integram ēdidērunt* (Liv.) — "the Romans, having risen up, delivered a fresh battle"
   b. *vōcēs coortae sunt* (Liv.) — "shouts broke forth"
   c. *in nōs Sarmatārum gentēs coortae* (Tac.) — "the Sarmatian tribes having risen against us"

## 关联词汇

- [[exorior, exorīrī, exortus sum 4. dep.]] — to spring up, arise

## 复习提示
- 本课出现：BG 5.43.1 *maximō coörtō ventō*（独立夺格）— "a very great wind having arisen"
- *coorior* 专用于突然爆发的自然现象、战争、暴动、情绪——携带"突然 + 猛烈"的语义色彩
- 和 *orior* 相比，*coorior* 多了一层"共同/集中爆发"的强度；实际 Caesar 用法中常可互换，但 *coorior* 更具戏剧性
- 完成分词 *coortus* 在 abl. abs. 中极常见
