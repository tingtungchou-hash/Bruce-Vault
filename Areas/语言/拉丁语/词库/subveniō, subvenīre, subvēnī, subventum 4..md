# subveniō, subvenīre, subvēnī, subventum 4.

## 基本信息
- **语言**: 拉丁语
- **词性**: v. intr. (第四变位，接与格)
- **词源**: sub- (up to, under, to the aid of) + veniō (to come)。语义从军事预备队上前支援演变而来（参见 `subsidium`）。

## 释义

1. **To come up to one's assistance, to aid, assist, relieve, succor** (古典高频)
   a. 接与格（最常见）：*Lucānius circumventō fīliō subvenit* — "Lucanius came to the aid of his surrounded son" (Caesar BG 5.35)
   b. 独立使用：*circumvenior, iūdicēs, nisi subvenītis* — "I am being surrounded, judges, unless you come to my aid" (Cicero)
   c. 延伸义："to remedy, heal, cure" — 用于疾病、恶事的解决

2. **In gen., to come up, come** (罕见，字面义)
   a. *aliud in eō (sāle) mīrābile est, quod tantundem nocte subvenit, quantum diē auferās* — "another remarkable thing about salt is that as much comes up at night as you take away by day" (Pliny)
   b. "to come to mind, occur to"（晚期拉丁语）

## 复习提示
- **核心语法**：`subveniō` 接与格（不是宾格！）— `fīliō subvenit`（"help the son"），不是 `fīlium subvenit`
- 同义动词系列（均接与格）：`subveniō`、`opitulor`、`succurrō`、`auxilior`
- Caesar 军事叙事中的高频动词，理解包围与救援场景的关键
