# convincō, convincere, convīcī, convictus 3.

## 基本信息
- **语言**: 拉丁语
- **词性**: v. (第三变位法)
- **词源**: *com-*（加强）+ *vincō*（战胜）

## 释义

1. V. a., to overcome, conquer（战胜、制服；此义超出军事语域）
2. With personal objects, to convict of crime or error, refute（使某人被证实有罪或有错、驳倒；极常用）
   a. With simple acc.（直接宾格）
   b. With the designation of the crime, error, etc., commonly in the gen., more rarely in the simple abl., or with *dē*, *in*, or inf.（罪状一般用属格，间或用夺格，或 *dē*、*in* 加夺格、不定式）
3. With things as objects, to prove something incontestably (esp. as criminal, false, punishable), to show clearly, demonstrate（无可辩驳地证明某事有罪、虚妄或应罚；清楚显示）
   a. With acc.（直接宾格）
   b. With acc. and inf.（宾格加不定式）
