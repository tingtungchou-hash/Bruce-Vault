# adipīscor, adipīscī, adeptus sum 3. dep.

## 基本信息
- **语言**: 拉丁语
- **词性**: v. dep. (第三变位，异态动词)
- **词源**: *ad-* + *apīscor* (获取、达到); 与 *apis* (能力) 同根

## 释义

1. (字面义，少见) to arrive at, to reach, to overtake
   a. *vix adipīscendī potestās fuit* — there was scarcely opportunity for overtaking (Plaut. Ep. 1.1.13)
   b. *fugientēs Gallōs Macedonēs adeptī cecidērunt* — the Macedonians overtook and slew the fleeing Gauls (Liv. 44.28)

2. (比喻义，核心用法) to attain to by effort, to get, obtain, acquire
   a. *senectūtem ut adipīscantur, omnēs optant; eandem accūsant adeptī* — all wish to reach old age; having attained it, they complain of it (Cic. de Sen. 2.4)
   b. *hanc adeptī victōriam...cōnfīdēbant* — **本课用法**: having gained this victory, they were confident... (Caes. B.G. 5.39)
   c. *summōs honōrēs adipīscī* — to attain the highest honors (Cic. Clu. 43)
   d. *glōriam adipīscī* — to gain glory (Vulg. Eccli. 44.7)

> 辨析: *adipīscor* (通过努力克服自然障碍获得) vs. *impetrāre* (通过战胜他人意志获得) vs. *nancīscī* (偶然获得) — Doed. Syn. III. pp. 145-146

## 复习提示
- 异态动词，形式被动但意义主动
- 完成分词 *adeptus* (已获得的) 高频出现，本课即以此形式出现: *adeptī* (阳性复数主格) = "having gained"
- *adipīscor* + 宾格直接宾语 (无需介词)
