# profiteor, profitērī, professus sum 2.dep.

## 基本信息
- **语言**: 拉丁语
- **词性**: v. dep. (第二变位，异态动词)
- **词源**: *prō* + *fateor* (to confess, acknowledge)

## 释义

1. to declare publicly, acknowledge openly, avow, profess
   a. absol.: *fatērī atque etiam profitērī* — to admit and even declare openly
   b. with acc.: *hoc profitērī* — to declare this
   c. with acc. + inf.: *profitentur sē nullum perīculum recūsāre* — they declare that they refuse no danger (Caes. *B.G.* 7.2)

2. (in partic.) to offer oneself voluntarily, promise
   a. *sē adiūtōrem profitērī* — to offer oneself as an assistant (see Caes. *B.G.* 5.38 — **本课用法**)
   b. *sē aliquid profitērī* — to volunteer for something
   c. *quis profitētur?* — who volunteers?

3. to profess to be something, declare oneself as
   a. *sē grammaticum profitērī* — to declare oneself a grammarian
   b. *philosophiam profitērī* — to profess philosophy, be a philosopher

4. to make a public return/registration: *nōmen profitērī* — to give in one's name (as candidate); *cēnsum profitērī* — to declare one's property

## 复习提示
**高优先级** — 异态动词，形式被动意义主动。今天翻译错误 #5：把 *profitētur* 误译为被动 "it is professed"。记住：*-tur* 结尾 ≠ 被动，异态动词是形式被动、意义主动。
