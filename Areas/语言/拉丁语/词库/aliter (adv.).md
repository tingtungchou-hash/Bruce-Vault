# aliter (adv.)

## 基本信息
- **语言**: 拉丁语
- **词性**: adv. (不变词)
- **词源**: from *alius* ("other") + *-ter* (adverbial suffix)

## 释义
1. Otherwise, in another manner, differently
   a. *aliter ac* (or *aliter atque*) = "otherwise than, differently from"
      - *coāctus est aliter ac superiōribus annīs* = "he was forced otherwise than in previous years"
   b. *aliter ... aliter* = "in one way ... in another"
2. *aliter ... ac (atque) ... aliter* = "differently ... than ... differently" (double contrast)

## 复习提示
- 本课出现: `aliter ac superiōribus annīs` (BG V.24.1)
- *aliter ac* 是固定搭配，不能拆开理解
- 与英语 "otherwise than" 用法一致
