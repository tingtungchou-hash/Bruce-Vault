# turbō, turbāre, turbāvī, turbātus 1.

## 基本信息
- **语言**: 拉丁语
- **词性**: v. trans. (第一变位)
- **词源**: 源自 *turba* (crowd, confusion, disorder); 字面义为 "使陷入混乱/骚动"

## 释义

1. Lit.: to disturb, agitate, throw into disorder or confusion
   a. *ventōrum vī agitārī atque turbārī mare* — 海被风力搅动翻腾
   b. *aequora ventīs turbāre* — 用风搅乱海面
   c. *comās turbāre* — 弄乱头发
   d. *alvum turbāre* — 扰乱肠胃 (Plin.)

2. Milit. t.t.: to throw into disorder, break the line of battle, disorganize
   a. *equitātus turbāverat ōrdinēs* — 骑兵已打乱了阵线 (Liv.)
   b. *aciem peditum turbāre* — 打乱步兵阵线

3. of water: to trouble, make thick or turbid
   a. *lacūs turbāre* — 搅浑湖水
   b. *flūmen imbre turbāre* — 雨水搅浑河流
   c. *aquam līmō turbāre* — 用泥把水搅浑 (Hor.)

4. Trop.: to disturb, agitate, confuse, throw into disorder (of political/social order)
   a. *omnia īnfima summīs paria fēcit, turbāvit, miscuit* — 他把一切都颠倒、扰乱、混淆了 (Cic.)
   b. *nē quid ille turbet vidē* — 注意别让他捣乱 (Cic.)
   c. *in rē pūblicā turbāre* — 扰乱国政
   d. *hostēs inopīnātō malō turbātī* — 敌人被突如其来的灾难打乱阵脚 (Caes. *B.C.* 2.12)

5. (impers. pass.) *turbātum est* — there is disturbance/confusion
   a. *nesciō quid absente nōbīs turbātum'st domī* — 我们不在家时不知家里出了什么乱子 (Ter.)

## 复习提示
- *turbō* 是整个复合词家族的核心，掌握它 = 一次性解锁 *dēturbō* (打下去)、*perturbō* (彻底扰乱)、*conturbō* (全面扰乱)
- 本课出现的 *dēturbātī* 直接来自 *dē-* (向下) + *turbō* (扰乱)
- 与 *turba* (人群/混乱) 同根记忆：人群 (= 混乱来源) → 使陷入混乱
