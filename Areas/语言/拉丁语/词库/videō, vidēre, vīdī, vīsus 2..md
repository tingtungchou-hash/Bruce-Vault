# videō, vidēre, vīdī, vīsus 2.

## 基本信息
- **语言**: 拉丁语
- **词性**: v. a. (第二变位)
- **词源**: 对应希腊语 ϝιδεῖν (ἰδεῖν), 梵语 vid, 英语 wit/wisdom

## 释义

1. Inf. viderier, Cic. Dom. 53, 136; Ter. Hec. 5, 1, 33: vidĕ,Plaut. Ps. 1, 1, 46),  and n. Sanscr. root vid-, vēda, know; vindāmi, find; cf. vēda-s, sacred book; Gr. root ἰδ, ϝιδ-, in εἶδον, saw; οἶδα, know; Germ. wissen; Engl. wit, wot, to see, perceive, with the eyes (syn. cerno).

2. Lit.
