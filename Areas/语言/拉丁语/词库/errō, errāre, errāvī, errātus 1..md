# errō, errāre, errāvī, errātus 1.

## 基本信息
- **语言**: 拉丁语
- **词性**: v. intr. (第一变位法)
- **词源**: 源自词根 *er-（走）；同源词：希腊语 ἔρχομαι（去）、德语 irren（迷路）、英语 err

## 释义
**不及物**
1. To wander, stray about, rove, wander up and down
   a. （转义，用于无生命事物）of planets, stars: to move erratically; of rivers: to meander; of hair: to fly about
   b. （比喻）to wander at random, stray in thought: *errāns et vaga sententia* = "a wandering and aimless thought"
2. To miss the right way, go astray, lose one's way
   a. （字面，罕见）*errāre viā* = "to lose one's way"
   b. **（比喻，极其常见）to wander from the truth, err, be mistaken**
     - 经典搭配：*errāre eōs dīcunt* (Caes. *BG* 5.41.5) = "they say that they are mistaken"
     - 与 *sī* 从句连用：*errās sī id crēdis* = "you are wrong if you believe that"
     - 程度修饰：*tōtā viā errāre*（完全错了）、*longē errāre*（大错特错）、*vehementer errāre*（严重错误）
     - 无人称被动：*sī errātur in nōmine* = "if a mistake is made in the name"

**及物**（仅见于奥古斯都时期诗歌的完成分词）
- To wander over or through: *errāta lītora* = "shores wandered over"

## 复习提示
- 核心区分：字面义"漫游" vs. 比喻义"犯错" — 在 oratio obliqua（间接引语）中几乎总是"犯错"
- 本课出现：BG 5.41.5 *errāre eōs dīcunt* — "they say that they are mistaken"
- 常见错误：只记住"漫游"而遗漏"犯错"义项，导致 oratio obliqua 段落完全读反
