# tollō, tollere, sustulī, sublātus 3.

## 基本信息
- **语言**: 拉丁语
- **词性**: v. a. (第三变位)
- **词源**: Skt. *tul-*, *tulajāmi* (lift up, weigh); Gk. *τλῆναι* (to bear), *τάλαντον* (talent/weight); cf. Lat. *tulī* (ferō的完成时), *lātus* (< *tlātus*), *tolerāre*

## 释义

1. to lift up, raise up, elevate, exalt (主导含义: 向上运动)
   a. *tollere aliquid in caelum* — 将某物/某人捧上天
   b. *tollere manūs* — 举起双手
   c. *tollere līberōs* — 举起新生儿 (表示父亲认可/收养); 养育子女
   d. *tollere ancorās* — 起锚; *sublātīs ancorīs* — 起锚后 (独立夺格)

2. to take away, remove, carry off, make away with
   a. *tollere aliquid dē locō* — 从某处拿走某物
   b. *tollere aliquem dē mediō* — 杀掉某人
   c. *posita tollere* — 拿走献祭之物 (见 Caes. *B.G.* 6.17 — **本课用法**)

3. (引申) to do away with, remove; to abolish, annul, cancel
   a. *tollere lēgem* — 废除法律
   b. *tollere dubitātiōnem* — 消除疑虑
   c. *tollere deōs* — 否定神的存在
