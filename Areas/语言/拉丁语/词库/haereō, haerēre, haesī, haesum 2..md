# haereō, haerēre, haesī, haesum 2.

## 基本信息
- **语言**: 拉丁语
- **词性**: v. (第二变位法)
- **词源**: 词源不明（L&S：etym. dub.）

## 释义

1. to hang or hold fast, to hang, stick, cleave, cling, adhere, be fixed（粘着、附着、紧贴）
   a. constr. with *in*, the simple abl., absol., less freq. dat., *ad*, *sub*, *ex*
   b. prov. *in lūtō haerēre*（陷在泥里，即身处困境）
2. to hold fast, remain attached or fixed, keep firm, adhere（固定、坚持）
   a. *in memoriā / in medullīs haerēre*（铭刻于记忆/骨髓）
   b. with the idea of duration, to remain fixed, abide, continue（留驻、持续）
3. to stick fast, be brought to a stand-still, be embarrassed, perplexed, at a loss, hesitate（卡住、困惑、迟疑）
