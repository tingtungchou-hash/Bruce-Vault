# collocō, collocāre, collocāvī, collocātus 1.

## 基本信息
- **语言**: 拉丁语
- **词性**: v. trans. (第一变位法)
- **词源**: *con-* + *locō* ("to place together")

## 释义
1. To place together, set up, station, arrange, dispose
   a. With *in* + abl.: *exercitum in hibernis collocare* = to station the army in winter quarters
   b. With *in* + acc.: to place into
   c. With other prepositions: to lodge, quarter; to lay an ambush
   d. Absol.: to set in order, arrange
2. Special uses
   a. To give in marriage: *filiam alicui collocare*
   b. (mercantile) To invest money: *pecuniam collocare* = to lay out, invest
3. Trop.: to place, set, employ, occupy
   a. *se in otio collocare* = to settle into retirement
   b. *spem in aliquo collocare* = to place hope in someone
