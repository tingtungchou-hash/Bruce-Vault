# peragō, peragere, perēgī, perāctus 3.

## 基本信息
- **语言**: 拉丁语
- **词性**: v. trans. (第三变位法)
- **词源**: *per-* + *agō* ("to drive through" > "to carry through")

## 释义
1. To thrust through, pierce through, transfix (poetic)
2. To pass through, traverse
3. To carry through, go through with, execute, finish, accomplish, complete
   a. *concilium peragere* = to complete a council/meeting
   b. *fabulam peragere* = to finish a play
   c. *comitia peragere* = to complete elections
   d. *vitam peragere* = to finish life / pass one's life
4. (jurid.) To prosecute to the end: *reum peragere* = to secure conviction
5. To work or work up
   a. *humum peragere* = to till the soil
   b. *cibum peragere* = to digest food
6. To go through, relate, describe, detail

## 复习提示
- 本课出现: *conciliō ... perāctō* = "the council having been completed" (BG V.24.1, abl. abs.)
- *per-* 前缀表示 "完全、贯穿"，与 *agō* 结合 = "从头做到尾"即"完成"
- 独立夺格中的 PPP: *perāctō conciliō* = "after the council was completed"
