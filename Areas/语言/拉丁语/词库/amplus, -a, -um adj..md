# amplus, -a, -um adj.

## 基本信息
- **语言**: 拉丁语
- **词性**: adj. (第一/第二变格)
- **词源**: 来自 [[Resources/lewis-short-json-master/ls_A.json|Lewis & Short]]: 或与 *am-* + *-plus* (如 *-pleo* "充满"、*plenus* "满的") 同族，核心义为"充盈的、圆满的"

## 释义
1. large, great, wide, spacious (空间)
2. abundant, ample, much (数量)
	1. ut quisque est genere copiīsque amplissimus [[collegecaesar.14jan25.pdf#page=50&selection=50,4,52,16|Caesar]]
3. strong, violent (力量)
4. magnificent, splendid, glorious (外观)
5. esteemed, renowned (声望)

## 关联词汇
- [[amplitūdō, -inis f.]] — greatness, extent
- [[amplificō, -āre]] — to enlarge, magnify
