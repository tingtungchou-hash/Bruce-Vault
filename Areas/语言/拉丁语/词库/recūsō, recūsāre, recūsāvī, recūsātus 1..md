# recūsō, recūsāre, recūsāvī, recūsātus 1.

## 基本信息
- **语言**: 拉丁语
- **词性**: v. trans. (第一变位法)
- **词源**: *re-*（回、反对）+ *causa*（理由、案件）→ "提出反对理由" → "拒绝"

## 释义
1. To make an objection against, decline, reject, refuse, be reluctant or unwilling
   a. With acc.: *uxōrem recūsāre* = "to reject a wife"; *nihil nisi hīberna recūsāre* (Caes. *BG* 5.41.5) = "to refuse nothing except winter quarters"
     - *perīculum recūsāre* = "to refuse a danger / shrink from danger"
     - *amīcitiam recūsāre* = "to reject friendship"
   b. With inf. (class. prose only in negative sentences or questions implying a negative): *morī recūsāre* (Caes. *BG* 3.22) = "to refuse to die"
   c. 与 *nē*：*recūsāre nē ... cōnstituerētur* = "to object that ... be not established"
   d. 与 *quīn*：*nōn recūsō quīn* = "I do not object that / refuse that"
   e. 与 *quōminus*：*nec recūsāvit quō minus poenam subīret* = "nor did he refuse to undergo the penalty"
2. 法律用语 — To protest against a complaint; to object, take exception, plead in defence
   a. *numquid recūsās contrā mē?* (Plaut.) = "do you raise any objection against me?"
3. 晚期拉丁语 — To refute, disprove: *nātīvitātis mendācium recūsāre* (Tert.) = "to refute the falsehood of the nativity"

## 复习提示
- 本课出现：BG 5.41.5 *ut nihil nisi hīberna recūsent atque hanc inveterāscere cōnsuētūdinem nōlint*
  — "that they refuse nothing except the winter quarters and do not wish this practice to become established"
- 核心区分：*recūsō* = 明确反对/拒绝（有理由地） vs. *nōlō* = 不愿意/不想要（情感/意愿） — 本课中二词并列，恰好形成对比
- 搭配总结：*recūsō* + acc.（拒某物）、*recūsō* + nē/quīn/quōminus（拒某行为）
