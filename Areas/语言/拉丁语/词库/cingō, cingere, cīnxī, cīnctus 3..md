# cingō, cingere, cīnxī, cīnctus 3.

## 基本信息
- **语言**: 拉丁语
- **词性**: v. trans. (第三变位法，-ō 型)
- **词源**: 同源词：希腊语 κυλλός（弯曲的）、κυρτός（凸的）；拉丁语 curvus（弯曲的）

## 释义
**字面 — 环绕、包围**
1. To go round, surround, encompass, gird, wreathe
   a. *flūmen Dubis paene tōtum oppidum cingit* (Caes.) — "the river Doubs almost entirely surrounds the town"
   b. *prōvincia marī cīncta* (Cic.) — "a province surrounded by the sea"
2. To gird on, belt (body or weapon)
   a. *Hispānō cingitur gladiō* (Liv.) — "he is girded with a Spanish sword"
   b. 束紧衣袍（准备行动）：*cingitur ipse furēns ... in proelia* (Verg.) — "he himself girds up in frenzy for battle"
3. To encircle with a garland, crown
   a. *tempora flōribus cingere* (Hor.) — "to wreathe the temples with flowers"

**军事用语 — 极其高频**
4. To surround, invest, beleaguer, besiege
   a. **本课用法**：*Nerviī vallō pedum IX et fossā pedum XV hīberna cingunt* (Caes. *BG* 5.42.1) — "the Nervii surround the winter quarters with a rampart of 9 feet and a ditch of 15 feet"
   b. *urbem obsidiōne cingere* (Verg.) — "to surround the city with a siege"
   c. *castra vallō cingere* (Liv.) — "to surround the camp with a rampart"
   d. *corōnā mīlitum cīncta urbs* (Liv.) — "the city encircled by a ring of soldiers"

## 复习提示
- 本课出现：BG 5.42.1 *vallō pedum IX et fossā pedum XV hīberna cingunt* — "they surround the winter quarters with a rampart of 9 feet and a ditch of 15 feet"
- 常见误读：*vallō/fossā* 是工具夺格（"用什么来围"），不是距离
- 军事语境中 *cingō* 几乎总是"围困/围攻"义，不是泛泛的"环绕"
