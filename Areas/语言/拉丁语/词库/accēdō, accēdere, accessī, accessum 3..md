# accēdō, accēdere, accessī, accessum 3.

## 基本信息
- **语言**: 拉丁语
- **词性**: v. (第三变位法)
- **词源**: *ad-*（向）+ *cēdō*（行进）

## 释义

1. to go or come to or near, to approach（走近、接近）
   a. constr. with *ad* / *in*, the local adverbs, the acc., dat., infin., or absol.
2. to approach a thing in a hostile manner, to attack（进攻）
3. to come to or upon one, to happen to, befall; with the accessory idea of increase, to be added（降临于；增添）
4. to give assent to, accede to, agree with, approve of（赞同、应允）
5. to come near to in resemblance, to resemble, be like（近似、接近）
6. to enter upon, to undertake（着手、承担，如 *ad causam*、*ad rem publicam*）
