# cieō, ciēre, cīvī, citus 2.

## 基本信息
- **语言**: 拉丁语
- **词性**: v. trans. (第二变位法；古体 *ciō, cīre* 留存于复合词 *acciō*, *exciō* 等)
- **词源**: 同源词：希腊语 κίω（去、走）→ 使动化（causative），类似于 κινέω（使动）之于 κίω

## 释义
**字面 — 使动**
1. To put in motion, move, stir, shake
   a. *nātūra omnia ciēns et agitāns* (Cic.) — "nature setting everything in motion and driving it"
   b. *rēmōs ciēre* (Stat.) — "to ply the oars"
   c. *aequora ciēre* (Verg.) — "to stir up the sea"
   d. 法律用语：*ciēre erctum* — "to divide an inheritance"（"使遗产动起来"→ 分割）

**方向性 — 召唤、唤起**
2. To move, excite, or call to / toward
   a. *ad arma ciēre* (Liv.) — "to call to arms"
   b. *in pugnam ciēre* (Sil.) — "to rouse to battle"
   c. *aere ciēre virōs* (Verg.) — "to summon men with the trumpet"
3. To call upon for help, invoke (gods, spirits)
   a. *nocturnōs mānēs ciēre* (Verg.) — "to invoke the spirits of the night"
   b. *deōs ciēre* (Liv.) — "to call upon the gods"
4. To mention by name, name, call out
   a. *singulōs nōmine ciēre* (Tac.) — "to name each one individually"
   b. *patrem ciēre* (Liv.) — "to name one's father"（法律术语：证明自由出身）

**比喻 — 激起、引发**
5. To excite, rouse, stimulate, produce, cause, begin
   a. *bellum ciēre* — "to stir up war"
   b. *sēditiōnēs ciēre* (Liv.) — "to stir up sedition"
   c. *pugnam / proelium ciēre* — "to begin battle"
   d. *lacrimās ciēre* (Verg.) — "to cause tears"
   e. *gemitūs / flētūs ciēre* — "to provoke groans/weeping"
   f. 医学用语：*alvum ciēre* (Plin.) — "to cause evacuation"; *ūrīnam ciēre* — "to promote urination"

## 复习提示
- 核心逻辑链：*cieō*（使动）→ *citus*（被驱动的 → 快的）→ *citō*（快速地）/ *concitō*（使快速 → 煽动）
- *cieō* 是"行为词根"——几乎所有的"召唤""刺激""引用"义都源自"使...动起来"的意象
- 与 *moveō* 区分：*cieō* 更强地暗示"从静止到运动"的启动瞬间；*moveō* 更通用
- 第二变位法中的 *-eō* 常与使动语义关联（cf. *moneō*, *doceō*, *torreō*）
