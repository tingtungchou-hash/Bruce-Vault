# impetrō, impetrāre, impetrāvī, impetrātus 1.

## 基本信息
- **语言**: 拉丁语
- **词性**: v. trans. (第一变位法)
- **词源**: *in-* + *patrō*（完成、达成、执行）→ "to bring to completion" → "to obtain by effort or request"

## 释义
1. To accomplish, effect, bring to pass; to get, obtain, procure (esp. by exertion, request, entreaty)
   a. With acc.: *cīvitātem ā Caesare impetrāre* (Cic.) — "to obtain citizenship from Caesar"
     - *quod voluī, impetrāvī* (Plaut.) — "what I wanted, I have obtained"
     - *quae petierint, impetrātūrōs* (Caes. *BG* 5.41.8) — "[he hoped] they would obtain what they had requested"
   b. With *ut* / *nē* / simple subj.: *ā Sēquanīs impetrat ut ... patiantur* (Caes.) — "he obtains from the Sequani that they allow..."
     - *impetrāvī ut nē quid succēnseat* (Plaut.) — "I have obtained that he not be angry"
     - *tandem impetrāvī abīret* (Plaut.) — "at last I got him to go away"
   c. 绝对用法：*facile impetrās* = "you easily get your request" = "I'll do as you wish"
   d. Pass. impers.: *impetrārī nōn potuit* (Caes.) — "it could not be obtained"

## 关联词汇
- [[patrō, patrāre, patrāvī, patrātus 1.]] — to accomplish, perform（词根）
- [[petō, petere, petīvī, petītus 3.]] — to seek, request（*petō* 提出请求 → *impetrō* 请求获批）

## 复习提示
- 本课出现：BG 5.41.8 *spērāre prō eius iūstitiā, quae petierint, impetrātūrōs* — "[he replied that] he hoped, in accordance with his justice, that they would obtain what they had requested"
- 核心区分：*petō* = to ask for / seek → *impetrō* = to get what one asked for（"请求获批"）。这是理解外交语境的关键区分。
- 将来时分词 *impetrātūrōs* = "about to obtain / would obtain" — 或 obl. 中的将来性必须翻译出来
