# īnferō, īnferre, intulī, illātus irreg.

## 基本信息
- **语言**: 拉丁语
- **词性**: v. irreg. (复合动词，第三变位混合)
- **词源**: in "入、向" + ferō "携带"；见 [[Resources/lewis-short-json-master/ls_I.json|Lewis & Short]]

## 释义
1. to bring in, carry in, introduce (带入)
2. to inflict, cause (施加，尤指伤害)
	1. utī aut ipsī iniūriās inferrent aut illātās prōpulsārent [[collegecaesar.14jan25.pdf#page=50&selection=19,5,38,4|Caesar]]
3. to throw upon, hurl at (投向)
4. to conclude, infer (推理)
5. to bury, inter (埋葬，与 sepeliō 相关)
