# obsideō, obsidēre, obsēdī, obsessus 2.

## 基本信息
- **语言**: 拉丁语
- **词性**: v. (第二变位)
- **词源**: *ob-* (在……前面/对面) + *sedeō* (坐); 字面义"坐在……对面"

## 释义

1. to sit, stay, remain, abide (仅诗歌)
   a. *domī obsidēre* — to remain at home (Ter. Ad. 4.6.6)

2. to sit at/on/in; to haunt, inhabit, frequent a place
   a. *rānae stāgna obsident* — frogs frequent the marshes (Plin. 11.18.19)

3. (军事术语，核心义) to sit down before, to beset, besiege, invest, blockade
   a. *Curiō Uticam obsidēre īnstituit* — Curio began to besiege Utica (Caes. B.C. 2.36)
   b. *viās obsidēre* — to blockade the roads (Caes. B.G. 3.23)
   c. *obsessīs omnibus viīs missī intercipiuntur* — **本课用法**: all roads having been blockaded, the messengers sent are intercepted (Caes. B.G. 5.40)

4. to occupy, fill, possess
   a. *senātum armīs obsidēre* — to occupy the senate with armed men (Cic. Phil. 7.5.15)

5. to watch closely, be on the look-out for
   a. *ad obsidendum stuprum* — to lie in wait for a violation (Cic. Cat. 1.10.26)

> 辨析: *obsideō* (持续包围、封锁) vs. *oppugnō* (主动进攻/突击) ——本课两句连用: *castra oppugnant, fossam complent* (XL ③)

## 复习提示
- 本课出现的是完成分词 *obsessus* 的夺格形式 *obsessīs* (= "having been blockaded")
- *obsessīs omnibus viīs* 是独立夺格 (ablative absolute): "所有道路被封锁之后/在道路被封锁的情况下"
- *viīs* = via 的夺格复数 ("道路")，不要与 *viribus* (力量) 混淆
