# versor, versārī, versātus sum 1.dep.

## 基本信息
- **语言**: 拉丁语
- **词性**: v. 1.dep.
- **词源**: *vertō* 的反复体/中动态，见 [[Resources/lewis-short-json-master/ls_V.json|Lewis & Short]]: 为 *versō* (反复转动) 的被动/中动态，核心义为"反复转动、四处移动"

## 释义
1. to move about, turn round (物理：来回移动)
	1. omnēs in bellō versantur [[collegecaesar.14jan25.pdf#page=50&selection=38,7,44,1|collegecaesar.14jan25, 页面 50]]
2. to be situated, be located (位于，某处)
3. to be engaged in, be occupied with (从事于)
4. to live, dwell, abide (居住、生活)
5. to be (存在，系词用法)

## 关联词汇
- [[vertō, vertere, vertī, versus 3]] — to turn (词根)
- [[versō, versāre, versāvī, versātus 1]] — to keep turning, twist (反复体)
- [[versus, -ūs m.]] — verse, line (字面义"转动" -> "一行")
- [[adversor, adversārī, adversātus sum 1.dep.]] — to oppose

## 本课用法

- BG 5.45: `Gallus inter Gallōs sine ūllā suspiciōne versātus ad Caesarem pervenit` — "作为一个高卢人，在高卢人中间活动/穿行，没有引起任何怀疑，到达了 Caesar 那里"
- `versātus` 为完成时分词（deponent），修饰主语 `ille` (奴隶)，表示"在活动中 / 往来穿梭之后"
- 本课强调了 `versor` 的"混迹/穿梭"义：`inter + 宾格` 表示所处的群体环境，`sine ūllā suspiciōne` 为伴随状语

## 复习提示
- *versor in bellō* — to take part in a war
