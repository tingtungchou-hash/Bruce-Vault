# observō, observāre, observāvī, observātus 1.

## 基本信息
- **语言**: 拉丁语
- **词性**: v. a. (第一变位)
- **词源**: *ob-* + *servō* (to watch, keep)

## 释义

1. (一般) to watch, note, heed, observe; to take notice of, pay attention to
   a. *nē mē observāre possīs, quid rērum geram* — 免得你看出我在做什么
   b. *tempus epistulae alicui reddendae observāre* — 找准送信的时机

2. (具体义项)
   a. to watch, guard, keep: *iānuam observāre* — 看守大门
   b. to observe, respect, keep, comply with (法律、规则): *lēgēs observāre* — 遵守法律; *praeceptum observāre* — 遵守指示
   c. to pay respect to, esteem, honor: *aliquem ut alterum patrem observāre* — 像尊敬另一位父亲那样尊敬某人
   d. to observe/celebrate (日期): *diēs nātālēs ... observant* — 他们庆祝/遵循生日 (见 Caes. *B.G.* 6.18 — **本课用法**)
