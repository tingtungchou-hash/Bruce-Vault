# prōdō, prōdere, prōdidī, prōditus 3.

## 基本信息
- **语言**: 拉丁语
- **词性**: v. a. (第三变位)
- **词源**: *prō-* + *dō* (to give); 古体 *prōdit* (= *prōdiderit*), 字面义为 "向前给出" → "公之于众"

## 释义

1. to give, put, or bring forth; to show
   a. *prōdere suspīria pectore* — 从胸中发出叹息 (诗歌用法)

2. to put forth in writing, to publish, make known, relate, report, record
   a. *prōdere memoriā* — 记录、传述后世
   b. *ut prōdunt* — 如他们所说/据记载
   c. *idque ab Druidibus prōditum dīcunt* — 他们声称这是由德鲁伊传下来的 (见 Caes. *B.G.* 6.18 — **本课用法**)

3. to hand down, transmit, bequeath
   a. *sacra posterīs prōdere* — 将祭祀传统传给后代
   b. *rēgnum ā Tantalō prōditum* — 自坦塔罗斯传下的王权

4. to betray perfidiously, surrender treacherously
   a. *prōdere patriam* — 背叛祖国
   b. *prōdere rempūblicam hostī* — 将国家出卖给敌人
