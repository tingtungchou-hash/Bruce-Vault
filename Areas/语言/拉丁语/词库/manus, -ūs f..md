# manus, -ūs f.

## 基本信息
- **语言**: 拉丁语
- **词性**: n. f. (第四变格法)
- **词源**: 词根 *man-/ma-* (to measure); 梵语 *ma* (measure, moon); 古高地德语 *mund* (hand)

## 释义

1. **A hand**
   a. *manus dextra / sinistra* — right / left hand
   b. *manum dare* — to give the hand; to surrender

2. **A hand, handwriting; work, workmanship**
   a. *librarii manus* — the scribe's hand (Cic.)
   b. *Praxitelis manus* — the work of Praxiteles (Mart.)

3. **Force, violence, fighting, close combat** 
   a. *res venit ad manus atque ad pugnam* — the matter came to blows and fighting (Cic.)
   b. *in manus venire* — to come to close quarters (Sall.)

4. **An armed force, corps of soldiers, band of men** (military)
   a. *magnam manum conducere* — to gather a large band (Caes. B.G. 5.27)
   b. *magna manu ad castra oppugnatum venerunt* — they came with a large band to attack the camp (Caes. B.G. 5.26)

5. **Power, control**
   a. *in tua manu est* — it is in your power
   b. *haec non sunt in nostra manu* — these things are not in our power (Cic.)

6. **The trunk of an elephant** (Cic. N.D. 2.47)

7. **In milit. lang.: *ferreae manus*** — grappling-irons (iron hooks for boarding enemy ships)

## 复习提示
BG 5.26: *magna manu* — 此处 `manus` = "band/force" (武装团伙), 不是 "hand"。Caesar 常说 *manus* 来指代军队中不成建制的分队或临时的武装力量，区别于 *legio* (军团) 和 *exercitus* (全军)。
