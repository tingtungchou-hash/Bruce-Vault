# intermittō, intermittere, intermīsī, intermissus 3.

## 基本信息
- **语言**: 拉丁语
- **词性**: v. trans. / intr. (第三变位法，-ō 型)
- **词源**: *inter-*（在...之间）+ *mittō*（送、放）→ "放到间隔中去" → "中断、留出空隙"

## 释义

**及物**
1. To leave off, intermit, omit, neglect, suspend
   a. *proelium intermittere* — to suspend battle; *opus intermittere* — to leave off work
   b. With *ab* + abl.: *tempus ab opere intermittere* (Caes. *BG* 7.24.2) — to leave time free from work
   c. **With *ad* + acc.**（本课关键结构）: *nūlla pars nocturnī temporis ad labōrem intermittitur* (Caes. *BG* 5.40.5) — "no part of the nighttime is left unused for work"
   d. With inf.: *nōn intermittit mittere litterās* — he does not cease to send letters

2. To let pass, suffer to elapse
   a. *diem intermittere* — to let a day go by
   b. *neque noctem neque diem intermittit* (Caes. *BG* 5.38) — he let neither night nor day pass

**不及物**
3. To leave off, cease, pause
   a. *febris intermittit* — the fever is intermittent
   b. *avēs intermittentēs bibunt* (Plin.) — birds drink with pauses

**完成分词 intermissus, -a, -um**
4. Interrupted, left between, separated by gaps
   a. *brevī tempore intermissō* — after a short interval
   b. *intermissīs circiter passibus quadringentīs* — at intervals of about 400 paces
   c. *intermissa pars oppidī* (Caes. *BG* 7.17) — the unenclosed part of the town

## 复习提示
- 本课出现：BG 5.40.5 *nūlla pars nocturnī temporis ad labōrem intermittitur* — "not a single part of the night is left unused for work"（每一分每一秒都在干活）
- 结构要点：*intermittō* + *ad* + acc. = 不中断 → 不浪费 — Caesar 常用此结构描述持续紧张的军事行动
