# intermittō, intermittere, intermīsī, intermissus 3.

## 基本信息
- **语言**: 拉丁语
- **词性**: v. (第三变位，及物/不及物)
- **词源**: *inter* + *mittō* (to send between → to leave a gap)

## 释义

1. (act.) to leave off, discontinue, interrupt, omit, neglect
   a. *iter intermittere* — to interrupt the march; *proelium intermittere* — to break off battle
   b. *opus intermittere* — to suspend work; *studia intermittere* — to neglect one's studies
   c. with inf.: *nōn intermittit mittere litterās* — he does not cease to send letters

2. to let pass, suffer to elapse (time)
   a. *neque noctem neque diem intermittit* — he let neither night nor day pass (Caes. *B.G.* 5.38 — **本课用法**)
   b. *diem intermittere* — to let a day go by; *tempus ab opere intermittere* — to let time pass away from work

3. (neutr.) to leave off, cease, pause
   a. *febris intermittit* — the fever is intermittent
   b. *nihil intermittere* — to pause at nothing

4. (P. a.) *intermissus, -a, -um* — interrupted, discontinued, left between
   a. *brevī tempore intermissō* — after a short interval; *intermissīs passibus* — at intervals

## 复习提示
*neque noctem neque diem intermittit* — 注意 *intermittō* 非 "waste"（浪费），而是 "let pass/interrupt"。Caesar 强调 Ambiorix 毫不停歇，夜以继日行军。
