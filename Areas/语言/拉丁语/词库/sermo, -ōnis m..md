# sermo, -ōnis m.

## 基本信息
- **语言**: 拉丁语
- **词性**: n. m. (第三变格法)
- **词源**: from *serō* (to join, connect); qs. *serta, cōnserta ōrātiō* — joined/connected speech

## 释义

1. conversation, talk, discourse
   a. *sermōnem cum aliquō cōnferre* — to enter into conversation with someone
   b. *sermōnem habēre* — to hold a conversation
   c. *longior cōnsultō ab Ambiorīge īnstituitur sermō* — a longer conversation is deliberately set up by Ambiorix (Caes. *B.G.* 5.37 — **本课用法**)

2. common talk, report, rumor
   a. *in sermōne omnium esse* — to be on everyone's lips; *sermōnēs hominum* — public talk, gossip

3. (literary) discussion, disputation, dialogue
   a. *sermō dē amīcitiā* — a discourse on friendship; *Sōcraticī sermōnēs* — Socratic dialogues

4. ordinary speech, conversational language (opp. *contentiō* — formal oratory)
   a. *sermō pūrus* — pure, natural speech; *sermōnī proprior* — closer to everyday speech

5. language, tongue, dialect
   a. *sermō Latīnus* — the Latin language; *patrius sermō* — native tongue

## 复习提示
注意与 "sermon"（英语 "布道"）的语义演变：拉丁语为日常谈话 → 中世纪拉丁语发展为宗教讲道。CG 中多取 "conversation / discussion" 义。
