# dēfīgō, dēfīgere, dēfīxī, dēfīxus 3.

## 基本信息
- **语言**: 拉丁语
- **词性**: v. trans. (第三变位法)
- **词源**: *dē-* + *fīgō* ("to fasten down, fix into")

## 释义
1. To fasten down or in; to drive, fix, or fasten into
   a. *verūtum in balteō dēfīgere* = to have a javelin lodged in one's belt (BG V.44.7)
   b. *crucem dēfīgere* = to set up a cross
   c. *sīcam in corpore dēfīgere* = to thrust a dagger into the body
2. To fix, fasten, render immovable
   a. *dēfīxa caelō sīdera* = stars fixed in the sky
   b. *dēfīxus sedēre* = to sit motionless
3. Trop.:
   a. To fix intently (eyes, attention): *oculōs in aliquem dēfīgere*
   b. To astound, stupefy: *dēfīxus stupēre* = to be rooted to the spot in amazement
   c. To bewitch, curse (*dēfīxĭō* = curse tablet)

## 关联词汇

- [[trānsfīgō, trānsfīgere, trānsfīxī, trānsfīxus 3.]] — to pierce through (BG V.44.7: *trānsfīgitur scūtum*)
- [[cōnfīgō, cōnfīgere, cōnfīxī, cōnfīxus 3.]] — to fasten together; pierce through

## 复习提示
- 本课出现: `verūtum in balteō dēfīgitur` — "a javelin lodges in (his) belt" (BG V.44.7)
- ⑦ 中 *trānsfīgitur* 和 *dēfīgitur* 并置：盾被刺穿 + 标枪卡在腰带上，两件倒霉事接连发生
- 注意与英语 "defix/defigure" 无关 — 不要被假朋友误导
