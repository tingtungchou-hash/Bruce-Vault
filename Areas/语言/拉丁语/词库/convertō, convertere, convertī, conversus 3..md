# convertō, convertere, convertī, conversus 3.

## 基本信息
- **语言**: 拉丁语
- **词性**: v. trans./intrans. (第三变位法)
- **词源**: *con-* + *vertō* ("to turn together, turn round")

## 释义
1. To turn round, wheel about, cause to turn
   a. *sē convertere* = to turn oneself (BG V.44.10: *sē ... convertit*)
   b. *omnium ōra in mē conversa sunt* = all faces turned toward me
   c. Milit.: *signa convertere* = to wheel the standards about, change direction
2. To turn attention, direct toward
   a. *ad hunc sē omnis multitūdō convertit* = the whole crowd turned to this man (BG V.44.10)
   b. *animum ad aliquid convertere* = to turn one's mind to something
3. To change, alter, transform
   a. *aliquid in aliquid convertere* = to change something into something
   b. *sē ad philosophōs convertere* = to turn to philosophy
4. To translate (a text from one language to another)

## 关联词汇
- [[vertō, vertere, vertī, versus 3.]] — to turn
- [[āvertō, āvertere, āvertī, āversus 3.]] — to turn away (BG V.44.8: *āvertit ... vāgīnam*)
- [[revertō, revertere, revertī, reversus 3.]] — to turn back, return
- [[versō, versāre, versāvī, versātus 1.]] — to turn often, toss (BG V.44.14: *versāvit*)

## 复习提示
- 本课出现: *ad hunc sē ... omnis multitūdō convertit* — "the whole crowd turned to him" (BG V.44.10)
- *sē convertere* 中的 *sē* 是反身代词: 人群"把自己转过去"，不是"把别人转过去"
- ⑧ *āvertit* (turn away) 与 ⑩ *convertit* (turn toward) 形成方向对比
