# prō (prep. + abl.)

## 基本信息
- **语言**: 拉丁语
- **词性**: prep. + abl.
- **词源**: 来自 [[Resources/lewis-short-json-master/ls_P.json|Lewis & Short]]: 与梵语 *pra*- (在前)、希腊语 πρό、拉丁语 *prae*、*prior*、*priscus* 同源。原始义为"在前"

## 释义
1. before, in front of (空间)
2. on behalf of, in defense of, for the benefit of (代表/保护)
3. in place of, instead of (替代)
4. in exchange for, in return for (交换)
5. according to, in proportion to, as (比例)
6. on account of (原因/为了)
