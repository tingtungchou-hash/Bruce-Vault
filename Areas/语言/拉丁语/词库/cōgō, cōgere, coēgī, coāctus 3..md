# cōgō, cōgere, coēgī, coāctus 3.

## 基本信息
- **语言**: 拉丁语
- **词性**: v. trans. (第三变位法，-ō 型)
- **词源**: contr. from *co-agō* (con- + agō, "to drive together")

## 释义
1. To drive together to one point, to collect, compress, bring together, assemble, gather
   a. Of troops: to collect, mass ( = *contrahere*)
   b. Of the senate: to call together, convene
   c. Of liquids: to thicken, condense, curdle
   d. Of places: to draw together into a narrow space
2. Trop., to urge, force, compel, constrain
   a. With *acc. + inf.*: to force (someone) to (do something)
   b. With *ut* + subj.: to compel that
   c. With *ad* + acc.: to drive toward
   d. With in + acc.: to drive into (a state)
3. (philos.) To infer, conclude logically

## 复习提示
- 本课出现: `coāctus est` — "he was forced" (BG V.24.1)
- 常见错误: 不要把主语当成物（meeting），实际主语是 Caesar
- 与 *iubeō* 区分: *cōgō* = force (不情愿的), *iubeō* = order (有权威的)
