# ūtor, ūtī, ūsus sum 1.dep.

## 基本信息
- **语言**: 拉丁语
- **词性**: v. 1.dep. (+ abl.)
- **词源**: 来自 [[Resources/lewis-short-json-master/ls_U.json|Lewis & Short]]: 古形式 *oetor*、*oitor*；与梵语同根，核心义为"使用、运用"

## 释义
*with abl.*
1. to use, employ, make use of (使用)
	1. administrīsque ad ea sacrificia Druidibus ūtuntur [[collegecaesar.14jan25.pdf#page=50&selection=167,9,172,6|Caesar]]
2. to enjoy, experience (享受、经历)
3. to manage, control, wield (操控)
4. to spend, use up (消耗)
5. to wear (穿着)
6. to accept, adopt (接受)
7. to resort to, consult (求助、咨询)
