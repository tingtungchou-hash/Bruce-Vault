# dō, dare, dedī, datus 1.

## 基本信息
- **语言**: 拉丁语
- **词性**: v. trans. (第一变位法，不规则)
- **词源**: Sanscr. *dā*, *da-dā-mi*, give; Gr. *δίδωμι*; cf. *dōs*, *dōnum*, *damnum*

## 释义
1. To give (the most general verb of giving)
   a. *alicui aliquid dare* = to give something to someone
   b. *obsidēs dare* = to give hostages
   c. *fīliam in mātrimōnium dare* = to give a daughter in marriage
   d. *litterās dare* = to send/write a letter
2. To grant, allow, permit
   a. *poenās dare* = to pay the penalty, suffer punishment
   b. *manūs dare* = to surrender (lit. "give hands")
   c. *terga dare* = to flee (lit. "give backs")
3. To cause, make, bring about
   a. *damnum dare* = to cause damage
   b. *hostēs in fugam dare* = to put the enemy to flight
4. To tell, relate, announce
   a. *fābulam dare* = to produce/exhibit a play

## 复习提示
- 本课出现: *dedit* — "he gave" (BG V.24.2)
- **第一变位法但完成体不规则**: *dare* > *dedī* (不是 *dāvī*)，因为它是 athematic 动词
- 注意与 [[dēdō, dēdere, dēdidī, dēditus 3.|dēdō]] 区分: *dō* = give | *dēdō* = surrender
