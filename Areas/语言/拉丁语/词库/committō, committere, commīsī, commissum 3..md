# committō, committere, commīsī, commissum 3.

## 基本信息
- **语言**: 拉丁语
- **词性**: v. a. (第三变位)
- **词源**: con- (together) + mittō (to send)。基本义 "to send together, join, connect"。

## 释义

1. **To join, combine, connect** (两个或多个对象)
   a. *committere inter se* — "to join together"
   b. *committere alicui* — "to join to something"

2. **To set together in contest, to match** (将人或动物投入对抗)
   a. *proelium committere* — "to join battle, to engage in battle"
   b. *pugnam committere* — "to fight a battle"

3. **To commit, perpetrate, do (wrong)** (高频义项)
   a. 带宾格：*nihil committere* — "to commit no fault, to do nothing wrong"
   b. 带 `ut` 从句：*committere ut...* — "to be guilty so that..., to act in such a way that..."
   c. 带 `contrā / in / adversus`：*committere in legem* — "to offend against the law"
   d. *poenam committere* — "to incur punishment"

4. **To entrust, commit to, give over to** (高频义项)
   a. *alicui aliquid committere* — "to entrust something to someone"
   b. *sē in alicuius fidem committere* — "to put oneself under someone's protection"

## 复习提示
- **Caesar 经典句式**：*nihil quod ipsīs esset indignum committēbant* — "they did nothing that would be unworthy of them" (BG 5.35)
- 注意不同义项差异巨大："join" / "engage in battle" / "commit (a crime)" / "entrust" — 必须根据上下文判断
- 义项 3 的否定用法 `nihil committere` 是 Caesar 道德评价的惯用表达
