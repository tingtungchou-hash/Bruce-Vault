# ulcīscor, ulcīscī, ultus sum 3.dep.

## 基本信息
- **语言**: 拉丁语
- **词性**: v. dep. (第三变位，异态动词)
- **词源**: etym. dub. (词源不明)

## 释义

1. to avenge oneself on, take vengeance on, punish for wrong done
   a. with personal object: *inimīcōs ulcīscī* — to avenge oneself on enemies; *aliquem prō scelere ulcīscī* — to punish someone for a crime
   b. with non-personal object: *iniūriās ulcīscī* — to avenge wrongs/injuries (see Caes. *B.G.* 5.38: *ulcīscendī Rōmānōs prō eīs... iniūriīs* — **本课用法**)

2. (transf.) to avenge a person (the one who suffered the wrong)
   a. *patrem ulcīscī* — to avenge one's father
   b. *sē ulcīscī* — to avenge oneself

## 复习提示
动形词 *ulcīscendī*（属格）带宾格宾语 *Rōmānōs* 的结构需特别注意：*ulcīscendī Rōmānōs* = "of avenging (themselves on) the Romans"。
