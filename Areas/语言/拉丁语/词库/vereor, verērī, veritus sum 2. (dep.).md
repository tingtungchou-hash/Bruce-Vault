# vereor, verērī, veritus sum 2. (dep.)

## 基本信息
- **语言**: 拉丁语
- **词性**: v. dep. (第二变位法，异态动词)
- **词源**: 可能与 `vereor` 的原始义 "to watch, observe with awe" 有关

## 释义

1. **To fear, be afraid of, apprehend** — with reverential or respectful awe mixed with fear
   a. Differs from *timeo* (simple fear) and *metuo* (fear of danger): *vereor* implies a respectful, often moral fear
   b. *Ille **veritus**, quod ad plures pertinebat, **ne** civitas deficeret* — he, fearing (because it pertained to many) lest the state should rebel (Caes. B.G. 5.25)

2. **To reverence, revere, respect, stand in awe of**
   a. *deos vereri* — to revere the gods
   b. *vereri patres* — to respect one's elders

3. Constructions:
   a. With simple object (acc.): *aliquem vereri*
   b. With ***ne* + subj.** (fear that something may happen): *vereor ne veniat* — I fear he may come
   c. With ***ut* + subj.** (fear that something may not happen): *vereor ut veniat* — I fear he may not come
   d. With inf. (rare, poetic)

## 关联词汇

- [[metuō, metuere, metuī, - 3.]] — to fear, dread (fear of danger)
- [[verendus, -a, -um]] — that is to be feared/reverenced, venerable

## 复习提示
BG 5.25 中 *veritus ... ne civitas ... deficeret*: `vereor` + `ne` + subj. = "fearing **lest** the state should rebel"（害怕会发生叛乱）。这是恐惧从句的标准结构，不要译成 "in case"。
