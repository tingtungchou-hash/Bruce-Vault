# circumsistō, circumsistere, circumstetī 3.

## 基本信息
- **语言**: 拉丁语
- **词性**: v. trans./intrans. (第三变位法)
- **词源**: *circum-* + *sistō* ("to stand around, take position around")

## 释义
1. To place oneself or take one's stand around; to surround, go or stand around
   a. With acc.: *hostēs impedītum circumsistunt* = the enemies surround the hindered man (BG V.44.8)
   b. With acc.: *nāvēs circumsistere* = to surround the ships
   c. Absol.: *circumsistāmus!* = "let us stand around!"

## 关联词汇
- [[sistō, sistere, stetī, status 3.]] — to place, set, stand
- [[circumveniō, circumvenīre, circumvēnī, circumventus 4.]] — to come around, surround, beset
- [[circumstō, circumstāre, circumstetī 1.]] — to stand around (near-synonym)

## 复习提示
- 本课出现: *impedītumque hostēs circumsistunt* — "and the enemies surround the hindered man" (BG V.44.8)
- 注意与 *circumveniō* 区别：*circumsistō* = "站到周围"（动作）→ "围住"（状态）；*circumveniō* = "绕过来包围"有更明显的军事"合围"含义
- ⑧ *circumsistunt* (现在时) 与 ⑫ *circumventō* (完成被动分词) 构成"包围"语义场的两个层次
