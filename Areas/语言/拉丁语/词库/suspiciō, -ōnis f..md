# suspiciō, -ōnis f.

## 基本信息
- **语言**: 拉丁语
- **词性**: f. (第三变格)
- **词源**: 源自 *suspiciō, suspicere* (to look up at, suspect); *sub-* + [[speciō, specere, spexī, spectus 3.|speciō]] (to look)

## 释义

1. suspicion, distrust, mistrust (经典通用义)
   a. *in suspiciōnem venīre* — 引起嫌疑/被怀疑 (见 Caes. *B.G.* 6.19 — **本课用法**)
   b. *suspiciōnem habēre* — 怀有嫌疑
   c. *suspiciōnem movēre* — 引起怀疑
   d. *suspiciō cadit in aliquem* — 嫌疑落在某人身上

2. slight indication, hint, inkling
   a. *suspiciō māiōris malī* — 对更大灾祸的预感

## 复习提示
