# palam (adv. & prep. + abl.)

## 基本信息
- **语言**: 拉丁语
- **词性**: adv. & prep. + abl.
- **词源**: 处所格形式，词根 `pal-/pla-` (如 `planus` = flat; 希腊语 `platus` = broad); 字面义 "on the surface, on the open plain"

## 释义

1. **Adv.** — Openly, publicly, undisguisedly, plainly
   a. Opposed to: *clam*, *occulte*, *secreto* (secretly, privately)
   b. *multis palam ex civitate auctoribus* — with many from the state openly (being) instigators (Caes. B.G. 5.25)
   c. *palam dicere* — to say openly

2. **Prep. + abl.** — before, in the presence of one (mostly poetical and post-Augustan)
   a. *te palam* — before you, in your presence (Hor.)
   b. *palam populo* — in the presence of the people (Liv.)
   c. *palam omnibus* — before everyone (Liv.)

## 关联词汇
- [[clam (adv. & prep.)]] — secretly, unknown to
- [[coram (adv. & prep. + abl.)]] — in the presence of, face to face
- [[apertē (adv.)]] — openly, frankly

## 复习提示
BG 5.25 中的 *multis palam ... auctoribus* 是独立夺格结构的核心: "with many openly instigating" (许多人公开地作为煽动者)。`palam` 强调事情不是暗中进行的——谋杀 Tasgetius 是公开有参与者的。
