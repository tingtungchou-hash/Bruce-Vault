# abiciō, abicere, abiēcī, abiectus 3.

## 基本信息
- **语言**: 拉丁语
- **词性**: v. (第三变位，-iō 类)
- **词源**: *ab* + *iaciō* (to throw)

## 释义

1. to throw away, cast away, throw down
   a. *arma abicere* — to throw away one's arms (see Caes. *B.G.* 5.37: *iussus arma abicere* — **本课用法**)
   b. *scūtum abicere* — to throw away one's shield (sign of cowardice)
   c. *sē abicere* — to throw oneself down (in despair or supplication)
   d. *sē ad pedēs abicere* — to throw oneself at someone's feet

2. (fig.) to cast off, give up, abandon, renounce
   a. *vītam abicere* — to throw away one's life; *salūtem prō aliquō abicere* — to sacrifice one's safety for someone
   b. *spem abicere* — to abandon hope; *memoriam abicere* — to cast aside the memory

3. (fig.) to degrade, humble, lower
   a. *sē abicere* — to degrade oneself, act in a base manner
   b. *senātūs auctōritātem abicere* — to lower the Senate's authority
   c. (P. a.) *abiectus, -a, -um* — downcast, disheartened, abject, low

## 复习提示
*iusus arma abicere* = "having been ordered to throw away his arms" — 注意 *iussus* 是被动完成分词（Sabinus 被命令），非主动。"被命令扔下武器" 而非 "下令扔下武器"。
