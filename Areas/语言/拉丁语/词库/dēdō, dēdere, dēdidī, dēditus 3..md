# dēdō, dēdere, dēdidī, dēditus 3.

## 基本信息
- **语言**: 拉丁语
- **词性**: v. trans. (第三变位法)
- **词源**: *de-* + *do* ("to give away from oneself")

## 释义
1. Lit., to give away, give up, surrender, deliver, consign, yield
   a. In general: *aliquem hostibus in cruciatum dedere* = to hand someone over to the enemy for torture
   b. Milit. t.t.: to deliver up, surrender to the enemy
      - *se dedere* = to surrender oneself, capitulate
      - *urbem dedere* = to surrender a city
      - *se suaque omnia Caesari dedere* = to surrender oneself and all one's possessions to Caesar
2. Trop., to give up, yield, devote, dedicate
   a. *se dedere* + dat.: to devote oneself to
      - *se totum philosophiae dedere* = to devote oneself entirely to philosophy
      - *se litteris dedere* = to apply oneself to literature
   b. *dedere operam* + dat. = to give one's attention to
3. *deditus, -a, -um* (PPP as adj.): given up to, addicted to, devoted to (+ dat.)

## 关联词汇
- [[dō, dare, dedī, datus 1.]] — to give (base verb)
- [[trādō, trādere, trādidī, trāditus 3.]] — to hand over, deliver
- [[prōdō, prōdere, prōdidī, prōditus 3.]] — to give forth, betray
- [[dēditiō, -ōnis f.]] — surrender, capitulation
- [[dēditīcius, -a, -um adj.]] — surrendered, capitulated

## 复习提示
- 注意与 [[dō, dare, dedī, datus 1.]] 区分: *dō* = give | *dēdō* = surrender/give up
- 本课中的 *dedit* 来自 *dō*，不是 *dēdō*
- *se dedere* 是 Caesar 中的高频表达（高卢部落投降的标准用语）
