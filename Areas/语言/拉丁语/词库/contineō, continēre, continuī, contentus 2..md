# contineō, continēre, continuī, contentus 2.

## 基本信息
- **语言**: 拉丁语
- **词性**: v. trans. (第二变位法)
- **词源**: *con-* + *teneō* ("to hold together")

## 释义
1. To hold or keep together
   a. Lit.: to enclose, bound, surround (often passive: "is contained within")
   b. To keep fast, hold, preserve, retain
2. To keep, hold still, detain, restrain, repress
   a. Milit.: *milites castris continere* = to keep troops in camp
   b. With *se*: to stay, remain (*se castris continere* = stay in camp)
   c. Trop.: to hold back, curb, check, subdue (*risum continere* = hold back laughter)
3. To comprise, contain, involve, comprehend
   a. Pass. *continērī aliquā rē* = to consist of, be composed of
4. Neutr.: to hold together, hang together
