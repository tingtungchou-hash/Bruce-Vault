# ultrō (adv.)

## 基本信息
- **语言**: 拉丁语
- **词性**: adv.
- **词源**: 源自 *ulter*（在更远那边的，比较级 *ulterior*）；词根 *ul- / oll-*（那一边）

## 释义
1. To the farther side, beyond, on the other side
   a. 最常见于对举短语 *ultrō citrōque* / *ultrō et citrō* = "to and fro, on both sides, mutually": *ultrō citrōque commētum est* (Caes.) = "there was passage back and forth"
2. （转义，极其常见）**Of one's own accord, spontaneously, voluntarily, without being asked**
   a. *ultrō aliquid offerre* (Cic.) = "to offer something of one's own accord"
   b. *ultrō pollicērī* (Caes. *BG* 1.42) = "to promise voluntarily"
   c. *ultrō ad mē vēnit* = "he came to me of his own accord"
   d. *ultrō mīlitum concursū ac vōcibus sibi parcere cōgerētur* (Caes. *BG* 5.40.7) = "of their own accord, by the soldiers' thronging and shouting, he was compelled to spare himself"
3. Besides, moreover, too, even over and above
   a. *ultrō ... dēbet* = "he even owes (money) besides"
   b. *miserēscimus ultrō* (Verg.) = "we even take pity"
4. Gratuitously, wantonly, without provocation
   a. *sibi ultrō per contumēliās hostem īnsultāre* (Liv.) = "the enemy even wantonly insulted them"

## 关联词汇
- [[ulter, -a, -um]] — farther, beyond（比较级 *ulterior*，最高级 *ultimus*）
- [[ultrōneus, -a, -um adj.]] — voluntary（晚期拉丁语形容词形式）

## 复习提示
- 本课出现：BG 5.40.7 *ut ultrō mīlitum concursū ac vōcibus sibi parcere cōgerētur* — "of their own accord, by the soldiers' thronging and shouting, he was compelled to spare himself"
- 核心区分：*ultrō* = 没有被要求就做（自发性） vs. *sponte* = 没有外力强制就做（主动性） — 尽管常可互换，但 *ultrō* 强调"对方没开口"的维度，*sponte* 强调"自己愿意"的维度
- Caesar 中使用频率很高，尤其在描述谈判、提议、攻击中一方"主动"行事时
