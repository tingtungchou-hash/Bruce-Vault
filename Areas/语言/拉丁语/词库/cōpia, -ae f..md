# cōpia, -ae f. / cōpiae, -ārum f. pl.

## 基本信息
- **语言**: 拉丁语
- **词性**: n. f. (第一变格法)
- **词源**: 来自 *co-ops* (共同 + 力量) -> *cōpia*; 与 *ops* "力量、资源" 同族。[[Resources/lewis-short-json-master/ls_C.json|Lewis & Short]] 释为 "co-ops"

## 释义
1. abundance, wealth, riches, resources (物质)
	1. cum eius generis cōpia dēfēcit [[collegecaesar.14jan25.pdf#page=50&selection=299,22,305,3|Caesar]]
2. fulness, copiousness, multitude, number (抽象)
3. troops, forces, army (军事用语，常 pl.)
	1. ut quisque est genere copiīsque amplissimus [[collegecaesar.14jan25.pdf#page=50&selection=50,4,52,16|Caesar]]

## 关联词汇
- [[cōpiōsus, -a, -um]] — abundant, wealthy
- [[ops, opis f.]] — power, resources
- [[inopia, -ae f.]] — scarcity (反义词)
