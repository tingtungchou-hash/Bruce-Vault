# opprimō, opprimere, oppressī, oppressus 3.

## 基本信息
- **语言**: 拉丁语
- **词性**: v. (第三变位，及物)
- **词源**: *ob* + *premō* (to press against/upon)

## 释义

1. to press down, press against, crush
   a. *terrā opprimī* — to be crushed by earth; *ruīnā opprimī* — to be crushed by a collapsing building
   b. *oculōs opprimere* — to close the eyes; *faucem manū opprimere* — to throttle with one's hand

2. to overpower, overwhelm, crush, subdue (military/political)
   a. *hostēs opprimere* — to overwhelm the enemy; *nātiōnem opprimere* — to subdue a nation
   b. *rem pūblicam opprimere* — to overthrow the republic

3. to fall upon, take by surprise, catch unawares
   a. *subitō oppressam legiōnem interficī* — that the legion, having been suddenly overwhelmed, could be slaughtered (Caes. *B.G.* 5.38 — **本课用法**)
   b. *imprūdentem opprimere* — to catch someone off guard
   c. *incautōs opprimere* — to surprise the unwary

4. to bear down, load, overwhelm (with emotions/circumstances)
   a. *metū/timōre opprimī* — to be overwhelmed by fear; *aere aliēnō opprimī* — to be crushed by debt

5. to suppress, conceal, hide: *īram opprimere* — to suppress anger; *mentiōnem opprimere* — to hush up a mention

## 复习提示
*subitō oppressam* — 完成时被动分词，修饰 *legiōnem*，表 "having been suddenly overwhelmed/taken by surprise"。强调出其不意的碾压，非普通 "attack"。今天翻译中误译为此义。
