# praeficiō, praeficere, praefēcī, praefectus 3.

## 基本信息
- **语言**: 拉丁语
- **词性**: v. trans. (第三变位法，-iō 型)
- **词源**: *prae-* + *faciō* ("to set/place before" > "to put in charge of")

## 释义
1. To set over (as officer, superintendent, leader), to place in authority over, appoint to the command of
   a. With dat.: *alicui praeficere* = to put someone in command of X
      - *eīs Marcum Crassum ... praefēcit* = he put Crassus in charge of them
   b. With dat. of thing: *legiōnibus legātōs praeficere* = to put legates in command of the legions
2. Trop.: to set over (rare)

## 复习提示
- 本课出现: `praefēcit` — "he put (them) in command" (BG V.24.3)
- 你译成了 "sent ... beforehand"，正确应为 "placed in command"
- 核心结构: *praeficiō + acc. (person) + dat. (thing/people)* = "appoint X over Y"
- 被动: *praeficior* + dat. = "I am put in charge of"
