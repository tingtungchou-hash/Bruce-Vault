## 第三变格法

[[Essential Latin Vocabulary (Mark Williams) OCR.pdf#page=22&selection=0,0,3,3|THIRD DECLENSION（M）]]

### 阳性

1. aethēr, aetheris -- pure upper air, ether, heaven, sky
2. amnis, amnis -- river, torrent
3. amor, amōris -- love
4. cinis, cineris -- ashes, embers
5. clāmor, clāmōris -- outcry, shout
6. collis, collis -- hill
7. color, colōris -- color
8. cōnsul, cōnsulis -- consul
9. crīnis, crīnis -- hair; lock of hair; plume of a helmet; tail of a comet
10. dīves, dīvitis -- the rich; those who are well off; (Gn. Pl. -um)
11. dolor, dolōris -- pain, grief; sorrow; resentment
12. dux, ducis -- leader, guide, general
13. eques, equitis -- horseman, rider; (Pl.) cavalry; equestrian order
14. error, errōris -- wandering; error, mistake, deception
15. fīnis, fīnis -- end point, termination; boundary
16. flōs, flōris -- flower, blossom; bloom; prime of youth
17. fōns, fontis -- spring, fountain
18. frāter, frātris -- brother
19. furor, furōris -- rage; fury
20. grex, gregis -- herd
21. gurges, gurgitis -- gulf, whirlpool; sea
22. homō, hominis -- person, man
23. honor, honōris -- honor, praise, glory
24. hospes, hospitis -- host; guest, visitor, stranger; soldier in billets
25. hymen, hymenis -- wedding chant; wedding; as a proper name: god of marriage
26. ignis, ignis -- fire
27. imber, imbris -- rain-storm, shower
28. imperātor, imperātōris -- commander, general
29. iūdex, iūdicis -- judge, juror, arbiter
30. labor, labōris -- toil, exertion
31. lapis, lapidis -- stone
32. lār, laris -- Lares (Gn. Pl. -um or -ium); god(s) of the home, hearth, household
33. leō, leōnis -- lion
34. mēnsis, mēnsis -- month
35. mercātor, mercātōris -- merchant
36. mīles, mīlitis -- soldier
37. mōns, montis -- mountain
38. mōs, mōris -- manner, way, custom, habit
39. nēmō, nēminis -- no one, nobody
40. nepōs, nepōtis -- grandson; descendant; spendthrift, prodigal, playboy
41. ōrātor, ōrātōris -- pleader, orator, spokesman
42. orbis, orbis -- circle; territory; sphere
43. ōrdō, ōrdinis -- succession, order, class, rank, row, etc.
44. pater, patris -- father
45. pauper, pauperis -- a poor person
46. pēs, pedis -- foot
47. prīnceps, prīncipis -- the first person on a list; the one who originates, first delivers, or declares, an opinion; the first, the most eminent, most distinguished
48. pudor, pudōris -- shame, modesty, propriety; sense of honor
49. rēx, rēgis -- king
50. sanguis, sanguinis -- blood
51. sapiēns, sapientis -- sage, philosopher, wise/virtuous man; a teacher of wisdom
52. senātor, senātōris -- senator
53. sermō, sermōnis -- conversation, discussion; diction; talk; word
54. sōl, sōlis -- sun
55. terror, terrōris -- fright, terror, panic
56. timor, timōris -- fear, apprehension
57. vertex (or vortex), verticis -- whirl; top of a whirl; summit, head, height
58. vetus, veteris -- antiquity; (Pl.) ancient times; traditional ways; forefathers; as adj.: old, of long standing
59. victor, victōris -- conqueror
