### 中性

[[Essential Latin Vocabulary (Mark Williams) OCR.pdf#page=20&selection=0,0,3,3|SECOND DECLENSION（N）]]

1. aevum, aevi -- lifetime, age, old age; eternity
2. antrum, antri -- cave, cavern, grot
3. arma, armorum -- (Pl. only) arms, armor; implements of war; gear, tackle
4. arvum, arvi -- field; plowed land
5. astrum, astri -- heavenly body, star, planet; constellation; sky
6. aurum, auri -- gold; money
7. auxilium, auxilii -- support, assistance; (Pl.) auxiliary forces, reinforcements
8. bellum, belli -- war
9. beneficium, beneficii -- kindness, favor, privilege
10. bonum, boni -- good, good thing; profit, advantage; material goods, supplies
11. caelum, caeli -- sky; heaven
12. castra, castrorum -- camp, military encampment (Pl. only)
13. ceteri, ceterorum -- the others, the rest; all the rest; (Pl.) from ceterus, used as a subst.
14. collum, colli -- neck
15. consilium, consilii -- plan, idea, advice; council, deliberative body; prudence, discretion
16. donum, doni -- gift, present
17. exemplum, exempli -- pattern; copy; analogy; archetype; reproduction, transcription
18. exsilium, exsilii -- exile, banishment; those exiled (Pl.)
19. factum, facti -- fact, deed, act; accomplishment, achievement
20. fatum, fati -- fate (lit. that which is spoken)
21. ferrum, ferri -- iron; iron tool; weapon, sword
22. forum, fori -- market-place; Roman forum; place of public meeting
23. fretum, freti -- strait (of water)
24. gaudium, gaudii -- delight, joy, pleasure
25. imperium, imperii -- empire, power, command
26. incendium, incendii -- conflagration; glow, heat
27. ingenium, ingenii -- character; temperament; ability
28. initium, initi -- beginning, commencement; entrance
29. iudicium, iudicii -- judgment, decision; opinion; trial
30. iugum, iugi -- yoke; ridge, chain of hills; summit
31. letum, leti -- ruin; (poetry) death
32. malum, mali -- evil, mischief; disaster; harm; also: apple
33. membrum, membri -- member, limb, organ; male genitals; room; section
34. monumentum, monumenti -- reminder; memorial, monument
35. negotium, negotii -- trouble, distress; work, business, activity, job
36. odium, odii -- hatred; unpopularity; boredom, impatience
37. officium, officii -- service, duty; kindness
38. oppidum, oppidi -- town
39. osculum, osculi -- kiss
40. otium, otii -- leisure
41. pelagus, pelagi -- sea (esp. the open sea)
42. periculum, periculi -- danger, peril
43. poculum, poculi -- drinking cup
44. praeceptum, praecepti -- lesson, teaching, precept; command, order
45. praemium, praemii -- bounty, reward
46. praesidium, praesidii -- protection; help; guard; garrison
47. pretium, pretii -- price
48. principium, principii -- beginning
49. proelium, proelii -- fight, battle
50. regnum, regni -- royal power; control; kingdom
51. sacrum, sacri -- sacrifice; sacred vessel; (Pl.) religious rites
52. saeculum, saeculi -- a generation; age, esp. century; time in general
53. saxum, saxi -- stone; stone face, cliff
54. sepulcrum, sepulcri -- place of burial, tomb, grave
55. sertum, serti -- wreath of flowers, garland
56. signum, signi -- sign, signal; mark, a seal; military standard
57. somnium, somnii -- dream
58. spatium, spatii -- space (of time, usually; sometimes: of place)
59. studium, studii -- eagerness, devotion; (Pl.) pursuits
60. supplicium, supplicii -- punishment, suffering; supplication; torture
61. tectum, tecti -- a covered place; roof; building, house
62. telum, teli -- weapon; bolt, javelin
63. templum, templi -- temple, holy place; place marked off for augury
64. tergum, tergi -- back, rear
65. vadum, vadi -- a ford; shallows, a shoal
66. velum, veli -- a cloth, covering; esp. sail
67. venenum, veneni -- poison; drug
68. verbum, verbi -- word
69. verum, veri -- truth, reality, fact; verum as adv.: yes, in truth, indeed
70. vestigium, vestigii -- footstep, footprint, track, trace
71. vinculum, vinculi -- bond, fetter, tie
72. vinum, vini -- wine; vine
73. vitium, vitii -- flaw, defect, foible; crime
74. votum, voti -- vow, sacred promise; prayer; sacred offering; vote
75. vulgus, vulgi -- the common people
