### 中性

[[Essential Latin Vocabulary (Mark Williams) OCR.pdf#page=20&selection=0,0,3,3|SECOND DECLENSION（N）]]

1. **aevum, aevī** -- lifetime, age, old age; eternity
2. **antrum, antrī** -- cave, cavern, grot
3. arma, armōrum -- (Pl. only) arms, armor; implements of war; gear, tackle
4. **arvum, arvī** -- field; plowed land
5. astrum, astrī -- heavenly body, star, planet; constellation; sky
6. aurum, aurī -- gold; money
7. auxilium, auxiliī -- support, assistance; (Pl.) auxiliary forces, reinforcements
8. bellum, bellī -- war
9. beneficium, beneficiī -- kindness, favor, privilege
10. bonum, bonī -- good, good thing; profit, advantage; material goods, supplies
11. caelum, caelī -- sky; heaven
12. castra, castrōrum -- camp, military encampment (Pl. only)
13. ceterī, ceterōrum -- the others, the rest; all the rest; (Pl.) from ceterus, used as a subst.
14. **collum, collī** -- neck
15. cōnsilium, cōnsiliī -- plan, idea, advice; council, deliberative body; prudence, discretion
16. dōnum, dōnī -- gift, present
17. exemplum, exemplī -- pattern; copy; analogy; archetype; reproduction, transcription
18. exsilium, exsiliī -- exile, banishment; those exiled (Pl.)
19. factum, factī -- fact, deed, act; accomplishment, achievement
20. fātum, fātī -- fate (lit. that which is spoken)
21. ferrum, ferrī -- iron; iron tool; weapon, sword
22. forum, forī -- market-place; Roman forum; place of public meeting
23. **fretum, fret**ī -- strait (of water)
24. gaudium, gaudiī -- delight, joy, pleasure
25. imperium, imperiī -- empire, power, command
26. **incendium, incendiī** -- conflagration; glow, heat
27. **ingenium, ingeniī** -- character; temperament; ability
28. initium, initī -- beginning, commencement; entrance
29. iudicium, iudiciī -- judgment, decision; opinion; trial
30. **iugum, iugī** -- yoke; ridge, chain of hills; summit
31. **lētum, lētī** -- ruin; (poetry) death
32. malum, malī -- evil, mischief; disaster; harm; also: apple
33. membrum, membrī -- member, limb, organ; male genitals; room; section
34. monumentum, monumentī -- reminder; memorial, monument
35. negotium, negotiī -- trouble, distress; work, business, activity, job
36. **odium, odiī** -- hatred; unpopularity; boredom, impatience
37. officium, officiī -- service, duty; kindness
38. oppidum, oppidī -- town
39. **ōsculum, ōsculī** -- kiss
40. ōtium, ōtiī -- leisure
41. **pelagus, pelagī** -- sea (esp. the open sea)
42. perīculum, perīculī -- danger, peril
43. **pōculum, pōculī** -- drinking cup
44. **praeceptum, praeceptī** -- lesson, teaching, precept; command, order
45. praemium, praemiī -- bounty, reward
46. praesidium, praesidiī -- protection; help; guard; garrison
47. pretium, pretiī -- price
48. prīncipium, prīncipiī -- beginning
49. proelium, proeliī -- fight, battle
50. rēgnum, rēgnī -- royal power; control; kingdom
51. **sacrum, sacrī** -- sacrifice; sacred vessel; (Pl.) religious rites
52. **saeculum, saeculī** -- a generation; age, esp. century; time in general
53. **saxum, saxī** -- stone; stone face, cliff
54. **sepulcrum, sepulcrī** -- place of burial, tomb, grave
55. **sertum, sertī** -- wreath of flowers, garland
56. sīgnum, sīgnī -- sign, signal; mark, a seal; military standard
57. somnium, somniī -- dream
58. spatium, spatiī -- space (of time, usually; sometimes: of place)
59. studium, studiī -- eagerness, devotion; (Pl.) pursuits
60. **supplicium, suppliciī** -- punishment, suffering; supplication; torture
61. **tēctum, tēctī** -- a covered place; roof; building, house
62. tēlum, tēlī -- weapon; bolt, javelin
63. templum, templī -- temple, holy place; place marked off for augury
64. **tergum, tergī** -- back, rear
65. **vadum, vadī** -- a ford; shallows, a shoal
66. **vēlum, vēlī** -- a cloth, covering; esp. sail
67. **venēnum, venēnī** -- poison; drug
68. verbum, verbī -- word
69. verum, verī -- truth, reality, fact; verum as adv.: yes, in truth, indeed
70. **vestīgium, vestīgiī** -- footstep, footprint, track, trace
71. vinculum, vinculī -- bond, fetter, tie
72. **vīnum, vīnī** -- wine; vine
73. vitium, vitiī -- flaw, defect, foible; crime
74. vōtum, vōtī -- vow, sacred promise; prayer; sacred offering; vote
75. **vulgus, vulgī** -- the common people
