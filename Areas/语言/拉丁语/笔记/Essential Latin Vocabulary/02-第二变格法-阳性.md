## 第二变格法

[[Essential Latin Vocabulary (Mark Williams) OCR.pdf#page=17&selection=64,0,65,10|SECOND DECLENSION（M&F）]]

### 阳性
1. ager, agri -- cultivated land, field, country
2. amicus, amici -- friend
3. animus, animi -- feeling, spirit, soul
4. annus, anni -- year
5. barbarus, barbari -- foreigner, stranger (of all but Greeks and Romans)
6. campus, campi -- plain, field
7. capillus, capilli -- head of hair, hair
8. captivus, captivi -- captive, prisoner of war
9. chorus, chori -- dance, choral dance; band, troop
10. cibus, cibi -- food
11. deus, dei -- god
12. digitus, digiti -- finger
13. dominus, domini -- householder, master, lord
14. equus, equi -- horse
15. filius, filii -- son
16. gladius, gladii -- sword (the prose word)
17. hortus, horti -- garden
18. inimicus, inimici -- personal enemy
19. legatus, legati -- envoy, ambassador; commander of a legion; officer
20. libellus, libelli -- booklet
21. liber, libri -- book, volume; inner bark of the tree
22. liberi, liberorum -- children (Pl. only)
23. libertus, liberti -- freedman, ex-slave
24. locus, loci -- place, location (Pl. is Nt.: -a, -orum); Pl. loci, -orum: passages in a book
25. lucus, luci -- grove; sacred grove
26. magister, magistri -- master, schoolmaster; foreman, chief; steersman
27. maritus, mariti -- husband, married man; lover, suitor
28. minister, ministri -- attendant, servant; tool, agent
29. modus, modi -- measure, manner, kind
30. morbus, morbi -- sickness, disease
31. mundus, mundi -- universe, the world; the earth; the inhabitants of the earth, mankind
32. murus, muri -- wall
33. natus, nati -- son; (of animals) a young one; (Pl.) offspring; sometimes nata, -ae, f., for daughter
34. numerus, numeri -- number, amount; rhythm
35. nuntius, nuntii -- messenger; message, news
36. oculus, oculi -- eye
37. polus, poli -- pole (extremity of an axis); sky, heavens
38. pontus, ponti -- the open sea, deep
39. populus, populi -- people; nation
40. puer, pueri -- boy; slave; (Pl.) children
41. ramus, rami -- branch, bough (of a tree)
42. rogus, rogi -- funeral pyre
43. servus, servi -- slave
44. socius, socii -- ally, confederate
45. somnus, somni -- sleep
46. sonus, soni -- sound, noise
47. taurus, tauri -- bull
48. thalamus, thalami -- bed-chamber, couch, (esp.) bridal bed
49. titulus, tituli -- inscription, label, title; notice, bill, placard; honourable appellation, title; pretext
50. torus, tori -- bed, couch; royal seat or throne
51. tribunus, tribuni -- tribune
52. triumphus, triumphi -- triumph
53. tumulus, tumuli -- hillock, mound; swelling
54. ventus, venti -- wind
55. vicinus, vicini -- neighbour
56. vir, viri -- man; husband; hero
