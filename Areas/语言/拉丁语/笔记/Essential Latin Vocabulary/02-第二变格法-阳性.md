## 第二变格法

[[Essential Latin Vocabulary (Mark Williams) OCR.pdf#page=17&selection=64,0,65,10|SECOND DECLENSION（M&F）]]

### 阳性
1. ager, agrī -- cultivated land, field, country
2. amīcus, amīcī -- friend
3. animus, animī -- feeling, spirit, soul
4. annus, annī -- year
5. barbarus, barbarī -- foreigner, stranger (of all but Greeks and Romans)
6. campus, campī -- plain, field
7. **capillus, capillī** -- head of hair, hair
8. captīvus, captīvī -- captive, prisoner of war
9. **chorus, chorī** -- dance, choral dance; band, troop
10. cibus, cibī -- food
11. deus, deī -- god
12. digitus, digitī -- finger
13. dominus, dominī -- householder, master, lord
14. equus, equī -- horse
15. fīlius, fīliī -- son
16. gladius, gladiī -- sword (the prose word)
17. **hortus, hortī** -- garden
18. inimīcus, inimīcī -- personal enemy
19. legatus, legātī -- envoy, ambassador; commander of a legion; officer
20. libellus, libellī -- booklet
21. liber, librī -- book, volume; inner bark of the tree
22. līberī, līberōrum -- children (Pl. only)
23. lībertus, lībertī -- freedman, ex-slave
24. locus, locī -- place, location (Pl. is Nt.: -a, -ōrum); Pl. locī, -ōrum: passages in a book
25. **lūcus, lucī** -- grove; sacred grove
26. magister, magistrī -- master, schoolmaster; foreman, chief; steersman
27. **marītus, marītī** -- husband, married man; lover, suitor
28. minister, ministrī -- attendant, servant; tool, agent
29. modus, modī -- measure, manner, kind
30. morbus, morbī -- sickness, disease
31. mundus, mundī -- universe, the world; the earth; the inhabitants of the earth, mankind
32. **mūrus, mūrī** -- wall
33. **nātus, nātī** -- son; (of animals) a young one; (Pl.) offspring; sometimes **nāta, -ae**, f., for daughter
34. numerus, numerī -- number, amount; rhythm
35. nūntius, nūntiī -- messenger; message, news
36. oculus, oculī -- eye
37. **polus, polī** -- pole (extremity of an axis); sky, heavens
38. pontus, pontī -- the open sea, deep
39. populus, populī -- people; nation
40. puer, puerī -- boy; slave; (Pl.) children
41. **rāmus, rāmī** -- branch, bough (of a tree)
42. **rogus, rogī** -- funeral pyre
43. servus, servī -- slave
44. socius, sociī -- ally, confederate
45. somnus, somnī -- sleep
46. **sonus, sonī** -- sound, noise
47. **taurus, taurī** -- bull
48. **thalamus, thalamī** -- bed-chamber, couch, (esp.) bridal bed
49. titulus, titulī -- inscription, label, title; notice, bill, placard; honourable appellation, title; pretext
50. **torus, torī** -- bed, couch; royal seat or throne
51. tribunus, tribunī -- tribune
52. triumphus, triumphī -- triumph
53. **tumulus, tumulī** -- hillock, mound; swelling
54. ventus, ventī -- wind
55. vīcīnus, vīcīnī -- neighbour
56. vir, virī -- man; husband; hero

### 阴性
1. **humus, humī** -- ground
