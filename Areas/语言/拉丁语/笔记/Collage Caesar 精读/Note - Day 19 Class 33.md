## 语法分析
LXV
① **Quantō** erat \[in diēs] gravior atque asperior oppugnātiō, 
**et** maximē quod \[magnā parte mīlitum cōnfectā vulneribus], rēs \[ad paucitātem defensōrum] **pervēnerat**, 
**tantō** crebriōrēs litterae nūntiīque ad Caesarem mittēbantur; 
quōrum pars dēprehēnsa in conspectū nostrōrum mīlitum cum cruciātū **necabātur**.

② **Erat** ūnus intus Nervius nōmine Verticō, locō nātus honestō, quī ā prīmā obsidiōne ad Cicerōnem perfūgerat suam**que** eī fīdem praestiterat.
③ Hic servō \[spē libertātis] magnīs**que** persuādet praemiīs, 
**ut** litterās ad Caesarem dēferat. 

④ Hās ille \[in iaculō] illigātās **effert** et Gallus inter Gallōs sine ūllā suspiciōne versātus ad Caesarem **pervenit**. 
⑤ Ab eō dē periculīs Cicerōnis legiōnisque cognōscitur. 

LXVI 
① Caesar, acceptīs litterīs, hōrā circiter XI diēī statim nūntium in Bellovacōs ad M. Crassum quaestōrem **mittit**, cuius hīberna aberant ab eō milia passuum XXV;
② iubet \[mediā nocte] legiōnem profīciscī celeriterque ad sē venīre.

③ Exit cum nūntiō Crassus. Alterum ad Gaium Fabium lēgātum mittit, **ut** in Atrebātium fīnēs legiōnem adducat, quā sibi iter faciendum sciēbat.

④ Scribit Labiēnō, sī reī pūblicae commodō facere posset, cum legiōne ad fīnēs Nerviōrum veniat. Reliquam partem exercitūs, quod paulō aberat longius, non putat exspectandam; equitēs circiter quadringentōs ex proximīs hibernīs colligit.

## 英文翻译
LXV
① ~~On this day~~ ***With each passing day***, the more harshly and violently the siege was, and especially because a great part of the soldiers were heavily wounded, the situation reached to the paucity of defence, the more letters and messengers were sent to Caesar, ~~of which part was caught in the close watching of our soldiers and harmed with torture~~ ***a part of whom, having been intercepted, were put to death with torture in the sight of our soldiers***. 

② There was one in the camp of Cicero who was a Nervius, name being Vertico, born to honourable status, who fled to Cicero from the siege and ~~dedicated himself under his good faith. (i.e. surrendered to him)~~ ***had proved his loyalty to him*** 

③ He persuaded a slave, exploiting his hope for liberation and offering a great reward, to carry the letter to Caesar. 

④ The slave carried what was fastened to a javelin and as a Gaul moving around without arousing any suspicion, reached Caesar. 
⑤ It was known from him the perils of Cicero and the legion. 

LXVI
① Caesar, with the letters having been received, immediately sent a messenger to Marcus Crassus in the Bellovaci at about the 11th hour of the day, whose winter quarters were 25 miles from him. 
② He orders the legionn to set out in midnight and quickly go to him. 

③ Crassus went out with the messenger. He (Caesar) sent another messenger to Gaius Fabius the legate, ordering that he lead the legion march to the borders of Atrebates, where he (Caesar) knew that a journey shall be made by him (Gaius Fabius). 

④ He wrote to Labienus, that if he was able to work for the interest of the republic, go with the legion to the borders of the Nervii. The rest of the army, because it was a little farther away, he ~~did not wnat to wait for~~ ***did not think should be waited for***. He collected 400 knights from the nearing camps.

## 补充说明

### 本课习语与固定搭配

以下搭配按"字面义 vs. 实际义差距"严格筛选，字面可直接推导的不列入。

| 搭配                   | 字面          | 实际义                    | 本课用例                                                                      |
| -------------------- | ----------- | ---------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **in dies** (+ 比较级)  | "在日子里"      | 逐日；一天比一天更（渐进式加剧）       | `Quantō erat **in diēs** gravior ... oppugnātiō` — 围城一天比一天更猛烈             |
| **fidem praestāre**  | "在信义前站立"    | 证明/履行忠诚（军政语境中固定搭配）     | `suamque eī **fidem praestiterat**` — 且已向他证明了自己的忠诚                        |
| **iter facere**      | "做一次行程"     | 行军（Caesar 军事用语标准搭配）    | `quā sibi **iter faciendum** sciēbat` — 他知道必须经此行军                         |
| **quantō ... tantō** | "以多少……以那么多" | 越……越……（比例关联对，拆开则不成完整义） | `**Quantō** erat gravior ... **tantō** crebriōrēs litterae` — 围城越猛烈，送信越频繁 |
