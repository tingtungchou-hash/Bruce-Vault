## 动词

1. [[dēsum, dēesse, dēfuī (irr.)]] -- to be absent, fail, be wanting in one's duty (+ dat.); BG 5.33 *nulla in re communi saluti deerat*
2. ★ [[obeō, obīre, obīvī, obitum 4.]] -- to go to meet; to attend to, perform, execute; BG 5.33 *omnia per se obire*

## 名词

3. [[insidiae, -ārum f. pl.]] -- ambush, ambuscade; plot, snare; BG 5.32 *collocatis insidiis bipertito in silvis*
4. ★ [[negōtium, -ī n.]] -- business, affair; trouble, difficulty; crisis; BG 5.33 *in ipso negotio consilium capere*
