## 动词

1. ★ [[cōnsulō, cōnsulere, cōnsuluī, cōnsultus 3.|cōnsulō]] — to take care for, look to (+ dat.); to consult (+ acc.); BG 5.27, 5.28 (3)
2. ★ [[polliceor, pollicērī, pollicitus sum 2.dep.|polliceor]] — to promise, offer; BG 5.27 *tūtum iter datūrum pollicērī* (2.dep.)
3. ★ [[doceō, docēre, docuī, doctus 2.|doceō]] — to teach, instruct; (here) to argue, demonstrate; BG 5.28 *sustinērī posse docēbant* (2)

## 副词/连词

4. ★ [[vix (adv.)|vix]] — hardly, scarcely, with difficulty; BG 5.28 *vix erat crēdendum* (adv.)
5. [[quoniam (conj.)|quoniam]] — since, seeing that, inasmuch as; BG 5.27 *quibus quoniam ... satisfēcerit* (conj.)
6. [[etsi (conj.)|etsi]] — although, even if; BG 5.28 *etsī ab hoste ea dīcēbantur* (conj.)
