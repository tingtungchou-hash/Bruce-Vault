Ipsōrum esse cōnsilium velintne priusquam fīnitimī sentiant ēductōs ex hībernīs mīlitēs aut ad Cicerōnem aut ad Labiēnum dēdūcere, quōrum alter mīlia passuum circiter quinquaginta, alter paulō amplius ab eīs absit. 10 . Lucius Aurunculēius complūrēsque tribūnī mīlitum et prīmōrum ōrdinum centuriōnēs nihil temerē agendum neque ex hībernīs iniūssū Caesaris discēdendum exīstimābant: quantāsvīs magnās etiam cōpiās Germānōrum sustinērī posse mūnītīs hībernīs docēbant: rem esse testimōniō, quod prīmum hostium impetum multīs ultrō vulneribus illātis fortissimē sustinuerint: rē frumentāriā non premī; intereā et ex proximīs hībernīs et ā Caesare conventūra subsidia: postrēmō quid esse levius aut turpius quam, auctōre hoste, dē summīs rēbus capere cōnsilium?

## 英文翻译
XXVII 
⑦ Since he had completed enough of these out of piety, 
he now had a regard of his duty for the good deeds of Caesar: 
he said he pleaded to Titurius on behalf of hospitality, 
that he consider the safety of himself and his soldiers. 

⑧ a great assembled army of the Germans had crossed over the Rhine; 
This would be present in two days. 

⑨ the plan is their own whether they retreat the soldiers out of the winter camp to either Cicero or Labienus, of whom one is about 500 miles, and the other was little more away from them. 

⑩ he promised it and confirmed by a swear that he would give a guarded journey through his borders. 
⑪ with that all done, he would tell his community why the winter camps were lifted, and convey gratitude to Caesar for his merits. 
After having this speech, Ambiorix went away.
