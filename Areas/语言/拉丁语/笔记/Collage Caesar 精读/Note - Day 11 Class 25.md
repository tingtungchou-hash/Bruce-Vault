33.5 nam et nostrīs mīlitibus spem minuit et hostēs ad pugnam alacriōrēs effēcit, quod nōn sine summō timōre et dēspērātiōne id factum vidēbātur. Praetereā accidit, quod fierī necesse erat, ut vulgō mīlitēs ab signīs discēderent, quae quisque eōrum cārissima habēret, ab impedīmentīs petere atque arripere properāret, clāmōre et flētū omnia complērentur. 34.1 At barbarīs cōnsilium nōn dēfuit. Nam ducēs eōrum tōtā aciē prōnūntiāre iussērunt, nē quis ab locō discēderet: illōrum esse praedam atque illīs reservārī quaecumque Rōmānī reliquissent: proinde omnia in victōriā posita existīmārent. 2 . Erant et virtūte et studiō pugnandī parēs; nostrī, tametsī ab duce et ā Fortūnā dēserēbantur, tamen omnem spem salūtis in virtūte ponēbant, et quotiēns quaeque cohors prōcurrerat, ab eā parte magnus numerus hostium cadēbat. 3 . Quā rē animadversā, Ambiorix prōnūntiārī iubet, ut procul tēla coniciant neu propius accēdant et, quam in partem Rōmānī impetum fēcerint, cēdant (levitāte armōrum et cōtidiānā exercitātiōne nihil eīs nocērī posse), 4 . rūrsus sē ad signa recipientēs īnsequantur. 35.1 Quō praeceptō ab eīs dīligentissimē observātō, cum quaepiam cohors ex orbe excesserat atque impetum fēcerat, hostēs vēlōcissimē refugiēbant. 2 . Interim eam partem nūdārī necesse erat et ab latere apertō tēla recipī.

## 语法分析
XXXIII
⑤ **nam** 
**et** nostrīs mīlitibus spem minuit **et** hostēs \[ad pugnam] ==alacriōrēs== effēcit, 
**quod** nōn \[sine summō timōre et dēspērātiōne] id factum **vidēbātur**. 

==Praetereā== accidit, quod fierī necesse erat, 
**ut** \[vulgō] mīlitēs \[ab signīs] **discēderent**, 
*quae quisque eōrum cārissima **habēret***, \[ab impedīmentīs] petere atque ==arripere== **==properāret==**, 
clāmōre et flētū omnia **complērentur**.

XXXIV
① At barbarīs cōnsilium nōn dēfuit. 
Nam ducēs eōrum \[tōtā aciē] prōnūntiāre **iussērunt**, 
**nē** quis ab locō **discēderet**: 
illōrum esse ==praedam== **atque** illīs reservārī quaecumque Rōmānī **reliquissent**: proinde omnia in victōriā posita **existīmārent**.

② Erant \[et virtūte et studiō] pugnandī parēs; 
nostrī, tametsī \[ab duce et ā Fortūnā] dēserēbantur, 
tamen omnem spem salūtis \[in virtūte] **ponēbant**, 
et quotiēns quaeque cohors prōcurrerat, \[ab eā parte] magnus numerus hostium cadēbat. 

③ Quā rē animadversā, *Ambiorix* prōnūntiārī iubet, 
**ut** procul tēla ==coniciant== neu propius **accēdant** **et**, 
quam in partem Rōmānī impetum **fēcerint**, **cēdant** (levitāte armōrum et cōtidiānā exercitātiōne nihil eīs ==nocērī== posse), 
④ rūrsus sē \[ad signa] recipientēs **īnsequantur**.

XXXV
① \[Quō praeceptō \[ab eīs] dīligentissimē observātō], 
**cum** quaepiam cohors ex orbe **excesserat** **atque** impetum **fēcerat**, 
hostēs vēlōcissimē refugiēbant. 
② Interim eam partem nūdārī necesse **erat** **et** \[ab ==latere== apertō] tēla recipī.

## 英文翻译
XXXV
⑤ it both demolished the hope of our soldiers, and made the enemies more eager to fight, because what is done seemed to be not without the greatest timidity and desperation. 

besides, it happened, which was bound to take place, 
that the soldiers ran away from the standards in flocks, and whatever anyone of them held the dearest, they hastened to seek and grab from the bagage, 
all was filled with clamour and weeping. 

XXXVI
① And the plan didn't fail the barbarians, for the chiefs of them ordered to announce, 
lest whoever shall leave their place, that the spoils are for them and are reserved to them whatever the Romans left: then all estimated that they are winning. 

② The fighting soldiers were equal both in terms of virtue and passion; 
Our people, though deserted by Fortune and away from their leader, 
Still persued all hope of safety in their courage, 
and as often as any cohort launched an attack, a great number of enemies fell to their fight. 

③ That had been noticed, Ambiorix ordered it to be announced, 
that the soldiers throw javelins from afar while not draw near 
and go where the Romans launches an attack (due to the lightness of their arms and everyday exercises, nothing is able to injure them), ④ and attack again the them regrouping to the standards. 

XXXV
① This order received from them and observed carefully by them, 
when any cohort was getting out of the circular defensive formation and launching an attack, 
the enemies fled with the greatest volecity. 
② Meanwhile it is nessesary that this part be discovered and the javelins be collected from the sides.
