## 英文翻译
XLI 
⑦ To this Cicero give only one answer: 
that it is not the costum of the Roman people to accept conditions from armed enemies:

⑧ If they want to go away from arms (stop the war), they employ him (Cicero) as the helper and send legates to Caesar; and he hope for their justice, that they'll attain what they seek. 

XLII 
① Repulsed by such hope, the Nervii surrounded the winter quarter 9 steps away from the ramparts and 15 steps away from the ditch. 

② These by the established conduct of previous years they now from us, and were taught by those who they had as captives from our army; 

③ but without an abundance of iron implements, which was suitable for this use, it seemed that they cut off the turves with gladii, and lift out the earth with hands and mantles. 

④ Because of the same thing Cicero was able to realise the great number of the enemies: for they completed 15000 feet of fortification in circuit in less than three hours, ⑤ and in the remaining days started to prepare and even build towers to the height of the ramparts, as well as pruning-hooks and tortoise.
