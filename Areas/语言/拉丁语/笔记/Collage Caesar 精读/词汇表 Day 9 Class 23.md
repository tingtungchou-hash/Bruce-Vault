## 动词

1. [[reposcō, reposcere, —, — 3.]] -- to demand back, exact, require; BG 5.30 *abs tē ratiōnem reposcent* (3)
2. ★ [[sustineō, sustinēre, sustinuī, sustentus 2.]] -- to undergo, bear, endure; BG 5.30 *commūnem bellī cāsum sustineant* (2)
3. ★ [[intereō, interīre, interiī, interitus irreg.]] -- to perish, die; BG 5.30 *aut ferrō aut famē intereant* (irreg.)
4. [[augeō, augēre, auxī, auctus 2.]] -- to increase, enlarge; BG 5.31 *perīculum augeātur* (2)

## 名词

5. ★ [[impedīmentum, -ī n.]] -- hindrance; (pl.) baggage, baggage-train; BG 5.31 *longissimō agmine maximīsque impedīmentīs* (2)
6. ★ [[īnstrūmentum, -ī n.]] -- equipment, stores, stock; BG 5.31 *quid ex īnstrūmentō hībernōrum relinquere cōgerētur* (2)
7. [[languor, -ōris m.]] -- faintness, weariness, sluggishness; BG 5.31 *languōre mīlitum et vigiliīs perīculum augeātur* (3)

## 副词/连词

8. ★ [[quārē (adv.)]] -- whereby, by which means, therefore; BG 5.31 *omnia excōgitantur, quārē ... maneātur ... augeātur*

## 其他
- **习语**: *dat manūs* = "he surrenders / gives in" (BG 5.31.3: *dat Cotta permōtus manūs*)
- **习语**: *ratiōnem reposcere* = "to demand an accounting" (BG 5.30.2)
- **短语**: *prīmā lūce* = "at first light / at dawn" (BG 5.31.3, 5.31.6)
- **短语**: *sī modo* = "if only / provided that" (BG 5.31.2)
