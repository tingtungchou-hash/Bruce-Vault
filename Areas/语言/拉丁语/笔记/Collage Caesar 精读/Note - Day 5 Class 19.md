## Collage Caesar 原文

[[collegecaesar.14jan25.pdf#page=58]]
1.Erat in Carnūtibus summō locō nātus Tasgetius, cuius māiōrēs in suā civitāte regnum obtinuerant. 2. Huic Caesar prō eius virtūte atque in sē benevolentiā, quod in omnibus bellīs singulārī eius operā fuerat ūsus, maiōrum locum restituerat. 3. Tertium iam hunc annum regnantem inimicī, multīs palam ex civitāte eius auctōribus, eum interfēcērunt. 4. Defertur ea rēs ad Caesarem. Ille veritus, quod ad plūrēs pertinēbat, nē cīvitās eōrum impulsū dēficeret, Lucium Plancum cum legiōne ex Belgiō celeriter in Carnūtēs profīciscī iubet ibique hiemāre quōrumque operā cognōverat Tasgetium interfectum, hōs comprehēnsōs ad sē mittere. 5. Interim ab omnibus lēgātīs quaestōreque, quibus legiōnēs trādiderat, certior factus est in hīberna perventum locumque hībernīs esse mūnītum. 26.1 Diēbus circiter XV, quibus in hīberna ventum est, initium repentīnī tumultūs ac dēfectiōnis ortum est ab Ambiorīge et Catuvolcō; 2. quī, cum ad fīnēs regnī suī Sabīnō Cottaeque praestō fuissent frūmentumque in hīberna comportāvissent, Indūtiomārī Trēverī nūntiīs impulsī, suōs concitāvērunt subitōque oppressīs lignātōribus magnā manū ad castra oppugnātum vēnērunt. 3. Cum celeriter nostrī arma cepissent vallumque adscendissent atque ūnā ex parte Hispānīs equitibus ēmissīs equestrī proeliō superiōrēs fuissent, dēspērātā rē hostēs suōs ab oppugnātiōne redūxērunt.

## 英文翻译
XXV
① Tasgetius was born among the Carnutes of the highest status, 
whose ancestors had held kingly power in his own community. 

② To him Caesar restored the status of his ancestors, 
because of his virtue and goodwill towards him (Caesar), 
and because ~~there were need for~~ ***Caesar used*** his service particularly in all wars

③ Now in the third year of his reign, 
his enemies murdered him,
with many of his community openly (being) promoters. 

④ This issue is reported to Caesar. 
Caesar, fearing ~~what tends to happen to the masses~~ ***because the matter pertained to many***, 
in case that their community rebels with incitement, ordered 
that Lucius Plancus set out fast from Belgium to the Carnutes with one legion, 
and that he winter there, and if he found those by whose efforts the murder of Tasgetius (was commited), arrest and send them to him. 

⑤ Meanwhile, from all legates and the quaestor under whose command  the legions were put, 
it was made clear that they had arrived in the winter camps and the location was fortified. 

XXVI
① Within about 15 days when they went into winter camps, 
the beginning of sudden uproar and rebellion was raised by Ambiorix and Catuvolcus.  

② who, after the Romans had been at hand for Sabinus and Cotta to the borders of their own kingdom, and after they had transported the supplies together into winter camps, 
Indutiomarus of the Treveri, incited by their anouncements, stirred up his men, and suddenly with ~~their~~ ***the (Roman)*** wood-cutters crashed, went with a large army to the camps (of the Romans) in order to launch an attack.  

③ After our men had quickly picked up their arms, crossed the stake and, with the Spanish cavalry released from one part, our men had been triumphant in a equestrian battle, the enemies retreated from the offensive, with their attempt frustrated.
