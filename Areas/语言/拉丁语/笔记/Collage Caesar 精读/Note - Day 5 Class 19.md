Huic Caesar prō eius virtūte atque in sē benevolentiā, quod in omnibus bellīs singulārī eius operā fuerat ūsus, maiōrum locum restituerat. 3. Interim ab omnibus lēgātīs quaestōreque, quibus legiōnēs trādiderat, certior factus est in hīberna perventum locumque hībernīs esse mūnītum. 26.1 Diēbus circiter XV, quibus in hīberna ventum est, initium repentīnī tumultūs ac dēfectiōnis ortum est ab Ambiorīge et Catuvolcō; 2. quī, cum ad fīnēs regnī suī Sabīnō Cottaeque praestō fuissent frūmentumque in hīberna comportāvissent, Indūtiomārī Trēverī nūntiīs impulsī, suōs concitāvērunt subitōque oppressīs lignātōribus magnā manū ad castra oppugnātum vēnērunt. 3. Cum celeriter nostrī arma cepissent vallumque adscendissent atque ūnā ex parte Hispānīs equitibus ēmissīs equestrī proeliō superiōrēs fuissent, dēspērātā rē hostēs suōs ab oppugnātiōne redūxērunt.

## 英文翻译
XXV
① Tasgetius was born in the Carnutes with high status, 
whose ancestors hold kingly power in his own community. 

② To him Caesar restored the status of his ancestors, 
because of his virtue and goodwill towards him (Caesar), 
and because there were need for his service particularly in all wars

③ Now in the third year of his reign, 
his enemies murdered him,
with many of his community openly (being) promoters. 

④ This issue is reported to Caesar. 
Caesar, fearing what tends to happen to the masses, 
in case that their community rebels with incitement, ordered 
that Lucius Plancus set out fast from Belgium to the Carnutes with one legion, 
and that he winter there, and if he found those by whose efforts the murder of Tasgetius (was commited), arrest and send them to him. 

⑤ Meanwhile, from all legates and the quaestor under whose command  the legions were put, 
it was made clear that they had arrived in the winter camps and the location was fortified.
