## Collage Caesar 原文

43.3 Hostēs maximō clāmōre, sīcutī partā iam atque explorātā victōriā, turrēs testudinēsque agere et scalīs vallum ascendere coepērunt. 4 . At tanta mīlitum virtūs atque ea praesentia animī fuit ut, cum undique flammā torrērentur maximāque tēlōrum multitūdine premerentur suaque omnia impedīmenta atque omnēs fortūnās cōnflagrāre intellegerent, nōn modo dēmigrandī causā dē vallō dēcēderet nēmō, sed paene nē respiceret quidem quisquam, ac tum omnēs ācerrimē fortissimēque pugnārent. 5 . Hic diēs nostrīs longē gravissimus fuit; sed tamen hunc habuit ēventum, ut eō diē maximus numerus hostium vulnerārētur atque interficerētur, ut sē sub ipsō vallō constīpāverant recessumque prīmīs ultimī nōn dabant. 6 . Paulum quidem intermissā flammā et quōdam locō turrī adactā et contingente vāllum tertiae cohortis centuriōnēs ex eō, quō stābant, locō recessērunt suōsque omnēs removērunt, nūtū vocibusque hostēs, sī introīre vellent, vocāre coepērunt; quōrum prōgredī ausus est nēmō. 7 . Tum ex omnī parte lapidibus coniectīs dēturbātī, turrisque succēnsa est.

④ At tanta mīlitum virtūs atque ea praesentia animī **fuit** 
**ut**, **cum** undique flammā ==**torrērentur**== maximā**que** tēlōrum multitūdine **premerentur** 
sua**que** omnia impedīmenta **atque** omnēs fortūnās cōnflagrāre **intellegerent**, 
**nōn modo** \[dēmigrandī causā] dē vallō dēcēderet nēmō, 
**sed** \[paene] **nē** respiceret **quidem** quisquam, ac **tum** omnēs \[ācerrimē fortissimēque] **pugnārent**.

⑤ Hic diēs nostrīs longē gravissimus fuit; **sed** tamen hunc habuit ēventum, 
**ut** \[eō diē] maximus numerus hostium vulnerārētur atque interficerētur, 
**ut** sē \[sub ipsō vallō] constīpāverant recessum**que** prīmīs ultimī nōn dabant.

## 英文翻译
XLIII
③ The enemies, with greatest clamour, as if victory had been produced and confirmed, started to drive forward the towers and tortoises and ascend the ramparts with ladders. 

④ But such was the valour of the soldiers and the presence of spirit, 
that, although they were burnt by flames from all sides, pressed by a greatest number of javelins, and they realised that all of their own bagage and all fortunes were on fire, that not only no one ran away from the palisaded ramparts for the sake of departing, but also almost no one even look back at anyone, and then all fought most fiercely and courageously.

⑤ This day was by far the gravest day for us; but still it had this outcome, 
namely that on this day a greatest number of enemies were wounded and even killed, ~~that they crammed~~ ***since they had crammed themselves*** under their own ramparts and the last soldiers didn't give an access of retreat to the first soldiers. 

⑥ ~~Someone, a little with the flames burning into it and at a certain place the tower  shaken and about to fall towards the rampart~~ ***The fire having died down a little, and at a certain place a tower having been driven up and touching the rampart***, 
the centurions of the third cohort left the place, out of where they stood, and removed all of their men from it. 
With a nod and ~~some words~~ ***shouts***, started to call the enemies if they'd like to go inside (the camps), and nobody dared to go forward. 

⑦ Then, the stones having been thrown up, out of all part the enemies were expelled, and the towers were set on fire.
