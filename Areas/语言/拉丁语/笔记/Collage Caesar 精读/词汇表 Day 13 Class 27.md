## 动词

- [[abiciō, abicere, abiēcī, abiectus 3.]] — throw away, cast away (*arma abicere*)
- [[intermittō, intermittere, intermīsī, intermissus 3.]] — interrupt, let pass, discontinue (*neque noctem neque diem intermittit*)
- [[opprimō, opprimere, oppressī, oppressus 3.]] — overwhelm, crush, take by surprise (*subitō oppressam*)
- ★ [[hortor, hortārī, hortātus sum 1.dep.]] — urge, exhort (*hortātur nē... dīmittant*)
- ★ [[profiteor, profitērī, professus sum 2.dep.]] — declare, profess, offer oneself (*sē adiūtōrem profitētur*)
- ★ [[ulcīscor, ulcīscī, ultus sum 3.dep.]] — avenge, take vengeance on (*ulcīscendī Rōmānōs*)

## 名词

- [[negōtium, -ī n.]] — business, difficulty, trouble (*nihil negōtī*)
- ★ [[sermo, -ōnis m.]] — conversation, talk, discourse (*longior sermō īnstituitur*)
- [[vallum, -ī n.]] — rampart, palisade, intrenchment (*intrā vallum*)
