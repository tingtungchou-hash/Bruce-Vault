39.1 Itaque cōnfestim dīmissīs nuntiīs ad Ceutronēs, Grudiōs, Levācōs, Pleumoxiōs, Geidumnōs, quī omnēs sub eōrum imperiō sunt, quam maximās manūs possunt cōgunt et dē improvīsō ad Cicerōnis hīberna advolant nōndum ad eum famā dē Titūrī morte perlātā. 2 . Huic quoque accidit, quod fuit necesse, ut nōnnūllī mīlitēs, quī lignātiōnis mūnītiōnisque causā in silvās discessissent, repentīnō equitum adventū interciperentur. 3 . Hīs circumventīs magnā manū Eburōnēs, Nerviī, Aduatucī atque hōrum omnium sociī et clientēs legiōnem oppugnāre incipiunt. Nostrī celeriter ad arma concurrunt, vāllum conscendunt. 4 . Aegrē is diēs sustentātur, quod omnem spem hostēs in celeritāte ponēbant atque hanc adeptī victōriam in perpetuum sē fore victōrēs cōnfīdēbant. 40.1 Mittuntur ad Caesarem cōnfestim ab Cicerōne litterae magnīs prōpositīs praemiīs, sī pertulissent: obsessīs omnibus viīs missī intercipiuntur. 2 . Noctū ex materiā, quam mūnītiōnis causā comportāverant, turrēs admodum CXX excitantur incrēdibilī celeritāte; quae dēesse operī vidēbantur, perficiuntur. 3 . Hostēs posterō diē, multō maiōribus coāctis copiīs, castra oppugnant, fossam complent. Eādem ratiōne, quā prīdiē, ab nostrīs resistitur: 4 . hoc idem reliquīs deinceps fit diēbus.

## 语法分析
XXXIX 
① **Itaque** \[==cōnfestim==] \[dīmissīs nuntiīs] \[ad Ceutronēs, Grudiōs, Levācōs, Pleumoxiōs, Geidumnōs], 
	**quī** omnēs \[sub eōrum imperiō] **sunt**, 
*quam maximās manūs possunt* **cōgunt** 
**et** \[dē ==improvīsō==] (?) \[ad Cicerōnis hīberna] **advolant** 
\[==nōndum== ad eum famā <= dē Titūrī morte | perlātā]. 

② Huic quoque accidit, 
**quod** fuit necesse, **ut** nōnnūllī mīlitēs, 
<= **quī** \[lignātiōnis mūnītiōnisque causā] \[in silvās] **discessissent**, 
\[repentīnō equitum adventū] **==interciperentur==**.

③ \[Hīs circumventīs <= magnā manū] Eburōnēs, Nerviī, Aduatucī atque hōrum omnium sociī et clientēs *legiōnem* oppugnāre **incipiunt**. 
Nostrī celeriter ad arma **concurrunt**, vāllum conscendunt.

④ Aegrē is diēs sustentātur, **quod** omnem spem *hostēs* \[in celeritāte] **ponēbant** 
**atque** *hanc ==adeptī== victōriam* \[in perpetuum] sē fore victōrēs **cōnfīdēbant**.

XL 
① Mittuntur \[ad Caesarem cōnfestim ab Cicerōne] litterae \[magnīs prōpositīs praemiīs], **sī** pertulissent: \[==obsessīs== omnibus viīs] missī **intercipiuntur**.

② Noctū \[ex materiā], <= quam \[mūnītiōnis causā] **comportāverant**, 
turrēs admodum CXX ==**excitantur**== \[incrēdibilī celeritāte]; 
quae dēesse operī **vidēbantur**, **perficiuntur**.

## 英文翻译
XXXIX
① And immediately the annoncement was sent to the Ceutrones, Grudios, Levacos, Pleumoxios, and Geidumnos, who are all under the power of them, and they assembled an army as large as they're able to assemble, and suddenly came to Cicero's camps when the rumour about Titurius' death hadn't been confirmed to him. 

② To this one also happened that it was unavoidable that some soldiers, who left to go into the forests for gathering timber and fortifying the post, were caught by the sudden come of the cavalry. 

③ These men surrounded by a great army, the Eburones, the Nervii, the Aduatuci and all of their allies started to attack the legion. Our men quickly ran to arms and ascended the ramparts. 

④ The day was spent with difficulties, because the enemies relied on volecity for all their hope, and believed that they would be the victors holding the victory forever. 

XL 
① Letters were immediately sent from Cicero to Caesar with a great award put forward (to the messengers), if they had made it to Caesar: besieged by all forces, many messengers sent away were captured. 

② At night, just about 120 towers were erected at incredible speed from the timber which the soldiers transported for fortifying the camps. 
Those things, which seem to be lacking of work(?), were done. 

③ The next day, the enemies, with much larger an army drawn together, besieged the camps, and filled the ditch. In the same manner of the previous day, the army was resisted by us:  this same thing happend the remaining successive days.
