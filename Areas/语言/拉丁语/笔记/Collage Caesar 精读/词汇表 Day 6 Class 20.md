## 动词

1. ★ [[faciō, facere, fēcī, factus 3.|faciō]] — to make, do, cause; 拉丁最高频动词 (3, 被动异干 fiō)
2. ★ [[teneō, tenēre, tenuī, tentus 2.|teneō]] — to hold, keep, possess, restrain (2, ten- 词根; *memoriā tenēre* = 记住)
3. ★ [[pendō, pendere, pependī, pēnsus 3.|pendō]] — to pay, weigh out; 完成时叠音 pependī (3, 勿与 pendeō 混淆)
4. ★ [[minuō, minuere, minuī, minūtus 3.|minuō]] — to lessen, diminish; BG 5.26 *contrōversiās minuī* (3)
5. ★ [[cōnfiteor, cōnfitērī, cōnfessus sum 2.dep.|cōnfiteor]] — to confess, acknowledge; BG 5.27 *plūrimum cōnfitērī* (2.dep., con- + fateor)
6. [[conclāmō, conclāmāre, conclāmāvī, conclāmātus 1.|conclāmō]] — to shout (together); BG 5.26 *conclāmāvērunt* (1, con- + clāmō)
7. [[prōdeō, prōdīre, prōdiī, prōditus irreg.|prōdeō]] — to go forth, come out; BG 5.26 *prōdiret* (irreg., prō- + eō)

## 名词

8. ★ [[stīpendium, -ī n.|stīpendium]] — tribute, tax, military pay; BG 5.27 *stīpendiō līberātus* (n., stips + pendō)

## 副词

9. [[praesertim (adv.)|praesertim]] — especially, chiefly; *praesertim cum* = especially since, BG 5.27 (adv.)
10. [[porrō (adv.)|porrō]] — furthermore, moreover; 叙述过渡词 (adv.)
