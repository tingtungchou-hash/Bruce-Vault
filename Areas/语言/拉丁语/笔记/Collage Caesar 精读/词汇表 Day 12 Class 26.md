## 动词

1. [[revertor, revertī, reversus sum 3.]] -- to turn back, return; BG 5.35 *cum in eum locum unde erant egressī revertī coeperant* 
2. [[conferciō, confercīre, ---, confertum 4.]] -- to cram together; (perf. part. as adj.) pressed close, densely packed; BG 5.35 *ab tantā multitūdine coniecta tēla confertī vitāre poterant* 
3. ★ [[committō, committere, commīsī, commissum 3.]] -- to commit, do; to entrust; to join battle; BG 5.35 *nihil quod ipsīs esset indignum committēbant* 
4. [[trāiciō, trāicere, trāiēcī, trāiectum 3.]] -- to pierce, transfix; to throw across; to transport; BG 5.35 *utrumque femur trāgulā trāicitur* 
5. ★ [[interpōnō, interpōnere, interposuī, interpositum 3.]] -- to interpose, insert between; to pledge; BG 5.36 *inque eam rem sē suam fidem interpōnere* 
6. ★ [[subveniō, subvenīre, subvēnī, subventum 4.]] -- to come to aid, assist, relieve (+ dat.); BG 5.35 *dum circumventō fīliō subvenit, interficitur* 
7. ★ [[parcō, parcere, pepercī, parsum 3.]] -- to spare, be sparing (+ dat.); to refrain; BG 5.36 *rogātum ut sibi mīlitibusque parcat* 

## 连词

8. ★ [[sīn conj.]] -- but if, if however, if on the contrary (转折条件连词); BG 5.35 *sīn autem locum tenēre vellent*

## 副词

9. ★ [[unde (adv.)]] -- from which place, whence; (transf.) from whom, from which (source/cause); BG 5.35 *in eum locum unde erant egressī*

## 补充说明

- **与格支配动词**：本课的 `subveniō` 和 `parcō` 均接与格（非宾格），这是 Day 11 `noceō` 同系列的延续。Day 11 学了 `noceō + dat.` 和 `cēdō + dat.`，Day 12 新增 `subveniō + dat.` 和 `parcō + dat.`。
- **异态动词注意**：`revertor` 为异态动词，现在不定式 `revertī` 容易与完成时混淆。
- **Caesar 核心习语**：
  - `fidem interpōnere` — to pledge one's word (谈判/外交场景)
  - `nihil committere quod indignum sit` — to do nothing unworthy (道德评价惯用表达)
  - `sīn autem` — Caesar 常用转折条件搭配
- **军事语境词汇**：`confertus`（密集阵型）、`trāicitur`（被刺穿）、`subvenit`（支援）均为 Caesar 军事叙事核心动词
