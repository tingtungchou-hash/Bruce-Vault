## 动词

1. ★ [[spectō, spectāre, spectāvī, spectātus 1.|spectō]] — to look at, behold, watch; (here) to look to, regard, consider the situation; BG 5.29 *nōn hostem auctōrem, sed rem spectāre* (1)
2. ★ [[subsum, subesse, subfuī —|subsum]] — to be under, near, at hand (+ dat.); BG 5.29 *subesse Rhēnum* (irreg.)
3. ★ [[ārdeō, ārdēre, ārsī, ārsus 2.|ārdeō]] — to burn, blaze; (here) to burn with indignation; BG 5.29 *ārdēre Galliam* (2)
4. [[accipiō, accipere, accēpī, acceptus 3.|accipiō]] — to take, receive; (here) to suffer; BG 5.29 *calamitātis acceptum* (3)

## 形容词

5. ★ [[tūtus, -a, -um adj.|tūtus]] — safe, secure, out of danger; BG 5.29 *suam sententiam in utramque partem esse tūtam*
6. ★ [[dūrus, -a, -um adj.|dūrus]] — hard, harsh, severe; BG 5.29 *sī nihil esset dūrius* (comp.)

## 副词/连词

7. ★ [[sērō (adv.)|sērō]] — late; (here) too late; BG 5.29 *sērō factūrōs clāmitābat*
