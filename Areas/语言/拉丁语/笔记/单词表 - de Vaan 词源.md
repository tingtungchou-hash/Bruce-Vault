1. **utilitas, utilitatis** f quality of being useful
2. **usus, usus** use, need, value
3. **usura, usurae** a using, use or enjoyment of a thing
4. **usurpo, usurpare** to carry out, make use of, take possession of
5. **inusitatus, inusitata, inusitatum** unusual
6. **abutor, abuti** to use up, to consume entirely
