# Consonant Declension 
## morphology 
| case                | masc./fem. | neuter |
| ------------------- | ---------- | ------ |
| sing. nom.          | — / -ς     | —      |
| sing. gen.          | -ος        | -ος    |
| sing. dat.          | -ι         | -ι     |
| sing. acc.          | -α / -ν    | —      |
| sing. voc.          | — / -ς     | —      |
| dual nom./acc./voc. | ε          | ε      |
| dual gen./dat.      | οιν        | οιν    |
| plur. nom.          | -ες        | -α     |
| plur. gen.          | -ων        | -ων    |
| plur. dat.          | -σι(ν)     | -σι(ν) |
| plur. acc.          | -ας        | -ᾰ     |
Attention:
- do pay special attention to **nom., acc., voc. in singular and dat. in plural**. 
- when there is no case ending added to the stem, the stem may lose its final consonant, because in ancient Greek it is a phonetic law that words must end either in a vowel or in -ν, -ρ, or -ς 
- when the nom. and voc. sing. ending -ς or the dat. pl. ending -σι is added to a stem ending in consonant, the sigma may combine and lead to consonants like ξ or ψ
- when there's no case ending added in the nom. masc. sing., there may be a long vowel in the final syllable of the stem in that form while the rest of the cases have a short vowel 
## accentuation 
- the accent is persistent, but for monosyllabic-stem nouns of the consonant declension, 
	- the ultima accented in the genitive and dative of all numbers, with acute on short U, circumflex on long U. 
	- In the other cases the accent falls on the first or only syllable, with acute on any short vowel or on a long vowel in the nominative and vocative singular, or circumflex on long P before a short U, as usual
# Labial and Velar Plosive Stems 
nouns of this type are all masculine or feminine, have stems ending in π, β, φ or κ, γ, χ
## morphology 

| case                | masc./fem. | κλωπ-    |
| ------------------- | ---------- | -------- |
| sing. nom.          | -ς         | κλώψ     |
| sing. gen.          | -ος        | κλωπός   |
| sing. dat.          | -ι         | κλωπί    |
| sing. acc.          | -α         | κλῶπα    |
| sing. voc.          | -ς         | κλώψ     |
| dual nom./acc./voc. | ε          | κλῶπε    |
| dual gen./dat.      | οιν        | κλωποῖν  |
| plur. nom.          | -ες        | κλῶπες   |
| plur. gen.          | -ων        | κλωπῶν   |
| plur. dat.          | -σι(ν)     | κλωψί(ν) |
| plur. acc.          | -ας        | κλῶπας   |
## features: 
- the original case endings are all well preserved 
- the nom. and voc. sing. as well as dat. pl. have -ς or -σι(ν) which combines with the plosive to form either ψ or ξ
- the acc. sing. has -α 
# Dental Plosive Stems 
nouns of this type are all masculine or feminine, have stems ending in τ, δ, θ
## morphology 

| case                | masc./fem. | ἀσπιδ-, ἀσπι- | χαριτ-, χαρι- |
| ------------------- | ---------- | ------------- | ------------- |
| sing. nom.          | -ς         | ἀσπίς         | χάρις         |
| sing. gen.          | -ος        | ἀσπίδος       | χάριτος       |
| sing. dat.          | -ι         | ἀσπίδι        | χάριτι        |
| sing. acc.          | -α / -ν    | ἀσπίδα        | χάριν         |
| sing. voc.          | —          | ἀσπί          | χάρι          |
| dual nom./acc./voc. | ε          | ἀσπίδε        | χάριτε        |
| dual gen./dat.      | οιν        | ἀσπίδοιν      | χαρίτοιν      |
| plur. nom.          | -ες        | ἀσπίδες       | χάριτες       |
| plur. gen.          | -ων        | ἀσπίδων       | χαρίτων       |
| plur. dat.          | -σι(ν)     | ἀσπίσι(ν)     | χάρισι(ν)     |
| plur. acc.          | -ας        | ἀσπίδας       | χάριτας       |
## features: 
- nouns in -ις were in origin i-stems without a dental plosive, and this accounts for the existence of two stems. 
	- the shorter stem survives in the **nom. and voc. sing.**, the **dat. pl.** and sometimes the **acc. sing.**; 
	- the longer stem, clear from the gen. sing., appears in the other cases. 
- the **acc. sing.** has a noteworthy variation in disyllabic stems ending in **ιτ, ιδ, ιθ**:
	- if the second syllable of the stem is accented, the longer stem is used, and the acc. sing. ending is -α
	- if the first syllable of the stem is accented, the shorter form of the stem is used, and the acc. sing. ending is -ν
- nouns in which a vowel other than iota precedes the consonant (as φυγάς) have a similar pattern by analogy (no dental in nom., voc. sing. or dat. pl.), but the voc. sing. has -ς, and the acc. sing. is always -ᾰ 
# Masculine Nouns with Stem in ντ 
nouns of this type are all masculine, have stems ending in ντ; the full form of the stem is visible, as usual, in the gen. sing., and the nom., voc. sing. and dat. pl. are again the cases that show variation 
## morphology 

| case                | masc.  | γεροντ-    | γιγᾰντ-   | ὀδοντ-    |
| ------------------- | ------ | ---------- | --------- | --------- |
| sing. nom.          | — / -ς | γέρων      | γίγᾱς     | ὀδούς     |
| sing. gen.          | -ος    | γέροντος   | γίγαντος  | ὀδόντος   |
| sing. dat.          | -ι     | γέροντι    | γίγαντι   | ὀδόντι    |
| sing. acc.          | -α     | γέροντα    | γίγαντα   | ὀδόντα    |
| sing. voc.          | — / -ς | γέρον      | γίγᾰν     | ὀδούς     |
| dual nom./acc./voc. | ε      | γέροντε    | γίγαντε   | ὀδόντε    |
| dual gen./dat.      | οιν    | γερόντοιν  | γιγάντοιν | ὀδόντοιν  |
| plur. nom.          | -ες    | γέροντες   | γίγαντες  | ὀδόντες   |
| plur. gen.          | -ων    | γερόντων   | γιγάντων  | ὀδόντων   |
| plur. dat.          | -σι(ν) | γέρουσι(ν) | γίγᾱσι(ν) | ὀδοῦσι(ν) |
| plur. acc.          | -ας    | γέροντας   | γίγαντας  | ὀδόντας   |
## features: 
- in nouns like γέρων the nom. sing. has no ending added (the tau has been dropped for phonetic reasons) but shows a strong-vowel form of the stem (ω for ο in γέρων); the voc. sing. has also lost its tau but retains the weak vowel ο in γέρον 
- in nouns like γίγας and ὀδούς the nom. sing. has -ς added, and the combination -ντς is simplified to -ς (Greek did not retain this harsh sequence), but the preceding vowel is lengthened in compensation (ο becomes ου in ὀδούς, ᾰ becomes ᾱ in γίγας) 
- the same ντσ sequence is also simplified in the dat. pl., with the same compensatory lengthening of the preceding vowel 
# Neuter Nouns with Stem in τ 
nouns of this type are all neuter, have stems ending in τ; the full form of the stem is clear in the gen. sing., and the nom., acc. and voc. sing. and the dat. pl. show variation 
## morphology 

| case                | neuter | πραγματ-    | τερατ-    |
| ------------------- | ------ | ----------- | --------- |
| sing. nom.          | —      | πρᾶγμα      | τέρας     |
| sing. gen.          | -ος    | πράγματος   | τέρατος   |
| sing. dat.          | -ι     | πράγματι    | τέρατι    |
| sing. acc.          | —      | πρᾶγμα      | τέρας     |
| sing. voc.          | —      | πρᾶγμα      | τέρας     |
| dual nom./acc./voc. | ε      | πράγματε    | τέρατε    |
| dual gen./dat.      | οιν    | πραγμάτοιν  | τεράτοιν  |
| plur. nom.          | -α     | πράγματα    | τέρατα    |
| plur. gen.          | -ων    | πραγμάτων   | τεράτων   |
| plur. dat.          | -σι(ν) | πράγμασι(ν) | τέρασι(ν) |
| plur. acc.          | -α     | πράγματα    | τέρατα    |
## features: 
- in nouns like πρᾶγμα no ending is added in the nom., acc. and voc. sing., and the final tau is dropped 
- in other nouns an alternative stem is used for the nom., acc. and voc. sing. (τέρας, ὕδωρ, φῶς) 
- the nom., acc. and voc. pl. form has short alpha (-ᾰ), like all neuter forms 
- in the dat. pl. the tau does not appear, by analogy with dental stems of the first type 
## accentuation 
- φῶς: the contracted stem φωτ- is treated like a monosyllabic stem (φωτός, φωτί, φωσί), but the gen. pl. is accented φώτων 
# The Suffix -ματ- 
a large number of neuter nouns of the consonant declension are formed from verb stems by adding the suffix -ματ- (nom. sing. -μα). The usual meaning of such a noun is the thing created by the action X 

| verb    |          | noun     |                                    |
| ------- | -------- | -------- | ---------------------------------- |
| βουλεύω | to plan  | βούλευμα | plan, proposal                     |
| πράττω  | to do    | πρᾶγμα   | action, event, circumstance, thing |
| ποιέω   | to make  | ποίημα   | creation; poem                     |
| γράφω   | to write | γράμμα   | writing, letter                    |
