# 单词总表

本页按教材 Unit 3–42 的 Vocabulary 顺序汇总每课课后单词，保留教材原有的词类分类与每课末尾的 Principal parts。Unit 1–2 为字母与重音，无单词表。

词条格式：词形 — 释义；方括号内为教材所给英文同源词。

## Unit 3

### O-declension masculine nouns

- ἄγγελος, ἀγγέλου, m. — messenger, herald [angel]
- ἀδελφός, ἀδελφοῦ, m. — brother [Philadelphia]
- ἄνεμος, ἀνέμου, m. — wind [anemometer]
- ἄνθρωπος, ἀνθρώπου, m. — human being, man (occasionally fem., “woman”) [anthropology]

- θάνατος, θανάτου, m. — death [euthanasia]
- θεός, θεοῦ, m. (or f.) — god, divinity (occasionally fem., “goddess”) [theology]
- ἵππος, ἵππου, m. (or f.) — horse (also fem., “mare”) [hippopotamus]
- λόγος, λόγου, m. — word, speech, tale, story; reckoning, account, proportion; reason, rationality [philology]
- νόμος, νόμου, m. — custom; law [economic]

- στρατός, στρατοῦ, m. — army, host [strategic]

### O-declension feminine nouns

- νόσος, νόσου, f. — sickness, disease [nosology]
- ὁδός, ὁδοῦ, f. — road, path, way; journey [odometer, method]
- ψῆφος, ψήφου, f. — small stone, pebble (used in reckoning, in games, in voting); vote [psephologist]

### O-declension neuter nouns

- βιβλίον, βιβλίου, n. — book [bibliophile]
- δῶρον, δώρου, n. — gift; (in political or judicial contexts) bribe [Eudora]
- ἔργον, ἔργου, n. — work, action, deed [erg, energy]
- μέτρον, μέτρου, n. — measure, size, distance; moderate amount, proportion [meter]
- παιδίον, παιδίου, n. — child [pediatrics]

### Prepositions

- ἀπό (ἀπ’ or ἀφ’ before vowels) — (+ gen.) away from, from [aphelion, apogee, apocalypse]
- ἐκ (ἐξ before vowels) — (+ gen.) out of, forth from; as a result of [eclectic, ecdysiast]
- ἐν — (+ dat.) in, within, on, at, among [encaustic, empathy, ellipsis]
- εἰς or ἐς — (+ acc.) into, to, toward; with respect to [isagogic]
- σύν — (+ dat.) together with, with [synonym, sympathy]

### Conjunctions

- ἀλλά — but, but rather
- καί — and; (adverbial) even, also, too

### Negative adverb

- οὐ (οὐ or οὐχ before vowels) — not (negating statements, words, or phrases) [Utopia]
