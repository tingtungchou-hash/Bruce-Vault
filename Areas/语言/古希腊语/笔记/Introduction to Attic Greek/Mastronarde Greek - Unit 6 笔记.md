# Prepositions II 

- ἀντί + gen. instead of, instead of, in place of 
- πρό + gen. in front of, indefense of, before 
- διά + gen. through, by (agent or means)
- διά + acc. because of, on account of, by aid of, by reason of 
- μετά + gen. among, with 
- μετά + acc. after
