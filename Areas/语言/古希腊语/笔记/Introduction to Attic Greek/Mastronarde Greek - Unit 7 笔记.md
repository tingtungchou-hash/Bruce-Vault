## Position of modifiers  

- most common: article - modifier - noun 
	- ὁ ἀγαθὸς βίος 
	- ὁ ἐν τῇ ἀγορᾷ στρατιώτης 
	- οἱ ἄνθρωποι οἱ τότε
- less common: article - noun - article (repeated) - modifier 
	- οἱ δικασταὶ οἱ δίκαιοι
- uncommon: noun - article - modifier 

\* the modifiers can also be a repositional phrase, or a participle, or certain dependent genitives, or (occasionally) even an adverb
