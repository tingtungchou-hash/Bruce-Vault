## International Admissions 

If you are an international student wishing to **enroll** in one of our many Bachelor's and Master's degree programmes, you must follow different procedures depending on whether you hold an EU or non-EU passport, need a visa, or are already living in Italy.

These procedures can sometimes be intimidating, but we are here to help you every step of the way!

If you are a student applying from abroad or holding foreign qualifications, you must go through certain specific steps before you can enroll. That is why we have created a dedicated summary document **Admission procedures - Orientation and information** and the webpage **Internationl Student Office** detailing what you need to do depending on your status, programme, and qualifications, with contacts directing you to the office or online service that best fits your needs.  
[Read the summary Admission procedures - Orientation and Information](https://www.uniroma1.it/sites/default/files/field_file_allegati/admission_procedures_orientation_and_information_1.pdf) (pdf)  
[Click on the page International Student Office](https://www.uniroma1.it/en/pagina/international-student-office)

Please remember: **Italian candidates** holding a foreign qualification must follow all procedures as set for **International students**.

Study in English at Sapienza: your admission starts here!

Are you ready to give your future an international edge? If you want to enroll in a degree programme taught entirely in English, you have to apply for an admission evaluation directly from home through our online pre-selection procedure.

Our academic offer in English is wide and diverse, tailored to your ambitions:

- **8 Bachelor's Degree Programmes** (First-cycle);
- **45 Master's Degree Programmes** (Second-cycle)

Please note: for 9 of these Master's degrees, the online pre-selection procedure is also required if you choose the curriculum taught in Italian.

Ready to check all the details, academic entry requirements, deadlines, and scholarship opportunities?

[Foundation Year](#foundation-year)

The Sapienza University Foundation Year was conceived for international students who need to complete their basic education in order to pass an admission test in Italian state universities (in a range of disciplines, i.e. Medicine, Architecture, Science, Engineering and Humanities). The programme is held in Italian and primarily addresses to students who do not meet the **minimum schooling requirements** outlined in current legislation for enrolment at Italian universities.
