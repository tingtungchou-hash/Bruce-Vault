|     | ———Inspiration |     |     | from | the  | Monument  | of  | National | Unity |     |
| --- | -------------- | --- | --- | ---- | ---- | --------- | --- | -------- | ----- | --- |
|     |                |     |     |      | ZHOU | Jing-hong |     |          |       |     |
(InstituteofEthnologyandSociology，ChineseAcademyofSocialSciences，Beijing100081)
［Abstract］After 60 years，when recalling the event of setting up the Monument of National Unity and
its historic role in adjusting national relationship and thinking over the strategic layout of " Four
Comprehensives" ( Comprehensively build a moderate prosperous society，comprehensively deepen reform，
comprehensively govern the nation according to law，and comprehensively strictly govern the Party．) and the
promotion of the carrier of national unity，we must admit that the monument is an innovative model of national
unity established by the old generation of the Communist Party and an important enlightenment for today’s
people． When the rich social，historical and cultural resources and their local influence in ethnic regions are
endowed with new ideas of national equality，unity，mutual help and cooperation，they will become new norms
to guide the behavior of social members and to help them resist and overcome negative forces hindering national
unity． They will also become new carriers of national unity for different ethnic regions in their unique ways and
provide an important guarantee for unity and struggle，prosperity and development of all ethnic groups．
［Key words］the Monument of National Unity; traditional social resources; national relationship
