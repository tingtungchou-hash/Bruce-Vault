“普洱盟誓”精神及其当代意义
———历史、记忆与边疆现代化视角
谷家荣
|     |     | (云南师范大学 | 法学与社会学学院，云南 |     |     | 昆明 650500) |     |     |     |

摘要:“普洱盟誓”是中国共产党边疆民族工作的伟大创举。自这一历史事件发生以来，云南普洱边区少数民族
代表誓言盟誓便一直是中国民族工作者和学界人士关注的题域。七十余年来，人们在充分发掘珍稀历史素材和
抢救性回访亲历者的过程中完整陈述了创立普洱民族团结誓词碑的历史背景、过程事件及其对维护中华民族统
一大业的重大意义。地方政府举行的历次大型纪念活动及主题精神宣扬，也再度加深民众牢固记忆传承盟誓精
神的动力。普洱盟誓精神是中国式现代化的宝贵精神财富。新时代，充分把握政学两界人士关于普洱盟誓的论
说观点，领会盟誓精神实质，深入调查誓词碑签名代表所在地的发展实情，可以研究促进边疆各民族在更加友好
的交往交流交融过程中铸牢中华民族共同体意识，高质量推进边疆现代化。
关键词:普洱盟誓精神;民族团结;边疆现代化

The Spirit of“Pu ’er Pledge”and Its Contemporary Significance
———From the Perspective of History，Memory，Frontier Modernization
Gu Jiarong
( School of Law and Sociology，Yunnan Normal University，Kunming 650500，Yunnan，China)
Abstract:The“Pu ’er Pledge”is a great initiative of the Chinese Communist Party in frontier ethnic work． Since the occur-
rence of the historical event，the oath of the minority representatives in Pu ’erspecial area of Yunnan has become a topic that
Chinese ethnic workers and academics have been enthusiastic about． Over the past 70 years，in the process of fully excavating
rare historical materials andinterviewingthewitnesses，peoplehavefullystatedthehistoricalbackground，processandgreatsig-
nificance of the establishment of Pu ’er National Unity Oath Tablet for safeguarding the great cause of Chinese national unity．
The large－scale commemorative activities held by local governments and the promotion of the theme spirit once again deepened
the motivation of the people to firmly remember and inherit the spirit of the pledge． The spirit of Pu ’erPledge is the precious
spiritual wealth of Chinese modernization． In the new era，we can fully grasp the viewpoint of the political and academic circles
on the Pu ’erPledge，grasp the spirit and essence of the pledge，and deeply investigate the development reality of the place
where the signature of the oath tablet is represented，so as to study and promote the ethnic groups in the border area to forge a
strongsenseofcommunityfortheChinesenationintheprocessofmorefriendlyexchangesandcommunication，andpromotethe
high－quality modernization of the border area．
Key words:spirit of Pu ’erPledge; national unity; frontier modernization
